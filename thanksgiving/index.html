Questions

What do these guides cover?

Four holiday checks: turkey fryers, including outdoor oil fryers, indoor oil fryers, and air fryers; pressure cookers and multicookers with a lid-lock notice; food thermometers and USDA turkey temperatures, thawing, and leftovers; and kitchen fires, including extinguishers. Each guide says what to read on the product and which official page states the rule.

Where is the official recall search?

CPSC Recalls is the United States search. Health Canada recalls and safety alerts is the Canada search. Search the brand and the model, item, or date code printed on the unit.

Does a guide assign a pass to a fryer or a cooker?

No. A guide quotes an official page and links it. A catalogue card is a separate dated match for one model. A clear line on one card does not cover a different model number. Gifts for the host names widely sold hosting tools and the check to do.
