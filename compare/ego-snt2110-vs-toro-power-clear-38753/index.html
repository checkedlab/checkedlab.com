How to read this

Clearing width and throw as the EGO and Toro pages state them for the SNT2110 and the Power Clear 38753, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The EGO page states a clearing width of 21 in and a throw of up to 40 ft. The Toro 38753 page states a clearing width of 21 in and a throw of up to 35 ft.

Those two labels are the shared rows. Engine, intake height, and weight are on the Toro card only. Auger, power, and tool warranty are on the EGO card only.

The width row does not separate them

Both maker pages print 21 in on the clearing-width line. A reader who stops at width has not found a difference. The throw line is the shared row that does differ. The EGO page prints up to 40 ft. The Toro page prints up to 35 ft. Both are the maker’s own “up to” wording.

The units match, inches for width and feet for throw, so the rows can sit in one table. A longer stated throw, at the same stated width, is the EGO line. A shorter stated throw is the Toro line. Neither throw line names a snow depth, a slope, or a wind speed.

Pick by the lines that stay off the table

Pick the EGO page if the decision turns on a battery power line at that 21 in width. The EGO page states that Peak Power uses two 56V ARC Lithium batteries, names a steel auger, and states a 5-year limited tool warranty. Those labels are not on the Toro card, so they are not in the table. Pick the EGO page as well if the throw figure you are matching is the longer one, up to 40 ft.

Pick the Toro page if the decision turns on a gas-engine line at the same stated width. The Toro page states a 212 cc engine, an intake height of 12.5 in, and a weight of 84 lb, with a throw of up to 35 ft. Intake height and weight are Toro-only labels. A stated weight of 84 lb is the figure to read if you are matching a walk-behind machine rather than a battery tool.

The auger material and the battery description answer a different question from the engine displacement. Read the label that matches the power source you already have. A two-battery 56V line and a 212 cc line are not the same kind of spec, which is why they stay in the cards and out of the shared table.

Older Power Clear notices are a model-number question

The recall block on this page is the catalogue match for SNT2110 and for 38753. Open each model page for the feed link. The Toro card also links older Power Clear notices. The 2010 CPSC notice names Power Clear 180 models 38272 and 38282. The 2011 CPSC notice names snowblower models 38588 and 38589, plus a mower model. The catalogue model on this page is 38753, which is not one of those model numbers.

Health Canada’s 2010 notice names the same Power Clear 180 models, 38272 and 38282. Health Canada’s 2011 notice names 38588 and 38589. Read the model and serial decal the notice describes before treating 38753 as one of those machines. The EGO card does not carry those Toro notices.
