Which specs to read

The Core 300S-P manual states noise of 22–54.5 dB and a weight of 7.48 lb. The Core 600S-P page states noise of 26 dB and a weight of 13.7 lb / 6.2 kg.

A lower stated weight is the Core 300S-P line. The 600S-P noise line is a single figure. The 300S-P noise line is a range. Power, size, CADR, and room size use labels that only one card uses.

Same brand, two cabinets

Both cards are Levoit Core models, and the shared labels are noise and weight. The 300S-P manual prints 22–54.5 dB and 7.48 lb, with dimensions of 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in and rated power of 39 W at AC 120 V, 60 Hz. The 600S-P page prints 26 dB and 13.7 lb / 6.2 kg, with a size of 12.3 x 12.3 x 23.6 in and power of 49 W at AC 120 V, 60 Hz.

CADR on the 600S-P page is 391 CFM. Room size on that page is 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour, or 606 sq ft at 4.8. The 300S-P card does not use a CADR label or a room-size label, so those rows stay off the table. Filtration on the 600S-P page says 3-stage. That label is not on the 300S-P card.

Pick the smaller stated weight or the CADR line

Pick the Core 300S-P manual if the weight you are matching is 7.48 lb and the cabinet you are matching is 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in. The power line that goes with that cabinet is 39 W. The noise line is a range, 22–54.5 dB, not a single operating point.

Pick the Core 600S-P page if the figure you are matching is 391 CFM, the room line that names 606 sq ft at 4.8 air changes, or the taller cabinet of 12.3 x 12.3 x 23.6 in. The stated weight on that page is 13.7 lb, and the noise line is the single figure 26 dB. A lower stated wattage is the 300S-P line, 39 W against 49 W, and those power labels are not identical, so wattage stays on the cards.

A range and a single decibel figure should not be read as “quieter” without the page saying which speed the number belongs to. The 600S-P page prints 26 dB. The 300S-P manual prints a span. The recall lines below are the catalogue match for each model.
