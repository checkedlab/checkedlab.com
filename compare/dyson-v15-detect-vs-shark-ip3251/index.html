How to read this

Weight, run time, and charge time as the Dyson and Shark pages state them for the V15 Detect Absolute and the IP3251, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-13 (UTC).

The V15 Detect Absolute page states 6.83 lb, up to 60 min of run time, and a 4.5 hr charge time. The IP3251 page states 18.47 lbs, up to 70 minutes measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, and a 6 hour charge time.

A lower stated weight is the V15 line. A longer stated run time is the IP3251 line. Bin volume is on the V15 card only. Dust cup capacity is on the IP3251 card only.
