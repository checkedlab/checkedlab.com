How we picked

Checked Lab lines up product pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

A Best for line needs a use the specs support: suction with a mop speed, or a runtime with its mode. A robot weight, a dust-cup size, or a voltage does not get a line. A dock dimension is not the robot dimension. The Roomba page prints both, and they are labeled robot and dock.

The two Shark cards are different watt lines. The AV2501S page states 650 watts. The AV2820AE page states 35 watts and 14.4 volts. The cleaning path on both pages is 5.7 in. Neither watt line is a room size or a runtime, so those cards have no Best for line.

What to look for

  1. Read whether the dimension is the robot or the dock. The Roomba Combo j9+ page states robot dimensions of 3.4 x 13.7 x 13.7 in and dock dimensions of 15.8 in W x 16.4 in D x 16.3 in H. The Qrevo S page states robot dimensions of 13.78 x 13.9 x 3.80 in and a separate dock.
  2. Read the suction unit the page uses. The Qrevo S page states 7,000 Pa. The X10 Pro Omni page states 8,000 Pa. The Roomba and Shark cards on this page do not print a pascal line.
  3. Read the runtime sentence with its mode. The X10 Pro Omni page states 173 min vacuum, or 136 min vacuum and mop, on standard. The other four cards on this page do not print that runtime sentence.
  4. Read the dust capacity in the unit printed. The AV2501S page states a dust cup of 0.1 qt. The X10 Pro Omni page states a dustbin of 330 ml. Those units are different.

Questions

Which robot card states 8,000 Pa?

The eufy X10 Pro Omni card. The page also states a run time of 173 min vacuum, or 136 min vacuum and mop, on standard, and a robot weight of 4,560 g.

Which robot card is the 8.9 lb robot?

The Roomba Combo j9+ card. Robot weight is 8.9 lb and robot dimensions are 3.4 x 13.7 x 13.7 in. Dock weight on that page is 21.9 lb.

Why are the two Shark watt lines different?

The AV2501S page states 650 watts. The AV2820AE page states 35 watts and 14.4 volts. Both pages state a cleaning path of 5.7 in.

Where does the robot vacuum recall line come from?

The US line is the CPSC SaferProducts match for that model. The Canadian line is the Health Canada open dataset match. A notice that names a different model stays on the card as a different model.
