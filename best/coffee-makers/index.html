How we picked

Checked Lab lines up maker pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The Best for line uses the brew the page describes: a 10-cup drip pot, a press for one or two cups, or a 30 ounce glass carafe. Capacity units are left as printed. Cups on the Moccamaster line and ounces on the AeroPress and Chemex lines are different units.

The Barista Express and the Encore ESP are on the kitchen list and are not on this page. This page is the three brewers: drip, press, and pour-over carafe.

Technivorm Moccamaster KBGV Select

The KBGV Select page states a volume of 1.25 L / 40 oz / 10 cups, power of 1475 W at 120 V, and auto-off of 100 minutes. Weight is 6.00 lb. The 10-cup volume line is the full-pot use. Auto-off on that page is 100 minutes.

AeroPress Original Coffee Maker

The AeroPress Original page states a capacity of 10 oz (1-2 cups), a weight of 7.75 oz with scoop and stirrer, and a material of polypropylene, BPA free. Dimensions are 5.25 in H x 4.2 in W. The capacity line says 10 oz (1-2 cups), which is the one-or-two-cup use.

Chemex Classic 6-Cup CM-6A

The Chemex CM-6A page states a capacity of 30 ounce, glass of borosilicate, and a height of 8 1/2 in. Diameter is 5 1/8 in. The item line is CM-6A. The 30 ounce borosilicate carafe is the glass pour-over use.

What to look for

  1. Read which tool the card is. The KBGV page states a volume of 1.25 L / 40 oz / 10 cups and power of 1475 W at 120 V. The AeroPress page states a capacity of 10 oz (1-2 cups) and a weight of 7.75 oz with scoop and stirrer. The Chemex page states a capacity of 30 ounce and borosilicate glass.
  2. Read auto-off only on the card that prints it. The KBGV page states auto-off of 100 minutes. The AeroPress and Chemex cards do not print an auto-off line.
  3. Read materials when the page names them. The AeroPress page states polypropylene, BPA free. The Chemex page states borosilicate glass, item CM-6A, a height of 8 1/2 in, and a diameter of 5 1/8 in.
  4. Read the footprint if the counter is the constraint. The KBGV page states dimensions of 14.00 in H x 12.75 in W x 6.50 in D and a weight of 6.00 lb. The AeroPress page states 5.25 in H x 4.2 in W.

Where does the coffee maker recall line come from?
