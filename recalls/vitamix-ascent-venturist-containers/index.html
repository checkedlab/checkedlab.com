Vitamix Ascent and Venturist container recall, and the E310

The June 20, 2024 CPSC notice covers Ascent and Venturist 8-ounce and 20-ounce blending containers and blade bases. The E310 card lists an Explorian blender with a 48 oz container. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-10 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2024-06-20

Vitamix Reannounces and Expands Recall of Ascent Series and Venturist Series 8-ounce and 20-ounce Blending Containers and Blade Bases

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves all Ascent and Venturist Series 8-ounce blending containers, 20-ounce blending containers, and blade bases, including containers repaired in the August 2018 recall. It says the clear containers and black blade bases came with certain Venturist and Ascent blenders and were also sold separately. It names the Venturist V1200 and the Ascent A2300, A2500, A3300, and A3500 as examples. It says the Vitamix logo is printed on the container.

Names in the 2024 CPSC notice

  • Ascent Series 8-ounce blending containers
  • Ascent Series 20-ounce blending containers
  • Venturist Series 8-ounce blending containers
  • Venturist Series 20-ounce blending containers
  • Blade bases for those containers
  • Venturist V1200
  • Ascent A2300
  • Ascent A2500
  • Ascent A3300
  • Ascent A3500

How to check your model

The notice identifies the recalled parts as Ascent and Venturist 8-ounce and 20-ounce containers and their blade bases, including units already repaired in 2018.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners, including people who took part in the 2018 recall, to stop using the recalled 8-ounce and 20-ounce containers and blade bases and contact Vitamix for a repair kit with a protective plastic shroud and additional labels.

CPSC · 2018-08-09

Vitamix Recalls Ascent and Venturist Series Blending Containers Due to Laceration Hazard

What the notice says

The 2018 CPSC notice, which the 2024 notice expands, covered Ascent and Venturist 8-ounce and 20-ounce containers with blade date codes of March 2018 (03-18) or earlier. It says a green or orange dot on the bottom of the blade base means that container was already repaired under that recall.

Names in the 2018 CPSC notice

  • Ascent 8-ounce containers with blade date code 03-18 or earlier
  • Ascent 20-ounce containers with blade date code 03-18 or earlier
  • Venturist 8-ounce containers with blade date code 03-18 or earlier
  • Venturist 20-ounce containers with blade date code 03-18 or earlier
  • Venturist Model 1200

How to check your model

The notice says the blade date code is laser-etched on the top of the blade in the blade base, in MM-YY form.

What the notice tells owners to do

That notice told owners to stop using the recalled containers and contact Vitamix for a free repair kit. The 2024 notice above is the later instruction, including for people who already received the 2018 repair.

CPSC · 2013-08-28

Vitamix Recalls 64-Ounce Low Profile Blender Container Due to Laceration Hazard

What the notice says

A separate 2013 CPSC notice covers the Vitamix 64-ounce Low-Profile container with blade part number 103208 A and blade date codes 03-12 through 07-13. It names blender models 7500, Professional Series 300, and Professional Series 750. It says replacement blades with part number 104602 A are not included.

Names in the 2013 CPSC notice

  • 64-ounce Low-Profile container
  • Blade part number 103208 A
  • Blade date codes 03-12 through 07-13
  • Blender model 7500
  • Professional Series 300
  • Professional Series 750

How to check your model

The notice says the blade part number and date code are laser-etched on the top of the blade at the bottom of the container.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled container and contact Vitamix for a free repair.

Health Canada · 2013-08-28

Vitamix 2-Litre (64-Ounce) Low-Profile Blender Container

What the notice says

The Health Canada record dated 2013-08-28 names the Vitamix 2-litre (64-ounce) Low-Profile blender container. It is a different notice from the 2024 CPSC Ascent and Venturist container notice.

Name in the Health Canada notice

  • Vitamix 2-litre (64-ounce) Low-Profile blender container

How to check your model

Use the linked Health Canada notice for the container it describes.

What the notice tells owners to do

Follow the steps printed on the linked Health Canada notice.

Checked Lab catalogue

The E310 card is the Explorian blender. Its maker-sourced spec line says the container is 48 oz. The 2024 CPSC notice names 8-ounce and 20-ounce Ascent and Venturist containers, not an Explorian E310.
