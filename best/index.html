How these pages were chosen

A type page is published when the catalogue already has at least three models of that type. These twelve types are the ones with the most models. Air purifiers have eight. Heaters have six. Robot vacuums and stick vacuums have five each. Air fryers and mattresses have four each. Six more types have three models, and they are listed in alphabetical order: automatic pet feeders, coffee makers, humidifiers, noise-cancelling headphones, pet fountains, and pillows.

Pet fountains and automatic feeders are separate types. A slow-feed bowl is a third pet type, and it has one model, so it has no page. Reverse osmosis systems, scan tools, and snow tools also have three models. Their names come after pillows, so they are outside this set of twelve. Espresso machines, stand mixers, dash cams, dehumidifiers, automatic litter boxes, and water-filter pitchers have fewer than three models in the catalogue.

Each label names a sentence already printed on that model’s maker spec list: a room line, a CADR line, a tank, a battery line, a weight. The card order follows those labels. Prices stay on the store sites. The recall line is the catalogue match against CPSC SaferProducts and the Health Canada open dataset.

Questions

How does a product type get a page?

The catalogue is grouped by product type. A type with at least three models can have a page. These twelve types have the most models. Types with the same count are ordered A to Z.

What does a Best for label mean?

The label names the maker sentence that card is matching, such as a stated room size, a CADR, a tank capacity, or a battery line. The sentences are copied from the spec list on the model page.

Where do the recall lines come from?

The US line is the CPSC SaferProducts match. The Canadian line is the Health Canada open dataset match. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Why is the price on the store button?

Prices stay on the store sites. A US button opens Amazon.com, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, or Home Depot. A Canadian button opens Amazon.ca, Best Buy, Canadian Tire, Walmart, or Wayfair.
