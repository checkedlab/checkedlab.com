How we picked

Checked Lab lines up maker pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The Best for line keeps the qualifier the maker printed. The V15 run is up to 60 min. The IP3251 run is up to 70 minutes, measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, so the line says hand-vacuum rather than a full-stick hour. The V8 is the lightest wand on the page, at 5.58 lb, so its line is carrying. The S15 PRO run is 40 min and its bin is 0.47 L, which is not a longer run or a larger bin than the cards next to it, so that card has no Best for line.

Air watts stay on the Dyson cards that print them. The V15 page states 230 AW in Boost mode. The V8 page states 115 AW in MAX mode. The Shark, Tineco, and BISSELL cards on this page do not print an air-watt line.

Dyson V8 Cordless Vacuum

Shark PowerDetect Clean and Empty Cordless Vacuum IP3251

The IP3251 page states a run time of up to 70 minutes, measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, a weight of 18.47 lbs, and a dust cup of 0.7 qt. Charge time is 6 hours. That run-time sentence includes the hand vacuum and ECO mode, so the use stays inside those words.

Tineco PURE ONE S15 PRO Cordless Vacuum

The PURE ONE S15 PRO page states a dust capacity of 0.47 L, a run time of 40 min, and a weight of 6.8 lbs. The battery line is 2500 mAh and charge time is 4 hours. The 40 min run is shorter than the 60 min line on the V15, and 0.47 L is not a larger bin than the other cups on this page, so this card has no Best for line.

BISSELL ICONpet Cordless Vacuum 2288

The ICONpet 2288 page states a cleaning path of 10.5 in, a dirty tank of 0.4 L, and a weight of 7 lbs. Power is 22 V and the bag line is bagless. The 10.5 in cleaning path is the wider-pass use.

What to look for

  1. Read the run-time sentence with its condition. The V15 page states up to 60 min. The V8 page states 40 min maximum. The S15 PRO page states 40 min. The IP3251 page states up to 70 minutes, measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode.
  2. Read weight in the unit printed. The V15 page states 6.83 lb. The V8 page states 5.58 lb. The S15 PRO page states 6.8 lbs. The ICONpet page states 7 lbs. The IP3251 page states 18.47 lbs.
  3. Read the bin in the unit printed. The V15 page states 0.2 gallon. The V8 page states 0.14 gallon. The IP3251 page states 0.7 qt. The S15 PRO page states 0.47 L. The ICONpet page states a dirty tank of 0.4 L.
  4. Read charge time next to run time. The V15 page states 4.5 hrs. The V8 page states 5 hrs. The S15 PRO page states 4 hours. The IP3251 page states 6 hours.

Questions

Which stick vacuum card states 230 AW?

The V15 Detect Absolute card. The page states 230 AW in Boost mode, a weight of 6.83 lb, and a run time of up to 60 min.

Which stick vacuum card is the lighter Dyson?

The V8 card. The page states a weight of 5.58 lb, a run time of 40 min maximum, and suction of 115 AW in MAX mode.

What does the IP3251 70 minute line include?

The page states up to 70 minutes, measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode. Weight on that page is 18.47 lbs and charge time is 6 hours.

Where does the stick vacuum recall line come from?

The US line is the CPSC SaferProducts match for that model. The Canadian line is the Health Canada open dataset match. A notice that names a different model stays on the card as a different model.
