How to read this

Portions and power as the Whisker and PetSafe pages state them for the Feeder-Robot and the Simply Feed, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Feeder-Robot page states portions of 1/8 cup to 1 cup and a backup battery. The Simply Feed page states portions of 1/8 to 4 cups and battery power.

A wider stated portion range, up to 4 cups, is the Simply Feed line. Meals and warranty are on the Feeder-Robot card only. Capacity, schedule, and dimensions are on the Simply Feed card only.

The shared rows are portion size and power

Portions and power are the labels both pages print. Whisker states 1/8 cup to 1 cup and a backup battery. PetSafe states 1/8 to 4 cups and battery. The low end of both portion lines is 1/8. The high end differs: 1 cup on the Feeder-Robot page, 4 cups on the Simply Feed page.

Whisker also states up to 8 scheduled meals a day and a warranty of 12 months included. PetSafe also states a 24 cup capacity, up to 12 meals a day, and dimensions of 19.0 x 9.0 x 12.5 in. Meal-count labels are not the same string on the two cards, “Meals” against “Schedule,” so the meal lines stay off the table even though both pages talk about meals.

Pick by hopper size or by the meal count

Pick the Feeder-Robot page if the portion cap you are matching is 1 cup, the meal line you are matching is up to 8 scheduled meals a day, or the power line you need says backup battery. The warranty line on that page is 12 months included. A hopper capacity in cups is not on that card.

Pick the Simply Feed page if the portion cap you are matching is 4 cups, the schedule line is up to 12 meals a day, or you need the 24 cup capacity and the 19.0 x 9.0 x 12.5 in dimensions. The power line on that page says battery, without the word backup.

A backup-battery line and a battery line are both power labels, which is why they share a row, and they are not the same sentence. Read the word the page prints. The recall block is the catalogue match for each feeder.
