Heated blanket recalls: Bedsure brand check

Brand check for Bedsure, Better Homes & Gardens, Cosi Home, and OHS.

Brand check

One card is one brand. A recall card states the product, date, hazard, and remedy from the official notice. A card with no notice uses the search pages opened on November 20, 2026.

Bedsure

CPSC recall

Product. Bedsure electric heating blankets and pads. Models BS-HB5060, BS-HB6284, BS-HB7284, BS-LV0A, BS-HP2422, BS-HP1224, and BS-HP2433.

Date. 2023-03-09. Hazard. The controller can malfunction, posing fire and thermal burn hazards. Remedy. Stop using them and contact Bedsure for a full refund. The notice says the owner must have the unit and submit photos.

CPSC notice

Better Homes & Gardens

No recall found

No CPSC or Health Canada recall found for Better Homes & Gardens heated blankets as of Nov 2026.

The CPSC Better Homes and Gardens room spray notice is not a heated blanket. The Health Canada search also returns a torch notice, not a heated blanket.

JKMAX

Two notices

Product. JKMAX heated blankets and heating pads. Models JKMAX-002, JKMAX-7284B, JKMAX-6284B, JKMAX-10090B, and JKMAX-8490B.

Date. CPSC 2026-09-17. Health Canada updated 2026-10-22. Hazard. Both notices say the internal wire heating elements can overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. Remedy. The CPSC notice says stop using them and contact JKMAX for a full refund. The Health Canada notice says stop using them and dispose of them so they cannot be used again.

CPSC notice Health Canada notice

OCTROT

CPSC recall

Product. OCTROT heated throws and blankets. Models OCT-5060, OCT-5062, OCT-6248, OCT-7284, and OCT-8490, printed on the packaging.

Date. 2026-07-30. Hazard. The electrical cord can detach and ignite, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire, burn, and shock. Remedy. Stop using them and contact OCTROT for a full refund. The notice says to unplug the cord, cut it in half, and send a photo of the cut cord.

CPSC notice

MaxKare

CPSC recall

Product. MaxKare electric blankets. Models HB18A-7284-1, HB18A-8490-2, and HB18A-6284-1, on the tag.

Date. 2025-06-18. Hazard. The blankets can overheat and ignite, posing burn and fire hazards. Remedy. Stop using them and contact Yumo for disposal instructions. The notice says a photo and confirmation of disposal are required for a full refund.

CPSC notice

Berkshire

CPSC recall

Product. Berkshire heated throws and blankets. Throws and blankets sold at L.L. Bean are L.L. Bean and Berkshire branded. The notice identifies them by tracker ranges on the wash label.

Date. 2023-11-30. Hazard. The electric throws and blankets may overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. Remedy. Stop using them and contact Berkshire Blanket & Home Co. for a full refund. The notice says to submit a photo of the destroyed blanket showing the cut cord, and a photo of the wash label after writing Refund and an assigned refund code on it.

CPSC notice

Sunbeam

Two notices

Product. Sunbeam queen-size heated blanket, model 32810027, printed on the wash label.

Date. 2023-03-16 on both notices. Hazard. The CPSC notice says the heated blankets can overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. The Health Canada notice says model 32810027 can potentially overheat, posing possible burn or fire hazards. Remedy. Both notices say stop using and unplug the blanket and contact Star Elite for a full refund. Both say to cut the power cord at the plug, mark an X on the wash label, and send a photo.

Date. 2020-11-12. Hazard. The blankets can overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. Remedy. Stop using them and contact InvoSpa for instructions on a full refund.

CPSC notice

Rural King

CPSC recall

Product. Rural King electric heated blankets and throws. Model numbers start with BLV-OB and end in the suffixes listed on the notice.

Date. 2018-02-15. Hazard. The electric cord can overheat and catch on fire, posing fire and burn hazards. Remedy. Stop using them and contact Rural King for a full refund.

CPSC notice

LUXE+WILLOW

CPSC recall

Product. LUXE+WILLOW heated blankets. The notice also prints L & W BIO on the label.

Date. 2022-04-21. Hazard. The blanket can overheat if left plugged in and powered on for an extended period, posing fire and burn hazards. Remedy. Stop using it and contact MWA for a full refund or a voucher. The notice says to cut the cord and send a photo of the cut cord and the label. The voucher amount is printed on the notice.

CPSC notice

Classic Beautyrest

CPSC recall

Product. Classic Beautyrest electric throw, model BST-06-THR, date codes B00106 through B36506.

Date. 2007-08-15. Hazard. Bunching, folding, or tucking can cause the throws to overheat, resulting in smoldering, melting, fire, and burn hazards. Remedy. Stop using the throws. The notice says the firm is out of business, a remedy is no longer available, and to destroy or discard the throw.

CPSC notice

Bilt-Safe

CPSC recall

Product. Bilt-Safe and Comfort Max electric blankets, models BST-03-A-F, BST-03-A-K, BST-03-A-Q, and BST-03-A-T.

Date. 2004-11-09. Hazard. The blanket can overheat when the controller is reset multiple times or the blanket is folded or covered, which can result in smoldering, melting, and a burn hazard. Remedy. Stop using the blankets and destroy the controller and the blanket. The notice says the firm is out of business and a remedy is no longer available.

CPSC notice

Perfect Fit

CPSC recall

Product. SOIREE blankets sold at Belk, and Soft n' Warm blankets sold at K-mart. The Soft n' Warm date codes on the notice run from 02228 through 02305.

Date. 2003-07-02. Hazard. These blankets can overheat, posing a risk of burn injuries, especially when the blanket is folded or bunched. Remedy. Unplug the recalled blankets. The notice says the firm is providing a free replacement blanket.

CPSC notice

Vellux

CPSC recall

Product. Vellux fahrenheit heated blankets manufactured July 2002 through January 2003.

Date. 2003-04-24. Hazard. The heating element can overheat when the blanket is folded, causing the element and the blanket to melt and posing a risk of thermal burns. Remedy. The notice says the firm is no longer in business and gives a WestPoint Home number to call for information.

CPSC notice

Lands' End

CPSC recall

Product. Lands' End Polartec Heat blankets, style numbers 73176, 73197, and 73198.

Date. 2002-01-28. Hazard. Some blankets may contain a loose connection that can keep the unit from working or cause a short circuit, posing a risk of fire. Remedy. Stop using the blanket and contact Lands' End for a full refund and a gift certificate. The certificate amount is printed on the notice.

CPSC notice

Biddeford

CPSC recall

Product. Biddeford electric blankets sold as Controlled Comfort, EH 2000, Starbright, Staywarm, Supreme 21, and Warm Comfort, serial numbers A001A to C210C.

Date. 2001-10-05. Hazard. The plug that connects the detachable control to the blanket can become loose, and the poor contact can overheat, posing a fire hazard. Remedy. The notice says the firm is no longer in business, the remedy is no longer available, and to discard the blanket and not donate or resell it.

CPSC notice

Is mine affected?

  • Where to look. Read the model number on the wash label or the tag sewn to the blanket, and on the controller. Berkshire uses tracker numbers on the wash label. OCTROT prints the model on the packaging.
  • Bedsure, Sunbeam, Berkshire. Bedsure models are BS-HB5060, BS-HB6284, BS-HB7284, BS-LV0A, BS-HP2422, BS-HP1224 and BS-HP2433, on a white label on the back and on the controller. Sunbeam queen model 32810027 is on the wash label. Berkshire and L.L. Bean throws and blankets in that notice are identified by the tracker ranges in the table. Bedsure CPSC notice Sunbeam CPSC notice Sunbeam Health Canada notice Berkshire CPSC notice
  • What to do. If the number on your blanket is in the table, stop using it and follow that row. The link is the official notice, and the remedy is not the same on every row. The Health Canada JKMAX notice says to dispose of the blanket. The CPSC JKMAX notice says to contact JKMAX for a refund.

If you're replacing it

Match the model on the wash label before you retire a blanket. The Sunbeam queen-size notice names 32810027. The Sunbeam throw on the winter pages is TSL8WS, a different model.

What the notices say

CPSC and Health Canada notices for Bedsure, Berkshire, Sunbeam, JKMAX, OCTROT and other heated blankets and throws, with the model or tracker and remedy. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-19 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2026-09-17

JKMAX Recalls Heated Blankets and Heating Pads Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves all heating pads and heated blankets sold by JKMAX, including the model numbers below. It says they are blue, gray, brown or red polyester, with a detachable white remote. It says about 195,000 units. It says JKMAX is aware of 577 reports of blankets smoking, melting or burning, including 53 burn injuries.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • JKMAX-002
  • JKMAX-7284B
  • JKMAX-6284B
  • JKMAX-10090B
  • JKMAX-8490B

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the back of the remote and on a white label on the packaging, with the brand name JKMAX.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the blankets and pads immediately and contact JKMAX for a full refund.

Health Canada · 2026-10-22

Health Canada warns that JKMAX Heated Blankets and Heating Pads previously available on Amazon.ca may pose a risk of fire or burns

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice, updated 2026-10-22, says the alert involves JKMAX heated blankets and heating pads, including the same model numbers as the CPSC notice. It says the model number is on the back of the remote and on a white label on the packaging. The remedy on this page is not the refund named on the CPSC notice.

Model numbers in the Health Canada notice

  • JKMAX-002
  • JKMAX-7284B
  • JKMAX-6284B
  • JKMAX-10090B
  • JKMAX-8490B

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the back of the remote and on a white label on the packaging, with the brand name JKMAX.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the affected products immediately and dispose of them so they cannot be used again.

CPSC · 2026-07-30

Heated Sherpa Throws and Blankets Recalled Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Fire and Burn Hazards; Sold on Amazon by OCTROT

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves OCTROT sherpa heated throws and blankets with a white controller that has five temperature settings. It says the controller cord has two or four small holes at the lower connection. It says about 178,000 units. It says the firm has received 555 reports of blankets or throws melting, sparking and smoking, resulting in 27 fires and 15 burn or electrical shock injuries.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • OCT-5060
  • OCT-5062
  • OCT-6248
  • OCT-7284
  • OCT-8490

How to check your model

The notice says the brand name and the model number are printed on the product packaging.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the throws and blankets immediately and contact OCTROT for a full refund. It says to unplug the cord, cut it in half, and send a photo of the destroyed product showing the cut cord.

CPSC · 2025-06-18

MaxKare Electric Blankets Recalled Due to Burn and Fire Hazards; Manufactured by Yumo and Sold Exclusively on Walmart.com
