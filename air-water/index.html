How to choose air and water gear

This hub mixes four jobs: cleaning a room's air, adding humidity, pulling water out of the air, and filtering drinking water. They do not share one spec. An air purifier card is about airflow and particle removal.

A humidifier card is about tank size and run time. A dehumidifier card is about pints removed in stated conditions. A water filter card is about certifications, capacity, and flow.

Read the card that matches the job, then match the model number on the unit to the listing before you trust the recall line.

On a purifier, keep the room size and the air-change rate together. The Coway Airmega Mighty page lists a smoke CADR of 233 and 361 ft² at 4.8 air changes per hour. The Levoit Core 600S page lists 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour and 606 sq ft at 4.8, with a CADR of 391 CFM.

The Coway Airmega 250 page lists 465 sq ft at 4 air changes per hour and 1,860 sq ft at 1. The Winix 5500-2 manual states an AHAM verified 360 sq ft. The larger square footage is often the one-air-change sentence. Mighty and Core 300S-P share only size and weight, because the Levoit card does not print CADR.

Filter words need the standard the maker named. The Honeywell HPA300 page lists filtration of up to 99.97 percent of particles that pass through the filter, and 4.8 air changes an hour in a 465 sq ft room. The Aquasana Claryum page names NSF/ANSI 42, 53 including P473, and 401, with 500 gallons or up to 6 months.

The Waterdrop G3P600 page names NSF/ANSI 42, 58, and 372, 8 stages, and 600 gallons per day. The iSpring RCC7 page lists 5 stages, 75 gallons per day, and NSF/ANSI 58. Gallons per day are not gallons of pitcher capacity.

Pitchers and machines that move water have their own traps. The Brita Tahoe page lists 10 cups and a 6 month filter life. The ZeroWater ZP-010 page also states 10 cups, and it states an included TDS meter. Tahoe and ZP-010 are the pair.

Humidifier tanks are not interchangeable claims either. The Levoit Classic 300S page lists a 6 L tank, up to 60 hours, and 505 ft². The Levoit LV600HH page lists 6 liters, up to 50 hours, and warm and cool mist.

Dehumidifier pints depend on the test condition. hOmeLabs states up to 50 pints a day at 80 F and 60 percent relative humidity, and a higher maximum at 95 F and 90 percent.

Ignore a CADR borrowed from a sibling model. Ignore a room size that drops the air-change rate. Ignore an NSF line that belongs to a different filter.

The recall check is separate from all of that. US cards match the CPSC SaferProducts feed. Canadian cards match Health Canada's open dataset.

Search the model on CPSC Recalls and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. A clear line on the Mighty does not cover the Airmega 250. How we check defines the date. Core 600S and Winix 5500-2 and HPA300 and Airmega 250 are the other purifier pairs.

Questions

Why does one purifier list two room sizes?

The maker attached different air-change rates. Core 600S states 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour and 606 sq ft at 4.8. Airmega 250 states 1,860 sq ft at 1 air change and 465 sq ft at 4.

Keep the rate with the number.

What is CADR on these cards?

A clean-air delivery figure the maker printed, often split by smoke, dust, and pollen. The Mighty page lists 233 smoke, 246 dust, and 240 pollen. If the other card does not print CADR, the comparison leaves that row out.

How should I read a water-filter certification?

Read the standard the maker named for that model. Claryum names NSF/ANSI 42, 53 including P473, and 401. Waterdrop G3P600 names NSF/ANSI 42, 58, and 372.

A certification on one filter is not a certification for the next model.

How does the recall check work here?

The US line matches the model to the CPSC SaferProducts feed. The Canadian line matches it to Health Canada's open dataset. Search CPSC Recalls or Health Canada recalls and safety alerts for the same model number.

A clear Mighty line does not cover the Airmega 250.

Are humidifier hours and dehumidifier pints the same kind of number?

No. A humidifier hour is how long the maker says the tank lasts at a stated setting. A dehumidifier pint figure is water removed per day at a stated temperature and humidity. hOmeLabs prints both an 80 F figure and a 95 F figure.
