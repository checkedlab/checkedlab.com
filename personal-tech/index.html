How to choose personal tech

Personal tech on this hub covers headphones, speakers, cameras, chargers, and a few body-care tools. The specs that survive a listing are the ones with a unit: hours, watts, a water-resistance code, a weight. A listening-time claim is only as good as the condition the maker attached.

Noise cancelling on, noise cancelling off, and a quick-charge minute are different sentences. Match the model number. WH-1000XM5, QuietComfort Ultra, AirPods Pro 3, and OpenRun Pro 2 are four cards, not one category of 'good headphones'.

Headphone hours have to keep their conditions. The Sony WH-1000XM5 page states a maximum of 30 hours with noise cancelling on, 40 hours with it off, about 8.82 oz, and a charge time of about 3.5 hours. The Bose QuietComfort Ultra page states up to 24 hours and 0.56 lb. WH-1000XM5 and QuietComfort Ultra is that pair.

The AirPods Pro 3 page states up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation and an IP57 rating. The Shokz OpenRun Pro 2 page states up to 12 hours, a 5 minute charge for about 150 minutes at 75 percent volume, and IP55. AirPods Pro 3 and OpenRun Pro 2 is the open-ear pair. IP55 and IP57 are the codes the pages print.

They are not a single 'waterproof' word.

Power banks and chargers are limited by the wattage and the capacity the page states. The Anker 737 page states 24,000 mAh and 140 W maximum on USB-C, at 22.22 oz. The Anker Nano page states 30 W maximum and the profiles 5 V at 3 A, 9 V at 3 A, 15 V at 2 A, and 20 V at 1.5 A.

A 140 W bank and a 30 W wall charger solve different problems. The Anker power-bank explainer is the page that quotes notices naming specific banks. Read it before you treat every Anker model as the same object.

Milliamp hours and watts are both useful and they are not substitutes.

Everything else on the hub has one hard spec worth finding. The GoPro HERO13 page states 5.3K60 video and waterproofing to 33 ft. The JBL Charge 5 sheet states up to 20 hours and IP67.

The Logitech MX Master 3S page states an 8,000 DPI sensor. The Keychron V3 page states an 80 percent layout and 1000 Hz polling. The Oral-B iO Series 3 page states three modes.

The Waterpik WP-660 page states 10 pressure settings and a 22 ounce reservoir. Pick the unit that matches the job. A speaker hour and a headphone hour are both hours, and they still belong to different pages.

Ignore a battery hour that drops the condition. Thirty hours with noise cancelling on is the Sony sentence. Forty hours is the sentence with it off.

Ignore a waterproof word when the page printed a code or a depth instead. The recall check is the model match. US cards use the CPSC SaferProducts feed.

Canadian cards use Health Canada's open dataset. Search CPSC Recalls and Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. A clear line on one Anker model does not cover another. How we check defines the date. Gift guides for tech regroup some of these cards.

Questions

Why do headphone battery lines disagree with the box slogan?

Because the maker often prints more than one condition. Sony states a maximum of 30 hours with noise cancelling on and 40 hours with it off. Bose states up to 24 hours.

AirPods Pro 3 states up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation. Keep the condition with the hour.

What is the difference between IP55 and IP57 on these cards?

They are the codes the pages print, not a shared word. Shokz states IP55 for the OpenRun Pro 2. Apple states IP57 for AirPods Pro 3.

JBL states IP67 for the Charge 5. GoPro states a depth, 33 ft, instead of an IP code. Read the code on the card.

How do I choose between the two Anker chargers?

By the job the number describes. The 737 page states a 24,000 mAh pack and 140 W maximum USB-C output. The Nano page states a 30 W wall charger and four voltage profiles.

The power-bank explainer is the notice list for specific banks, not a spec sheet for every Anker model.

How does the recall check work for headphones or a charger?

The US line matches the brand and model to the CPSC SaferProducts feed. The Canadian line uses Health Canada's open dataset. A clear line on one Anker model does not cover another.

Search CPSC Recalls or Health Canada recalls and safety alerts with the model number on the card.

Where do the weights on the headphone cards come from?

From the maker pages. Sony states about 8.82 oz for the WH-1000XM5. Bose states 0.56 lb for the QuietComfort Ultra.
