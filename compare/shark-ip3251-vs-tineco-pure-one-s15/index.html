How to read this

Weight, run time, and charge time as the Shark and Tineco pages state them for the IP3251 and the PURE ONE S15 PRO, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The IP3251 page states 18.47 lbs, up to 70 minutes measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, and a 6 hour charge time. The PURE ONE S15 PRO page states 6.8 lbs, a run time of 40 min, and a charge time of 4 hours.

A lower stated weight and a shorter stated charge time are the Tineco lines. A longer stated run time is the Shark line, and that line names the hand vacuum in ECO mode. Dust cup, dimensions, and voltage are on the Shark card only. Battery capacity and dust capacity are on the Tineco card only.

The run-time lines name different setups

The shared labels are weight, run time, and charge time. Shark prints 18.47 lbs, up to 70 minutes measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, and 6 hours. Tineco prints 6.8 lbs, 40 min, and 4 hours. The Shark run-time sentence names a configuration, the hand vacuum in ECO mode. The Tineco run-time field prints 40 min without that phrase.

Shark also states a 0.7 qt dust cup, dimensions of 10.24 in L x 18.5 in W x 46.46 in H, and 21.6 volts. Tineco also states a 2500 mAh battery and a 0.47 L dust capacity. Dust cup and dust capacity are different labels, so they stay off the table.

Pick by weight or by the ECO-mode sentence

Pick the IP3251 page if the run-time sentence you are matching is up to 70 minutes at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, or you need the 0.7 qt dust cup, the 21.6 volt line, and the full-size dimensions. The stated weight on that page is 18.47 lbs, and the charge line is 6 hours.

Pick the PURE ONE S15 PRO page if the weight you are matching is 6.8 lbs, the charge line is 4 hours, or you need the 2500 mAh battery and the 0.47 L dust capacity. The run-time figure on that page is 40 min, shorter than the Shark ECO-mode line.

The Shark cards in this catalogue link a 2015 CPSC notice for Shark Rotator Lift-Away vacuums. That notice names NV650W, NV651, NV652, and NV660. IP3251 is not one of those model numbers. The Health Canada notice from the same date names NV650W and NV652. Read the sticker the notice describes. The recall block below is the catalogue match for IP3251 and for the S15 PRO.
