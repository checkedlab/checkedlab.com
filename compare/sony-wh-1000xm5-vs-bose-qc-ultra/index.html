How to read this

Weight, charge time, and battery life as the Sony and Bose pages state them for the WH-1000XM5 and the QuietComfort Ultra, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-13 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Sony specifications page states approx. 8.82 oz, a charge time of approx. 3.5 hours, and max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on or max. 40 hours with it off. The Bose specifications table states 0.56 lb, a 3 hour charge time, and up to 24 hours.

The weight lines use the units each page prints, ounces on the Sony page and pounds on the Bose page. A longer stated playback line with noise cancelling on is the Sony line. A shorter stated playback line is the Bose line. A shorter stated charge time is on the Bose specifications table.
