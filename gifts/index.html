Questions

What does the recall line on a gift pick mean?

It is the recorded check for that model. On the US view the source is CPSC. On the CA view the source is Health Canada. The date is the day that check was recorded. No matching recall was found for that model as of that date. A notice for a different model can still exist, and the model page links it.

Where do the spec lines come from?

Each spec line is copied from the maker fields stored for that model. The model page links the maker source.

Which recipient lists are published?

The lists are the recipient groups this catalogue can fill with at least five models: Home cook, Coffee lovers, The home, Pet owners, Tech and gadgets, The garage, and Sleep.
