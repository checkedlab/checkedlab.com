How to read this

Capacity and weight as the Brita and ZeroWater pages state them for the Tahoe Elite and the ZP-010, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Tahoe Elite page states a 10 cup capacity and 1.5 lb. The ZP-010 page titles a 10 cup pitcher, and the specifications table states 4 lb.

Both capacity lines say 10 cup. A lower stated weight is the Tahoe Elite line. Height, width, and filter life are on the Brita card only. Dimensions, certifications, and the included TDS meter are on the ZeroWater card only.
