How to read this

The NC301 uses 16 oz pints and 7 programs, and the NC501 uses 24 oz tubs and more programs.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-13 (UTC) catalogue check.

The NC301 card says 7 preset programs, two 16 oz pints, dishwasher-safe containers, lids, and paddle, and a one-year limited warranty. The NC501 page is the XL Deluxe. Its box list says 11 one-touch programs and two 24 oz XL tubs. The same page’s spec table says 12 automatic programs.

Both warranty lines are one year. The pint size and the program count are the split. Ninja prints two program counts on the NC501 page, and this page does not pick one of them for you.

The Deluxe page disagrees with itself on programs

SharkNinja’s NC501 page titles the machine the CREAMi XL Deluxe and prints the model NC501. In the box list it says 11 one-touch programs. In the specification block it says 12 automatic programs. Both sentences are on that page. A shopper who quotes only one of them is quoting half of what Ninja printed. The NC301 card says 7 preset programs, and that card does not have a second program count.

Pints are easier. NC301 includes two 16 oz pints. NC501 includes two 24 oz CREAMi XL tubs, plus an outer bowl and lid and a Deluxe Creamerizer paddle. The NC501 page also says all parts are dishwasher safe, and the spec block says dishwasher safe: yes. The NC301 line is narrower: containers, lids, and paddle. Read the noun in the sentence before you assume every loose part on the NC301 is covered by the NC501 wording.

Size is published for the Deluxe

The NC501 spec block lists 15.43 lb, 18.9 in long, 10.04 in wide, and 19.88 in high, 120 volts, and 800 watts. Those labels are not on the NC301 card, so weight, dimensions, and wattage stay off the shared table and under the fold. A Ninja support note for the NC500 series says the Deluxe pint’s maximum fill line is 24 oz, which matches the tub size on the product page.

The product title on the NC501 page says 11-in-1, and the box list says 11 one-touch programs, while the spec table says 12. If a listing you are reading uses a different count, match it to one of those two official lines or walk away from the listing. This catalogue does not have a separate model page for the NC501.

Pick the pint size, then read the program line twice

Pick the NC301 if the pints you are matching are 16 oz, the program line is 7 presets, and the care line names the containers, lids, and paddle. The warranty on that card is one year limited. That is the machine with a model page here.

Pick the NC501 if the tubs you are matching are 24 oz and you have decided what to do with the 11-versus-12 program lines on Ninja’s own page. The warranty line there is also one year limited. The 15.43 lb weight and the 18.9 by 10.04 by 19.88 in cabinet are the size figures on that spec block.

A one-year warranty on both pages does not make the machines the same. The container volume is the line that changes the batch you can freeze.
