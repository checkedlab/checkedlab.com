The lines that answer a headphone match

The Sony specifications page states a weight of about 8.82 oz, a charge time of about 3.5 hours, and battery life of max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on or max. 40 hours with it off. The product page also states the model WH-1000XM5, that 2 processors control 8 microphones, and multipoint. Worth it means you are buying that model and those battery and weight lines are the ones you meant to match.

The QuietComfort Ultra specifications table states up to 24 hours, a weight of 0.56 lb, and a 3 hour charge, plus Quiet, Aware, and Immersion modes, multipoint for two devices, and Bose Immersive Audio. The weight units differ: ounces on the Sony page, pounds on the Bose page. This page does not convert them.

Pick the longer battery line or the shorter charge line

Pick the WH-1000XM5 if the battery line you are matching is max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on, or max. 40 hours with it off, and the weight line you are matching is about 8.82 oz. The charge line that goes with that page is about 3.5 hours. Pick it if the microphone sentence, 2 processors and 8 microphones, is the noise-cancelling description you meant.

Pick the QuietComfort Ultra if the battery line you are matching is up to 24 hours, the charge line is 3 hours, and the mode line names Quiet, Aware, and Immersion. A shorter stated charge time is the Bose line. A longer stated playback line with noise cancelling on is the Sony line. Multipoint appears on both cards, with the Bose card adding “two devices.”

Neither card is a US or Canadian recall match on the catalogue date below. Confirm WH-1000XM5 on the box if that is the model you are buying. The Bose card is a different model with its own battery sentence. The comparison page for this pair repeats only the labels both cards share, which are battery, weight, and charge time, and it leaves the microphone line and the mode line on the separate cards.
