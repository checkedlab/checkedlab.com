How to read this

The Pro 3 listens for up to 8 hours and tracks heart rate, and the Pro 2 listens for up to 6 hours.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-13 (UTC) catalogue check.

Apple’s AirPods Pro 3 card says up to 8 hours of listening with Active Noise Cancellation, a heart-rate sensor, a Hearing Aid feature, and an IP57 mark. The USB-C AirPods Pro 2 tech specs say up to 6 hours of listening on a single charge, IP54, and an H2 chip.

The US shop path that used to name AirPods Pro 2 now opens the AirPods Pro 3 buy page. Apple still lists a refurbished AirPods Pro 2 with a MagSafe charging case (USB-C) on the US store. The specs below are the USB-C tech specs, not the older Lightning sheet.

What Apple still prints for Pro 2

The support page titled AirPods Pro 2 with MagSafe Charging Case (USB-C) says the year introduced is 2023. Listening is up to 6 hours on a single charge, and up to 5.5 hours with Spatial Audio and head tracking. Apple’s test note for that 6-hour figure says Active Noise Cancellation was enabled, volume was at 50 percent, and the playlist was AAC. The Pro 3 card states the 8-hour line with Active Noise Cancellation in the spec itself. The Pro 2 bullet does not put those words in the bullet. The test note does.

With the case, the Pro 2 page says up to 30 hours of listening. The Pro 3 card on this site does not carry a case-hours line, so that row stays off the table. Resistance is IP54 for the Pro 2 earbuds and case. The Pro 3 card says IP57. Hearing health on the Pro 2 page lists a Hearing Test, a Hearing Aid feature, and Hearing Protection. The Pro 3 card’s hearing line names the Hearing Aid feature. The heart-rate sensor is on the Pro 3 card and is not in the Pro 2 sensor list.

Which listing is actually on the US shop

Opening Apple’s US address for buying AirPods Pro 2 currently shows the AirPods Pro 3 buy page, including the 8-hour line and the IP57 description. The refurbished product page for AirPods Pro 2 with the USB-C MagSafe case is a separate Apple listing, and it repeats up to 6 hours on one charge and up to 30 hours with the case. If you want a new pair from that US shop path, the page in front of you is the Pro 3. If you want the Pro 2, use the refurbished listing or another Apple storefront that still names it, and match the USB-C case in the title.

The Pro 2 box list on the tech specs includes four silicone tip sizes, XS through L, and a USB-C charge cable. The Pro 3 buy page says five tip sizes, XXS through L, and says a USB-C charge cable is not in the box. Tip count and the cable are not shared labels on the cards, so they stay in this note.

Pick the hour line and the listing type

Pick the Pro 3 if the listening line you are matching is up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, you want the heart-rate sensor, and the resistance mark you are matching is IP57. That is the model with a page in this catalogue, and it is the model the US new-buy path shows.

Pick the Pro 2 if the listening line you are matching is up to 6 hours on a single charge, the case line is up to 30 hours, and the resistance mark is IP54, on the USB-C tech specs. Then confirm the listing is the refurbished USB-C pair, or another current Apple listing that still uses the Pro 2 name. The H2 chip is on that tech specs page.

The Sony and Bose comparison is a different kind of headphone. The Shokz comparison uses the Pro 3 card. Neither of those pages describes the Pro 2.
