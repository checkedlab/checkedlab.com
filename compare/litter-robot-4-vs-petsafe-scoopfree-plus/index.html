How to read this

Power, cleaning interval, and household line as the Whisker and PetSafe pages state them for Litter-Robot 4 and the ScoopFree Crystal Plus, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Litter-Robot 4 page states 15 V DC, up to 8 days before emptying the waste drawer in a 1-cat household, and suitability for up to 4 cats. The ScoopFree Crystal Plus page states plug-in power, up to 30 days of cleaning, and single or multi-cat households.

A longer stated cleaning interval is the ScoopFree line. The Litter-Robot line names a waste-drawer interval for one cat. Height, footprint, entryway, and empty weight are on the Litter-Robot card only. Tray dimensions are on the ScoopFree card only.
