Is it worth it?

A plain look at who each product suits, and which spec is worth weighing.

  • Is the Litter-Robot 4 worth it? The Litter-Robot 4 suits a household that can spare 29.5 in of height and a 22 × 27 in footprint, and that wants up to 8 days before emptying the waste drawer for one cat, with a stated cap of 4 cats. If the box has to be much shorter, or you want a longer cleaning interval, weigh the ScoopFree Crystal Plus.
  • Is the Vitamix E310 worth it? The E310 suits someone who wants a blender with a 2 HP motor, a 48 oz container, and a 5-year full warranty, if a cabinet of 9 × 8 × 18 in and a weight of 10.5 lb will fit. The Cuisinart food processor on the kitchen list is a different tool, and it has a US recall.
  • Is the Sony WH-1000XM5 worth it? The WH-1000XM5 suits someone who wants over-ear headphones with max. 30 hours of battery with noise cancelling on, or max. 40 hours with it off, at about 8.82 oz, and can wait about 3.5 hours to charge. Weigh the Bose QuietComfort Ultra if you want a 3 hour charge and up to 24 hours of battery.
  • Is the Zojirushi rice cooker worth it? The NS-ZCC10 suits someone who wants a rice cooker for up to 5.5 cups and has 8-1/8 inches of height for it. An Instant Pot Duo Plus can cook rice too, but it is a 6 quart multi-cooker, not a dedicated rice pot.
  • Is the Breville Barista Express worth it? The Barista Express suits someone who wants one machine that grinds the beans and pulls the espresso. The Baratza Encore ESP is a separate grinder, and the Fellow Stagg EKG is a kettle with temperature control.

How to use this list

Each page says who the product suits and which spec to weigh: a cabinet height, a run time, a jar size, or a battery life. Prices stay on the store.

If those specs do not fit, the page names another model already listed here. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-13 (UTC).
