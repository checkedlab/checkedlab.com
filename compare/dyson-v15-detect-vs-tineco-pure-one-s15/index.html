How to read this

Weight, run time, and charge time as the Dyson and Tineco pages state them for the V15 Detect Absolute and the PURE ONE S15 PRO, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The V15 Detect Absolute page states 6.83 lb, up to 60 min of run time, and a 4.5 hr charge time. The PURE ONE S15 PRO page states 6.8 lbs, a run time of 40 min, and a charge time of 4 hours.

A longer stated run time is the V15 line. A shorter stated charge time is the Tineco line. Bin volume, filtration, and suction are on the V15 card only. Battery capacity and dust capacity are on the Tineco card only.

Three shared labels, close weights

Weight, run time, and charge time are the labels both pages print. Dyson states 6.83 lb, up to 60 min, and 4.5 hrs. Tineco states 6.8 lbs, 40 min, and 4 hours. The weight lines are close and use pounds on both pages. The run-time lines are not close: up to 60 min on the Dyson page, 40 min on the Tineco page.

Dyson also states a 0.2 gallon bin, whole-machine HEPA filtration, and 230 AW in Boost mode. Tineco also states a 2500 mAh battery and a dust capacity of 0.47 L. Bin volume and dust capacity are different labels and different units, so they stay off the shared table.

Pick by run time, charge time, or the filtration line

Pick the V15 Detect Absolute page if the run-time figure you are matching is up to 60 min, the suction figure you are matching is 230 AW in Boost mode, or the filtration line you need is whole-machine HEPA. The charge line that goes with that page is 4.5 hrs. The bin line is 0.2 gallon.

Pick the PURE ONE S15 PRO page if the run-time figure you are matching is 40 min, the charge line is 4 hours, or you need the 2500 mAh battery line and the 0.47 L dust-capacity line. The stated weight, 6.8 lbs, sits next to Dyson’s 6.83 lb. Both weights are as the maker pages state them.

A Health Canada notice linked from the Dyson V15 card names a FirstPower replacement battery for Dyson V6 series vacuums. That notice names V6, not the V15 Detect Absolute. Read the model on the notice before applying it here. The recall block is the catalogue match for each cordless vacuum.
