How we picked

Checked Lab lines up maker pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The Best for line is a use a shopper can act on. A stated room size, a smoke or pollen CADR, or a noise floor of 22 dB can carry a line. A cabinet weight cannot. Square feet are read inside the sentence that prints them. A room at 4.8 air changes per hour, an AHAM verified room, a recommended room, and a room at 1 air change per hour are four different sentences.

The grid has six cards. The Airmega 250 and the Vital 200S-P stay linked below. The 465 sq ft figure is already on the HPA300 card, and the once-per-hour figures sit in that note.

Levoit Core 300S-P Smart Air Purifier

The Core 300S-P manual states noise of 22–54.5 dB, dimensions of 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in, and a rated power of 39 W. The low end of that range is 22 dB, a quiet figure, so this is the bedroom card.

Winix 5500-2 Air Purifier

The Winix 5500-2 page states a room of AHAM verified 360 sq ft, CADR of 243 dust / 246 pollen / 232 smoke, and a weight of about 15 lb. Power on that page is 70 W, 120 V / 60 Hz. That AHAM room sentence is the large-room use, up to 360 sq ft.

Coway Airmega Mighty AP-1512HH

The Airmega Mighty AP-1512HH page states room coverage of 361 ft² at 4.8 air changes per hour, CADR of 233 smoke, 246 dust, 240 pollen, and a weight of 12.3 lb. Power on that page is 77 W. The dust and pollen CADR figures, next to the 361 ft² room sentence, are the pollen-and-dust use on this card.

Honeywell HPA300 True HEPA Air Purifier

The HPA300 page states a smoke CADR of 300, air changes of 4.8 per hour in a 465 sq ft room, and a weight of 17 lb. Dust CADR on that page is 320 and pollen CADR is 300. The smoke CADR of 300, in a 465 sq ft room at 4.8 air changes, is the smoke use on this card.

Blueair Blue Pure 211i Max

The Blue Pure 211i Max page states a room of 674 sq ft recommended, a weight of 16.2 lb, and sound of 23 to 53 dB. Dimensions on that page are 22.7 x 14.4 x 14.4 in. The recommended-room sentence, 674 sq ft, is the largest room line on this page. Sound of 23 to 53 dB is quoted beside it.

Levoit Core 600S-P Smart Air Purifier

The Core 600S-P page states a room size of 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour, or 606 sq ft at 4.8, CADR of 391 CFM, and a weight of 13.7 lb / 6.2 kg. Power on that page is 49 W, AC 120 V, 60 Hz. The 606 sq ft half of that sentence, at 4.8 air changes, is the room use. The once-per-hour figure is a different sentence and stays in the quote.

What to look for

  1. Read the room sentence to the end. The AP-1512HH line is 361 ft² at 4.8 air changes per hour. The HPA300 line is 4.8 per hour in a 465 sq ft room. The 211i Max line is 674 sq ft recommended. The 5500-2 line is AHAM verified 360 sq ft. The Core 600S-P line gives both 606 sq ft at 4.8 and 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour.
  2. Read the CADR unit. Coway and Winix print smoke, dust, and pollen CADR. Honeywell prints a smoke CADR of 300. The Core 600S-P prints 391 CFM. The Core 300S-P card does not print a CADR label.
  3. Read noise when the room line is absent. The Core 300S-P page states 22–54.5 dB. The 211i Max page states 23 to 53 dB. Weight is still printed when the page gives it: the HPA300 page states 17 lb and the 211i Max page states 16.2 lb.
  4. Read the filtration sentence on the model that prints one. The HPA300 page states up to 99.97% of particles that pass through the filter, and a True HEPA filter life of once per year. That sentence is about particles that pass through the filter.

Questions

Which air purifier is the bedroom card?

The Core 300S-P card. The manual states noise of 22–54.5 dB, dimensions of 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in, and a rated power of 39 W. The bedroom line uses the 22 dB low end.

Why are 360 sq ft and 361 ft² on different cards?

The Winix 5500-2 line says AHAM verified 360 sq ft. The AP-1512HH line says 361 ft² at 4.8 air changes per hour. The sentences name the figure differently, so each card keeps its own sentence.

Where is the Airmega 250?

It is linked under Also listed. Its coverage line is 465 sq ft at 4 air changes per hour, and its large-room line is 1,860 sq ft at 1 air change per hour. Smoke CADR on that page is 249.

Where does the air purifier recall line come from?

The US line is the CPSC SaferProducts match for that model. The Canadian line is the Health Canada open dataset match. The date on the card is the day the match was recorded.
