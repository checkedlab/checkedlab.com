AP-1512HH is the model with this CADR trio

The Airmega Mighty page states CADR of 233 smoke, 246 dust, and 240 pollen, room coverage of 361 ft² at 4.8 air changes per hour, dimensions of 16.8 × 18.3 × 9.6 in, a weight of 12.3 lb, and 77 W. A listing that says Coway, or Airmega, or Mighty, still has to show AP-1512HH if these are the lines you are matching. The Airmega 250 is a different card, with its own CADR trio and a different cabinet.

The Core 300S-P manual states 7.48 lb, 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in, and 39 W, with noise of 22–54.5 dB, and no CADR label. The Core 600S-P page states 391 CFM, 13.7 lb, and a room line of 606 sq ft at 4.8 air changes or 2,909 sq ft at 1. The 211i Max page states a recommended room of 674 sq ft, 16.2 lb, and 22.7 x 14.4 x 14.4 in.

Pick the CADR trio or a different room sentence

Pick the AP-1512HH if the listing shows that model and you are matching smoke 233, dust 246, pollen 240, and 361 ft² at 4.8 air changes per hour, at 12.3 lb and 77 W. The cabinet line is 16.8 × 18.3 × 9.6 in.

Pick the Core 300S-P if you are matching the smaller cabinet, 7.48 lb and 39 W, and you do not need a CADR figure from the same page. Pick the Core 600S-P if you are matching 391 CFM and the 606 sq ft line at 4.8 air changes. Pick the 211i Max if you are matching the 674 sq ft recommended line and 16.2 lb. Those room sentences are not the same kind of sentence as the AP-1512HH coverage line.

The recall block is the catalogue match for each purifier. Confirm AP-1512HH on the rating label if that is the Mighty you are buying. The 300S-P noise line is 22–54.5 dB, and the 600S-P noise line is 26–55 dB (as low as 26 dB per the maker).
