What the notices say

These are the air fryer notices on file from CPSC and Health Canada. They cover Cosori, Gourmia, Insignia, PowerXL, Secura, and Ninja Foodi OP300, with the hazard, what owners should do, and the model numbers. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-13 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2023-02-23

Two Million COSORI Air Fryers Recalled by Atekcity Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 23-130, says this recall involves Cosori air fryers in the model numbers listed below. It says the brand name Cosori is on the front, and the model number is printed on the bottom label and in the user manual. It says the units are 3.7 and 5.8 quart, sold in black, gray, white, blue, or red. It says about 2 million were recalled, and about 250,000 were sold in Canada and about 21,000 in Mexico. The Health Canada notice below prints different unit figures. It says the firm has received 205 reports of the air fryers catching fire, burning, melting, overheating, and smoking, including 10 reports of minor superficial burn injuries and 23 reports of minor property damage.

How to check your model

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled air fryers and contact Cosori for a free replacement air fryer or another Cosori product. It says registration asks for contact information and photos of the unit with the cord cut, and that no receipt is needed.

Health Canada · 2023-02-23

Certain models of Cosori-branded Air Fryers recalled due to potential fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice, updated 2023-02-23, identification RA-72618, says the recall involves certain Cosori-branded air fryers. It says the model number is on the bottom label and in the user manual, and the batch number is on the bottom or at the back below the rear exhaust vent. It says the company reported 252,828 units sold in Canada, 2,042,631 in the United States, and 21,490 in Mexico, sold from March 2019 to December 2022. Those unit figures are not the same figures as the CPSC notice’s “about 2 million” and “about 250,000” in Canada. It says that as of February 2023 the company had received 205 reports globally, including 56 in Canada, of the air fryers catching fire, burning, melting, overheating, and smoking, with 10 reports of minor superficial burn injuries including 4 in Canada, and 23 reports of minor property damage globally. It says the notice is a joint recall with the CPSC.

CPSC · 2026-11-05

Gourmia Recalls 9-Slice Digital Air Fryer Ovens Due to Burn Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 27-069, dated November 05, 2026, says this recall involves Gourmia stainless steel digital 9-slice air fryer ovens, model GTF7530, UPC 810002862779. It says the ovens have two spring-loaded glass front French doors with metal handles. It says model GTF7530 and codes 3021 to 0126 can be found on a label on the back of the unit. It says about 175,951 units. It says Gourmia has received 57 reports of the doors closing unexpectedly when retrieving food, resulting in 40 incidents of burns to hands or wrists.

Model number in the CPSC notice

  • GTF7530

How to check your model

The notice says the model and UPC are on the packaging, and model GTF7530 and codes 3021 to 0126 are on a label on the back of the unit.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the ovens immediately and contact Gourmia for a free repair kit by mail. It says the kit includes an added doorstop and installation instructions.

CPSC · 2024-03-14

Best Buy Recalls Insignia Air Fryers and Air Fryer Ovens Due to Fire, Burn and Laceration Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 24-159, says this recall involves Insignia air fryers and air fryer ovens in the model numbers listed below. It says INSIGNIA is on the top or front, and a rating label on the underside identifies the brand and the model number. It says capacities range from about 3.4 to 10 quarts. It says about 187,400 units, and about 99,900 were sold in Canada. It says the firm has received 24 reports of overheating, melting, or glass shattering, including six reports of air fryers catching fire, and that no injuries or property damage have been reported.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • NS-AF34D2
  • NS-AF5DSS2
  • NS-AF5MSS2
  • NS-AF8DBD2
  • NS-AF10DBK2
  • NS-AF10DSS2

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the product rating label on the underside of the unit.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the units and follow the instructions at the Best Buy recall registration page, including photos of the unit, the model number, the purchase receipt, and the destruction of the unit. It says the refund is a refund check or a Best Buy store credit, in the amount on the receipt, or the average sales price for the model if no receipt is provided. It says not to return the units to Best Buy stores.

CPSC · 2023-12-14

Empower Brands Recalls Power XL Dual Basket Air Fryers Due to Burn Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 24-051, says this recall involves PowerXL Vortex dual-basket air fryers, models DUAF-10 and DUAF-005. It says the 10-quart DUAF-10 is about 17 inches long by 16 inches wide by 12 1/2 inches tall and weighs about 14 1/2 pounds, and the 9-quart DUAF-005 is about 14 inches long by 13 inches wide by 15 inches tall and weighs about 14 pounds. It says they were sold in black or cinnamon, with a silver PowerXL label on the front and a white label on the bottom or on the power cord that lists the model number. It says about 319,000 units. It says the firm has received 41 reports of the product breaking during use, including three reports of burns.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • DUAF-10
  • DUAF-005

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on a white label on the bottom of the unit or on the power cord.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled dual-basket air fryers immediately and contact Empower Brands for a full refund.

CPSC · 2023-09-28

Secura Recalls Air Fryers Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 23-794, says this recall involves Secura air fryers, model SAF-53D (TXG-DT16E) with date code 1901, and model SAF-53 (TXG-DS16) with date codes 1903 and 1904. It says they are black with a silver accent, Secura is on the top, and the model number and four-digit date code are on silver labels at the bottom. It says they measure about 12 inches high, 11 inches deep, and 12 inches wide. It says about 6,400 units, and about 680 were sold in Canada. It says the firm has received nine reports of the air fryers catching fire, burning, and smoking, and that no injuries or property damage have been reported. The Health Canada notice below is narrower: it names only SAF-53 (TXG-DS16) with date codes 1903 and 1904.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • SAF-53D (TXG-DT16E), date code 1901
  • SAF-53 (TXG-DS16), date codes 1903 and 1904

How to check your model

The notice says the model number and the four-digit date code are on the silver labels at the bottom of the unit.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled air fryers and contact Secura. It says registration asks for contact information, photos of the unit with the power cord cut, and the date code. It says no receipt is needed to receive a replacement. It also names an Amazon gift card. The amount is printed on the CPSC notice, which is linked here.

Health Canada · 2026-05-22

Secura Air Fryers recalled due to fire and burn hazards

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice, updated 2026-05-22, identification RA-82098, says this recall involves Secura air fryers with model number SAF-53 (TXG-DS16) only, with date codes 1903 and 1904. It does not name SAF-53D or date code 1901. It says they are black with a silver accent, Secura is on the top, and the model number and date code are on silver labels at the bottom. It says they measure about 30 cm high, 28 cm deep, and 30 cm wide. It says 680 units were sold in Canada from May 2019 to October 2020. It says that as of May 14, 2026, the company had received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

Model number in the Health Canada notice

  • SAF-53 (TXG-DS16), date codes 1903 and 1904

How to check your model

The notice says the model number and the four-digit date code are printed on the silver labels at the bottom of the unit.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled product and contact Secura for an Amazon.ca gift card code. It says valid proof of purchase is required, and it points owners to the company’s recall instructions.

CPSC · 2025-05-01

SharkNinja Recalls Foodi Multi-Function Pressure Cookers Due to Burn Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 25-247, says this recall involves all Ninja Foodi OP300 Series multi-function pressure cookers. It says the cookers have functions that include pressure cooking and air frying, were sold in black, and have a 6.5-quart capacity. It says Ninja is printed on the front and on the product label. It says an extra code after the model number is not part of the model designation, and gives the example that a unit labeled OP301 I07 is a model OP301. It says replacement pressure-cooker lids bought as a separate part are included. It says about 1,846,400 units, and about 184,240 were sold in Canada. It says SharkNinja has received 106 reports of burn injuries, including more than 50 reports of second- or third-degree burns, with 26 lawsuits filed. This is a pressure-cooking lid notice. It is not a notice for the NC301 ice cream maker or for the Crispi FN101.

  • OP302HCN

  • OP350CO

How to check your model

The notice says the model numbers are printed on a label on the side of the cooker.

What the notice tells owners to do
