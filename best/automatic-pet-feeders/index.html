What to look for

  1. Read the food capacity unit. PLAF105 states 5 L. Simply Feed states 24 cups. The Feeder-Robot page states portions of 1/8 cup to 1 cup and does not print a hopper volume in this spec list.
  2. Read meals per day when the card has that line. The Feeder-Robot page states up to 8 scheduled meals a day. The Simply Feed page states up to 12 meals a day. The PLAF105 card does not print a meals line.
  3. Read the portion range in cups. Feeder-Robot portions are 1/8 cup to 1 cup. Simply Feed portions are 1/8 to 4 cups. Those ranges are different.
  4. Read the power line when the card has one. The Feeder-Robot page states a backup battery. The Simply Feed page states battery. The PLAF105 spec list on this site does not print a power line. Kibble size on the PLAF105 page is 2–15 mm.
