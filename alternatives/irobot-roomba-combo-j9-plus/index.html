What the Combo j9+ card states

The Combo j9+ page states a robot weight of 8.9 lb, robot dimensions of 3.4 x 13.7 x 13.7 in, a battery line of Lithium-ion, dock dimensions of 15.8 in W x 16.4 in D x 16.3 in H, and a dock weight of 21.9 lb. The model string on that page is C975020. An alternative here is another robot vacuum already on the cleaning list, with its own maker lines beside those five.

The Qrevo S page states suction of 7,000 Pa, robot dimensions of 13.78 x 13.9 x 3.80 in, dock dimensions of 13.38 x 19.17 x 20.51 in, mop speed of 200 RPM, a battery of 14.4 V/5200 mAh lithium-ion, and a charge time of Approx. 4 hours. The X10 Pro Omni page states suction of 8,000 Pa, a run time of 173 min vacuum, or 136 min vacuum and mop, on standard, robot dimensions of 327 mm x 353 mm x 113.5 mm, a robot weight of 4,560 g, and a dustbin of 330 ml. The AV2501S page states a weight of 14.77 lbs, a cleaning path of 5.7 in, dimensions of 15.35 in L x 12.01 in W x 17.72 in H, a dust cup of 0.1 qt, voltage of 120 volts, and wattage of 650 watts.

Pick by the line you can place

Pick the Combo j9+ if the listing is C975020 and you are matching a robot of 8.9 lb at 3.4 x 13.7 x 13.7 in, plus a dock of 15.8 in W x 16.4 in D x 16.3 in H that weighs 21.9 lb. The battery line says lithium-ion. It does not state a milliamp hour or a run time. A listing that says Roomba, or robot vacuum, still has to show C975020 if this is the card you mean.

Pick the Qrevo S if the suction line you are matching is 7,000 Pa, the mop line is 200 RPM, and the charge line is Approx. 4 hours. The dock on that page is 13.38 x 19.17 x 20.51 in. The Combo j9+ dock height line is 16.3 in H. Those are different sentences. The Qrevo battery line names 14.4 V/5200 mAh lithium-ion. This page does not convert that into the Combo j9+ battery sentence.

Pick the X10 Pro Omni if the suction line is 8,000 Pa or the run-time sentence is 173 min vacuum, or 136 min vacuum and mop, on standard. The robot weight is 4,560 g and the dimensions are 327 mm x 353 mm x 113.5 mm. The dustbin line is 330 ml. Grams, millimetres, and millilitres are the units on that card. The Combo j9+ card uses pounds and inches. Leave each figure in the units printed with it.

Pick the AV2501S if you are matching 14.77 lbs, a cleaning path of 5.7 in, and a dust cup of 0.1 qt. The dimension line is 15.35 in L x 12.01 in W x 17.72 in H. The electrical lines are 120 volts and 650 watts. That card does not print a pascal figure. A watt line is not a pascal line, and this page does not treat them as the same claim.

Robot weight and dock weight stay apart

The Combo j9+ card prints the robot and the dock as separate weights, 8.9 lb and 21.9 lb. This page does not add them into one figure. The Qrevo S card prints robot dimensions and dock dimensions and does not print a robot weight. The X10 card prints a robot weight in grams and a dustbin in millilitres. The AV2501S card prints one weight, 14.77 lbs, on a dimension line that is taller than the Combo j9+ robot height of 3.4 in. Read the sentence that names the part you are placing.

Qrevo S, X10 Pro Omni, and AV2501S are different model strings from C975020. The recall block below is the catalogue match for each of the four cards. Open the model page for the feed link, and search the model on CPSC Recalls and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts before you buy. A brand name in a listing title is not the model string on the card.
