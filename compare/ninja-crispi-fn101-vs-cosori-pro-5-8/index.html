How to read this

Dimensions and weight as the Ninja and COSORI pages state them for the FN101 and the Pro II, with a dated recall line for each. Specs that only one page lists are left out.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-20 (UTC).

The shared labels are dimensions and weight. The Crispi page states 11.5 × 9.76 × 11.81 in and 14.55 lb. The Pro II page states 11.8 × 13.8 × 12.6 in and 12.3 lb.

Power, containers, and modes are on the Crispi card only. Capacity, rated power, and power supply are on the Pro II card only. Those rows are left out because the other card does not use the same label.

Related spec pages: NC301 and NC501 specs.
