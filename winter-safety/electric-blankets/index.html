Electric blankets and heated throws

What to read on a heated blanket label and controller, where to search CPSC and Health Canada, and official notices for overheating and a controller fault.

Space heatersElectric blanketsCO and smoke alarmsOutage power

What to read on the label

  • Read the controller for an auto-off timer, and read the label for a certification mark such as UL, ETL, or CSA.
  • Read the model number on the wash label and on the controller. The notices below tell owners to match that number, not the size or the color alone.

How to search a recall

For a purchase in the United States, search the brand and the model on CPSC Recalls. For a purchase in Canada, search the same words on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. Open the notice and match the model number on the unit. A family name on the box can point at a different model.

A heated throw and a queen blanket from the same brand can be different model numbers. Search the number on the wash label.

Patterns in official notices

Each pattern below is one official notice. The sentence is what that notice says.

  • CPSC: Berkshire Blanket and Home Company Recalls Heated Throws and Blankets Due to Fire and Thermal Burn Hazards. The notice says the electric throws and blankets may overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. It says the model numbers are on a white label on the back of the throws and blankets and on the back of the controller.
  • CPSC: Bedshe International Recalls Bedsure Electric Heating Blankets and Pads Due to Fire and Thermal Burn Hazards. The notice says the controller can malfunction, posing fire and thermal burn hazards. It lists model numbers BS-HB5060, BS-HB6284, BS-HB7284, BS-LV0A, BS-HP2422, BS-HP1224, and BS-HP2433, on a white label on the back and on the controller.
  • Health Canada: Sunbeam Queen Size Heated Blankets recalled due to potential fire hazard. The notice says model 32810027 can potentially overheat, posing possible burn or fire hazards. It says that model number is printed on the wash label.

Questions

Where do I search a heated blanket recall?

Search the brand and the model number from the wash label on CPSC Recalls for a US purchase and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts for a Canadian purchase.

What does the Bedsure notice say failed?

The CPSC recall alert says the controller for the electric heating blankets and pads can malfunction, posing fire and thermal burn hazards. The model number is on a white label on the back and on the controller.

Is the Sunbeam TSL8WS the queen blanket in the Health Canada notice?

No. The catalogue card is the LoftTec heated throw TSL8WS. The Health Canada notice names queen-size model 32810027, printed on the wash label.
