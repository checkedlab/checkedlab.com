What the V15 page actually states

The V15 Detect Absolute page states 6.83 lb, up to 60 min of run time, a 4.5 hr charge time, a 0.2 gallon bin, whole-machine HEPA filtration, and 230 AW in Boost mode. Those six lines are the whole spec card. An alternative in this catalogue is another cordless vacuum whose card prints a weight, a run time, or a charge time you can put next to those lines.

Three cards do that. The PURE ONE S15 PRO page states 6.8 lbs, 40 min, and 4 hours, plus a 2500 mAh battery and a 0.47 L dust capacity. The IP3251 page states 18.47 lbs, up to 70 minutes measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, and a 6 hour charge, plus a 0.7 qt dust cup, full-size dimensions, and 21.6 volts. The V8 page states 5.58 lb, 40 min maximum, a 5 hr charge, a 0.14 gallon bin, and 115 AW in MAX mode.

Pick by the line you are matching

Pick the V15 Detect Absolute if the run-time line you need is up to 60 min, the suction line is 230 AW in Boost mode, or the filtration line is whole-machine HEPA. The stated weight is 6.83 lb and the charge line is 4.5 hrs. Pick it when those are the figures on the box you are about to match, not because a page on this site ranked it.

Pick the PURE ONE S15 PRO if the weight you are matching is 6.8 lbs, the run time is 40 min, and the charge line is 4 hours, or you need the 2500 mAh battery line and the 0.47 L dust capacity. The weight sits next to the V15 line. The run time is shorter than the V15 line.

Pick the IP3251 if the run-time sentence you are matching is up to 70 minutes at the hand vacuum in ECO mode, and you can accept a stated weight of 18.47 lbs and a 6 hour charge. That run-time sentence names a configuration. It is not the same sentence as “up to 60 min” on the V15 page.

Pick the V8 if the weight you are matching is 5.58 lb, the run time is 40 min maximum, and the suction line is 115 AW in MAX mode. The bin on that page is 0.14 gallon, against 0.2 gallon on the V15 page. The charge line is 5 hrs, longer than the V15 charge line. Both are Dyson pages, and the numbers are not the same.

Notices that name a different model

The V15 and V8 cards link a Health Canada notice for a FirstPower replacement battery for Dyson V6 series vacuums. That notice names V6. It does not name the V15 Detect Absolute or the V8 card’s model line. Read the model on the notice before applying it.

The IP3251 card links the 2015 CPSC notice for Shark Rotator Lift-Away vacuums, models NV650W, NV651, NV652, and NV660, and the Health Canada notice that names NV650W and NV652. IP3251 is not those model numbers. The recall block on this page is the catalogue match for each of the four cards.
