Pressure cookers and multicookers

Lid-lock notices for Crock-Pot and Ninja Foodi multicookers, including the model and date-code lines that differ between the CPSC and Health Canada pages.

Turkey fryersPressure cookersTurkey temperaturesKitchen fires

What to read on the label

  • Read the model number on the bottom or side label before using the pressure function. On a Crock-Pot Express Crock, also read the date code engraved on a prong of the electrical plug and on the bottom of the base. The United States and Canada notices below do not use the same date-code range.
  • On a Ninja Foodi, read the model on the side or on the rating label near the power cord. The CPSC Foodi notice says an extra code after the model number is not part of the model, and gives the example that a unit labeled OP301 I07 is a model OP301. It also includes OP300-series replacement pressure-cooker lids bought separately.

How to search a recall

For a purchase in the United States, search the brand and the model on CPSC Recalls. For a purchase in Canada, search the same words on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. Open the notice and match the model number on the unit. A family name on the box can point at a different model.

Instant Pot notices on this site name Smart, Smart-60, and Gem 65 multicookers. Those are different models and different hazards from the lid notices below. The Instant Pot official notices page copies the model and serial lines. The Duo Plus card is not one of those names.

Patterns in official notices

Each pattern below is one official page. The sentence is what that page says.

  • CPSC: Crock-Pot 6-Quart Express Crock Multi-Cookers recalled. The notice, recall number 21-035, is dated November 24, 2020. It says the cooker can pressurize when the lid is not fully locked, so the lid can suddenly detach and eject hot food and liquids. It names model SCCPPC600-V1 on the bottom label, manufactured from July 1, 2017 through October 1, 2018, with date codes K196JN through K365JN and L001JN through L273JN. It says the date code is engraved on one prong of the plug and on the bottom of the base. It says about 914,430 units were sold in the United States and about 28,330 in Canada. It says Sunbeam had 119 reports of lid detachment and 99 burn injuries, from first-degree to third-degree burns. It tells owners to stop using pressure-cooker mode and contact Crock-Pot for a free replacement lid. It says slow cooking and sautéing can continue, and that anyone who keeps using pressure mode while waiting should align the arrow on the lid with the lock symbol.
  • Health Canada: Crock-Pot 6-Quart Express Crock Multi-Cookers recalled. The notice, identification number RA-74391, starts November 24, 2020. It says the cooker can pressurize when the lid is not properly locked and the lid can suddenly detach. It names models SCCPPC600-V1 and SCCPPC600-V1-033 on the bottom label, manufactured from March 20, 2018 through September 18, 2018, with date codes L079JN through L261JN. It says the date code is on a prong of the plug and on the bottom. It says 28,330 units were sold in Canada and 914,430 in the United States. It says that as of November 17 the company had 2 reports of the lid detaching and 2 burn injuries in Canada, and 119 reports and 99 burn injuries in the United States. It tells owners to contact Sunbeam for a free replacement lid. The date-code range and the second model number are the lines to use for a Canadian unit, not the longer range on the CPSC notice.
  • CPSC: SharkNinja recalls Foodi OP300-series pressure cookers. The notice, recall number 25-247, is dated May 1, 2025. It says the pressure-cooking lid can be opened during use, so hot contents can escape. It says the recall covers all Ninja Foodi OP300 Series Multi-Function Pressure Cookers, sold in black with a 6.5-quart capacity, and any OP300-series replacement pressure-cooker lid bought as a separate part. It lists model numbers OP300, OP301, OP301A, OP302, OP302BRN, OP302HCN, OP302HAQ, OP302HW, OP302HB, OP305, OP305CO, and OP350CO, printed on a label on the side. It says about 1,846,400 were sold in the United States and about 184,240 in Canada. It says SharkNinja had received 106 reports of burn injuries, including more than 50 reports of second- or third-degree burns to the face or body. It tells owners to stop using the pressure-cooking function and contact SharkNinja for a free replacement lid. It says the air-frying and other functions can still be used.
  • Health Canada: Ninja Foodi TenderCrisp pressure-cooking lid recalled. The notice says the pressure-cooking lid can be opened during use, so hot contents can escape. It says the cookers have a 6.5-quart chamber and that Ninja is on the front of the hinged lid. It includes replacement pressure-cooking lids bought separately. It says the model number is on the rating label on the back, near the power cord. It lists OP300C, OP301, OP301C, OP302, OP305, OP305CCO, and OP305CO. It says other functions, such as air crisping, are not part of this recall. It says that as of April 10, 2025 the company had 5 reports of lids opened during use in Canada and no reports of injury in Canada, and 106 reports of burn injuries in the United States. It says about 184,240 units were sold in Canada and 1,846,400 in the United States, plus replacement lids sold as service parts. It tells owners to stop using the pressure-cooking function, dispose of the lid so it cannot be used, and contact SharkNinja for a replacement lid. That model list is the one to use for a Canadian purchase. It is not the same list as the CPSC notice.

Questions

What failed in the Crock-Pot Express Crock notice?

Both the CPSC and Health Canada notices say the multi-cooker can pressurize when the lid is not fully locked, and that the lid can suddenly detach. The CPSC notice names model SCCPPC600-V1 and date codes K196JN through K365JN and L001JN through L273JN. The Health Canada notice names SCCPPC600-V1 and SCCPPC600-V1-033 and date codes L079JN through L261JN. Use the notice for the country where the cooker was bought.

What failed in the Ninja Foodi lid notice?

The May 1, 2025 CPSC notice says the OP300-series pressure-cooking lid can be opened during use. The Health Canada notice says the same about the TenderCrisp pressure-cooking lid and prints a different model list. Both say to stop using the pressure-cooking function. The CPSC notice says air frying can continue. Match the side or back label to the list for that country.

Is the Instant Pot Duo Plus card one of those lid recalls?

No. The Duo Plus card is a different model. The Instant Pot notices copied on this site name Smart, Smart-60, and Gem 65. Read the model on the cooker and compare it with those notices and with the Crock-Pot and Foodi lists above.
