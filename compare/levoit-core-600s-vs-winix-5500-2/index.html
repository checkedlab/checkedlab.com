How to read this

CADR, weight, size, and power as the Levoit and Winix pages state them for the Core 600S-P and the 5500-2, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-20 (UTC).

The Core 600S-P specifications list states 391 CFM, 13.7 lb, 12.3 x 12.3 x 23.6 in, and 49 W at AC 120 V, 60 Hz. The 5500-2 manual states 243 dust, 246 pollen, and 232 smoke, about 15 lb, 15 x 8.2 x 23.6 in, and 70 W at 120 V / 60 Hz.

A lower stated weight and a lower stated wattage are the Core 600S-P lines. The CADR row is one CFM figure on the Levoit page and three CADR figures on the Winix page. Room coverage uses a different label on each card, so it is left out. Noise and filtration are on the Levoit card only.
