Cosori air fryer recall: check your model + replacement options

Read the number on the bottom label against the February 23, 2023 notices, then follow the remedy on the notice that lists it.

Sources: CPSC & Health CanadaChecked: Nov 20, 2026

Is my Cosori recalled?

It is recalled when the model number on the bottom label is one of the numbers below. Both notices are dated February 23, 2023.

Stop using the air fryer and contact Cosori for a free replacement air fryer or another Cosori product. Registration asks for contact information and photos of the unit with the cord cut. No receipt is needed.

Health Canada notice

The model number is on the bottom label and in the user manual. Health Canada also says the batch number is on the bottom of the unit, or at the back below the rear exhaust vent.

Where to look and what to do
  • Sold sizes. The CPSC notice says the recalled units are 3.7 and 5.8 quart, sold in black, gray, white, blue, or red. 2023 CPSC notice 2023 Health Canada notice
  • A number that is not listed. CS358-AF, the Pro II series on the model card below, is not in either notice.

Replacement options

Cosori's remedy comes first. The CPSC notice says to stop using a listed fryer and register for a free replacement air fryer or another Cosori product. That registration asks for photos of the unit with the cord cut, and no receipt is needed. The Health Canada notice says to stop using it and request a replacement unit at the Cosori recall page or 1-888-216-5974. The Pro II card is the CS358-AF series, which is not in either model list, and its capacity line is 5.8 quarts. The comparison beside it is the Crispi, which cooks in glass containers.

What the notices say

CPSC recall number 23-130 and Health Canada identification RA-72618. The model numbers below are copied from those pages. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-20 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2023-02-23

Two Million COSORI Air Fryers Recalled by Atekcity Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Cosori air fryers with the model numbers listed below. It says the brand name Cosori is on the front of each unit, the model number is printed on the bottom label and in the user manual, and the units are 3.7 and 5.8 quart, sold in black, gray, white, blue, or red.

How to check your model

The notice says to read the model number on the bottom label of the unit and in the user manual.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled air fryers and contact Cosori for a free replacement air fryer or another Cosori product. It says registration asks for contact information and photos of the unit with the cord cut, and that no receipt is needed.

Health Canada · 2023-02-23

Certain models of Cosori-branded Air Fryers recalled due to potential fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice, updated 2023-02-23, says the recall involves certain Cosori-branded air fryers and describes a fire hazard. It says the notice is a joint recall with the CPSC. Its model column also uses the shorter names CO137, CO158, CP137, CP158, CP258, and CS158.
