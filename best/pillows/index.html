How we picked

Checked Lab lines up product pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The Best for line uses the fill or the height choice the maker page prints. Purple states a ventilated Talalay latex core. Tempur-Neck states three heights in one size line. Coop states a fill of cross-cut memory foam and microfiber and a feel of medium-firm.

Feel words are quoted from the maker page. Medium-firm on the Coop card is that page’s word. It is not a measurement added here.

Purple Harmony Pillow

The Purple Harmony page states a core of ventilated Talalay latex, a cover of 92% nylon, 8% spandex, and a standard low size of 26 × 17 × 5.5 in, 4.2 lb. King low on that page is 34 × 17 × 5.5 in, 6 lb. The ventilated Talalay latex core is the airflow use. The standard low size is 26 × 17 × 5.5 in, 4.2 lb.

Tempur-Pedic TEMPUR-Neck Pillow

The TEMPUR-Neck page states sizes of small 20 × 12.5 × 3.5 in, medium 20 × 12.5 × 4 in, large 20 × 12.5 × 4.75 in, a fill of TEMPUR-Material, and cover care of removable and washable. Small, medium, and large state three heights, which is the neck-height choice.

Coop Original Pillow

The Coop Original page states a fill of cross-cut memory foam and microfiber, a feel of medium-firm, and a cover fabric of Lulltra, polyester and bamboo-derived viscose. The listed shell is polyester. Cross-cut memory foam and microfiber, with a medium-firm feel, is the fill use.

What to look for

  1. Read the fill noun. Purple states a core of ventilated Talalay latex and a cover of 92% nylon, 8% spandex. Tempur-Neck states a fill of TEMPUR-Material and a cover of 100% polyester knit, hypoallergenic. Coop states a fill of cross-cut memory foam and microfiber.
  2. Read height. Purple’s standard low size is 26 × 17 × 5.5 in at 4.2 lb. Tempur-Neck states small 20 × 12.5 × 3.5 in, medium 20 × 12.5 × 4 in, and large 20 × 12.5 × 4.75 in. Coop’s spec list on this site does not print a height.
  3. Read the cover care line when it is present. The Tempur-Neck page states the cover is removable and washable. The Purple and Coop cards print cover fabric and do not print that care line.
  4. Read listed shell or fabric on the Coop card before assuming a fiber. The Coop page states a cover fabric of Lulltra, polyester and bamboo-derived viscose, and a listed shell of polyester.
