KSM150PSER is the model line

The Artisan page states a 5 quart capacity, 10 speeds, a 5 quart stainless steel dishwasher-safe bowl, more than 10 optional hub-powered attachments sold separately, and model KSM150PSER. A listing that says KitchenAid, or Artisan, or 5 quart, still has to show KSM150PSER if this is the mixer you mean. Bowl size and a tilt-head description on a different model number are a different card, and this catalogue does not have a second stand mixer.

The Cuisinart DFP-14BCNY card is a food processor, not a mixer. It states a 14 cup bowl, a 720 W motor, dimensions of 11 in L x 7.9 in W x 14.8 in H, a 5-year motor warranty, and a 3-year limited warranty on the whole unit. It shares a kitchen counter with the Artisan. It does not share a job.

Pick the mixer line, and stop on the processor recall

Pick the Artisan if the listing is KSM150PSER and you are matching a 5 quart bowl, 10 speeds, and the attachment sentence that says more than 10 optional hub-powered attachments are sold separately. The match is those lines. There is no second mixer card here to pick instead.

Do not pick the DFP-14BCNY as the mixer. Pick that card only if you are matching a 14 cup food processor and a 720 W motor line. The US catalogue line for that processor is a recall match. The notice on the model page is the Conair Cuisinart food-processor recall. Read the model numbers there before buying the processor.

The recall block below repeats the catalogue status for the Artisan and for the processor. Confirm KSM150PSER on the mixer base if the Artisan is the one in the cart. The bowl line says 5 quart stainless steel and dishwasher-safe. The attachment line says the hub-powered tools are sold separately, so a listing that bundles a different model number is not this card.
