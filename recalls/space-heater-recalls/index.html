What the notices say

These are the space heater notices on file from CPSC and Health Canada. They cover Vornado, Lasko, DREO, Govee, and One Stop Gardens, with the hazard, what owners should do, and the model numbers. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-14 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2026-06-04

Vornado Air Recalls SRTH Small Room Tower Heaters Due to Fire Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 26-532, says the recalled Vornado SRTH small room tower heaters were sold in black and white and measure about 12.5 inches high by 6 inches in diameter. It says they have two heat settings and a fan-only setting. It says TYPE SRTH is printed on the silver rating label on the bottom. It says about 255,000 units, and about eight were sold in Canada. It says Vornado has received 32 reports of overheating from fan displacement, including eight reports of fire and one report of smoke inhalation.

Model line in the CPSC notice

  • TYPE SRTH

How to check your model

The notice says TYPE SRTH is on the silver rating label on the bottom of the heater.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters immediately and contact Vornado for instructions on submitting photos of the product and proof of destruction to receive a full refund.

CPSC · 2025-01-16

Vornado Air Recalls VH2 Whole Room Heaters Due to Electric Shock and Fire Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 25-093, says the recall involves Vornado VH2 whole room heaters with a JUL24 or AUG24 date code and TYPE VH2 on the silver rating label on the underside. It says only those date codes are included. It says the heaters are off white, with thermostatic control, automatic or continuous fan operation, and two heat settings. It says about 7,780 units. It says no incidents were reported.

Model line in the CPSC notice

  • TYPE VH2 with date code JUL24 or AUG24

How to check your model

The notice says TYPE VH2 and the JUL24 or AUG24 date code are on the silver rating label on the underside.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled heaters and contact Vornado to confirm the unit is included and to receive instructions for a free replacement heater.

CPSC · 2014-08-14

Vornado Air Recalls Electric Space Heaters

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 14-256, says this recall involves Vornado VH110 whole room vortex heaters in black and white. It says recalled heaters have the numbers 1 and 3 as the fourth and fifth digits of the serial number, on a silver decal on the bottom. It says the heaters measure about 10.5 inches high, 9 inches deep, and 10.5 inches at the base. It says about 79,000 units. It says Vornado has received 29 reports of units overheating and melting, including seven reports of heaters catching fire, one report of smoke inhalation, and one report of property damage from soot and smoke.

Model line in the CPSC notice

  • VH110 with serial digits 1 and 3 in the fourth and fifth places

How to check your model

The notice says the model and serial numbers are on a silver decal on the bottom, and that recalled units have 1 and 3 as the fourth and fifth serial digits.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to turn the heater off, unplug it, and contact Vornado for a free replacement unit, including free shipping.

CPSC · 2011-02-08

Lasko Recalls Portable Electric Heaters Due To Fire Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, release 11-121, says the recall involves Lasko model 5540 and Air King model 8540 portable electric tower heaters manufactured in 2002, with date codes that begin with 2. It says heaters with date codes beginning with 3, 4, or 5 are not in this recall. It says the heaters are 20.5 inches tall, dark grey with silver front covers and black vent slats. It says about 107,500 units. It says Lasko received 36 reports of the connection overheating, with no reports of injury, and 18 reports of minor burn damage to floors or carpets. A note on the page says the firm’s recall hotline number has changed.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • Lasko 5540
  • Air King 8540

How to check your model

The notice says the date code is a four-digit number on the label on the bottom of the unit, on the bottom left of the label, above the voltage number.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters immediately and contact Lasko for a free replacement heater.

CPSC · 2007-06-28

Ceramic Heaters Recalled By Lasko

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 07-227, says the recall involves Lasko ceramic heaters manufactured in 2005, in the model numbers listed below. It says about 1.2 million units. It says Lasko has received 28 reports of failed power cords, including six reports of minor property damage, and that no injuries have been reported. A note on the page says the firm contact number and website changed.

How to check your model

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters immediately and contact Lasko for a free replacement heater.

CPSC · 2004-03-04

CPSC, Lasko Products Inc. Announce Recall of Space Heaters

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 04-094, says two heaters are in this recall. It says model 5500 is an oscillating ceramic heater with dark grey vents and a bronze trim, and model 5700 is an oil-filled radiator. It says Lasko is printed near the top of both. It says 186,000 units. It says four consumers reported burned and detached power cords, and that no injuries were reported.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • 5500
  • 5700

How to check your model

The notice says the 5500 model number is on a label under the round base, and the 5700 model number is on a label on the side near the right front wheel.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters, unplug them, and contact Lasko for instructions on returning the heater for a free replacement or a product of similar value.

Health Canada · 2026-10-29

DREO Space Heaters recalled due to fire and burn hazards

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice, updated 2026-10-29, identification RA-82854, says the recall involves DREO portable oscillating ceramic space heaters, model DR-HSH015. It says affected units were manufactured from April 2025 (2025/04) to January 2026 (2026/01). It says the heaters are black, about 29 cm wide by 33 cm tall, and include a remote control. It says 36,288 units were sold in Canada and 338,800 in the United States, from September 2025 to August 2026. It says that as of October 22, 2026, the company had received one reported incident and no reports of injury in Canada, and 16 reports of incidents with one report of injury in the United States. It says the notice is a joint recall with the CPSC. The Atom 316 card is a different model string.

Model number in the Health Canada notice

  • DR-HSH015

How to check your model

The notice says the model number and the manufacture date are on labels on the underside.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled product immediately and contact DREO for a free replacement space heater.

Health Canada · 2024-11-07

GoveeLife and Govee Smart Electric Space Heaters recalled due to fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice, updated 2024-11-07, identification RA-76452, says the recall involves GoveeLife and Govee smart electric space heaters in the model numbers listed below, including the H7130101 variation of H7130. It says the brand is on the front or side, the heaters were sold in black and white, and height runs from 9 inches to 26 inches depending on the model. It says approximately 48,584 units were sold in Canada. It also prints a United States count on the same page. It says that as of October 28, 2024, the company had received 7 reports of overheating in Canada and no report of injuries, and 106 reports of overheating and one report of a minor burn injury in the United States. It says the notice is a joint recall with the CPSC.

Model numbers in the Health Canada notice

  • H7130
  • H7130101
  • H7131
  • H7132
  • H7133
  • H7134
  • H7135

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the manufacturer’s label on the underside of the unit.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled heaters and register at the GoveeLife heater recall page for a refund.

CPSC · 2021-07-21

Harbor Freight Tools Recalls One Stop Gardens Tank Top Propane Heaters Due to Burn Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice, recall number 21-168, says this recall involves the One Stop Gardens 15,000 BTU tank-top propane heater, item 63073, and the 30,000 BTU heater, item 63072. It says the heaters mount on a standard 20 lb propane tank. It says about 350,000 units. It says the firm has received nine reports of flame redirection or a melted tip switch, and that three incidents resulted in minor burn injuries when consumers tried to turn the unit off. These item numbers are not the catalogue heater model numbers.

Item numbers in the CPSC notice

  • 63073
  • 63072

How to check your model

The notice says item 63073 is on the hang tag of the 15,000 BTU heater and item 63072 is on the label of the 30,000 BTU heater.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters and return them to a Harbor Freight Tools store for a refund of the purchase price, plus applicable tax, as a Harbor Freight Tools gift card. It says Harbor Freight is contacting known purchasers.
