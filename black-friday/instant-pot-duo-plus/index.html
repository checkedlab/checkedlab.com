Duo Plus is a specific function list

The Duo Plus page states pressure cook, slow cook, sauté, sous vide, steam, sterilize, keep warm, rice, and yogurt, a 6 quart volume, 1,000 W at 120 V and 60 Hz, dishwasher-safe cleanup, and dimensions of 12.2 L x 13.4 W x 12.9 H in. A listing that says Instant Pot, or 6 quart, or Duo, still has to match this function list and this wattage if this is the card you mean.

The Foodi 10-in-1 page states Air Fry, Whole Roast, Air Roast, Bake, Dehydrate, Pizza, Broil, Toast, Bagel, and Reheat, a capacity line for a 5 lb chicken and a sheet pan of vegetables, two 12 in pizzas, or a 12 lb turkey, 1,800 W, and dimensions of 62.2 × 50.6 × 42.49 cm. Those are different functions and a higher stated wattage. The model line on that card is DT201C.

Pick the function list, then read the other model numbers

Pick the Duo Plus if the listing is the 6 quart Duo Plus and you are matching pressure cook among the functions, 1,000 W, and the inch dimensions above. Pick the Foodi 10-in-1 if the listing is the DT201C and you are matching the air-fry and roast function list, 1,800 W, and the centimetre dimensions.

Official Instant Pot notices on the recall desk name Smart, Smart-60, and Gem 65. They do not name Duo Plus. The Foodi OP300-series notice names pressure-cooker model numbers in the OP300 series, not DT201C and not the FN101 Crispi. Open those explainers and read the model list before treating a family name as this card.

The recall block below is the catalogue match for the Duo Plus and for the Foodi 10-in-1. A family name on a seasonal listing is not the model number. The wattage row is 1,000 W on the Duo Plus page and 1,800 W on the Foodi page, and the dimension row uses inches on one page and centimetres on the other.
