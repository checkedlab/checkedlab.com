How to read this

Robot weight and robot dimensions as the iRobot and eufy pages state them for the Combo j9+ and the X10 Pro Omni, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-20 (UTC).

The Combo j9+ page states a robot weight of 8.9 lb and robot dimensions of 3.4 x 13.7 x 13.7 in. The X10 Pro Omni service page states a robot weight of 4,560 g and robot dimensions of 327 mm x 353 mm x 113.5 mm.

The weight and dimension rows use the units each page prints, pounds and inches on the iRobot page and grams and millimetres on the eufy page. Battery and dock size are on the Roomba card only. Suction, run time, and dustbin volume are on the eufy card only.

The shared rows do not share a unit

Robot weight and robot dimensions are the labels both pages use. iRobot prints 8.9 lb and 3.4 x 13.7 x 13.7 in. eufy prints 4,560 g and 327 mm x 353 mm x 113.5 mm. This table does not convert those units. A reader who wants one unit has to do that arithmetic outside the maker’s sentence.

The Roomba page also states a lithium-ion battery, dock dimensions of 15.8 in W x 16.4 in D x 16.3 in H, and a dock weight of 21.9 lb. The eufy page also states 8,000 Pa suction, a run time of 173 min vacuum or 136 min vacuum and mop on standard, and a 330 ml dustbin. None of those labels are on both cards.

Pick by dock lines or by the suction line

Pick the Combo j9+ page if the figures you are matching are the inch dimensions of the robot, the 8.9 lb robot weight, or the dock at 15.8 in by 16.4 in by 16.3 in and 21.9 lb. The battery line on that page names the chemistry, lithium-ion, and does not print a milliamp-hour figure.

Pick the X10 Pro Omni page if the figure you are matching is 8,000 Pa, the run-time line of 173 minutes vacuum or 136 minutes vacuum and mop on standard, or the 330 ml dustbin. The robot weight on that page is 4,560 g, and the dimensions are in millimetres. A dock weight is not on the eufy card used here.

The Canadian catalogue note on the Roomba card links a Clean Base notice for the Roomba i7+, which is a different model name from Combo j9+. Read that notice’s model line before applying it to C975020. The recall block below is the catalogue match for each robot on the check date.
