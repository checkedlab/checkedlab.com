Instant Pot official notices: Smart, Smart-60, and Gem 65

Official CPSC and Health Canada notices name Instant Pot Smart, Smart-60, and Gem 65 multicookers. They do not name the Duo Plus. The Duo Plus card has no matching recall record as of the catalogue check date. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2015-07-15

Instant Pot Pressure Cookers Recalled by Double Insight Due to Shock Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves Instant Pot Smart and Smart-60 electric pressure cookers. It says they are stainless steel with black plastic attachments, about 12 inches in diameter and 12 inches tall, with Instant Pot and Smart printed on the front of the control panel. It says serial numbers between 1410 and 1503 and manufacture dates between December 1, 2014 and June 1, 2015 are included.

Models in this CPSC notice

  • Instant Pot Smart
  • Instant Pot Smart-60
  • Serial numbers 1410 through 1503
  • Manufacture dates December 1, 2014 through June 1, 2015

How to check your model

The notice says the serial number and date are printed on a label on the bottom of the base.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled cooker and contact Instant Pot for a free replacement base, which is the bottom of the cooker including the control panel.

Health Canada · 2015-07-15

Double Insight Inc. recalls Instant Pot Smart Pressure Cooker

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says date codes between October 2014 (1410) and March 2015 (1503) are affected. It says those date codes are the first four digits of a longer serial number on the bottom of the base, and that the model name Smart-60 is on a label on the back of the unit.

Names in this Health Canada notice

  • Instant Pot Smart
  • Smart-60
  • Date codes 1410 through 1503

How to check your model

The notice says to read the first four digits of the serial number on the bottom, and the Smart-60 name on the back label.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the product and contact Double Insight for a replacement or refund.

CPSC · 2018-03-01

Double Insight Recalls Multicookers Due to Fire Hazard; Sold Exclusively at Walmart

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves Gem 65 8-in-1 multicookers sold exclusively at Walmart. It says Instant Pot is printed on the front, and Gem 65 8-in-1 plus a batch code of 1728, 1730, 1731, 1734, or 1746 is printed on the rating label on the underside.

Names in this CPSC notice

  • Gem 65 8-in-1
  • Batch code 1728
  • Batch code 1730
  • Batch code 1731
  • Batch code 1734
  • Batch code 1746

How to check your model

The notice says to read the underside rating label for Gem 65 8-in-1 and one of those batch codes.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled multicooker, unplug it, and return it to Walmart for a free replacement.
