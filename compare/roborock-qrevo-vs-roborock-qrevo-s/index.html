Roborock Qrevo vs Qrevo S: which to buy

The Qrevo lists 5,500 Pa, a 7 mm mop lift, and mopping up to 400 sqm on one fill. The Qrevo S lists 7,000 Pa, a 10 mm mop lift, and mopping up to 330 sqm on one fill. Choose the Qrevo S when the higher suction figure and the higher mop lift matter. Choose the Qrevo when you want the longer mopping-range line and can fit the taller dock.

Choose the Qrevo if

  • You want 5,500 Pa suction.
  • You want a 7 mm mop lift and up to 400 sqm of mopping on one fill.
  • You can fit a dock that is 561 mm tall.

Choose the Qrevo S if

How to read this

A row is shown when both makers print that label. Source opens the sentence the figure came from. Details only one of them prints are listed under that name below.

Check the model number on CPSC (US) and Health Canada (CA) recalls before buying.

Roborock’s Qrevo page prints 5,500 Pa, a robot of 350*353*96.5 mm, a dock of 340*487*561 mm, mops up to 200 RPM, a lift up to 7 mm, and mopping up to 400 sqm (4305 sqft). The Qrevo S page prints 7,000 Pa, a robot of 350 * 353 * 96.5 mm, a dock of 340 * 487 * 521 mm, mops up to 200 RPM, a lift of 10 mm, and mopping up to 330 sqm (3552 sqft).

The robot millimeter lines match. The dock height does not: 561 mm against 521 mm. Suction and mop lift are higher on the Qrevo S line. The one-fill mopping line is longer on the Qrevo.

Suction is 5,500 Pa against 7,000 Pa

The suction lines are the clearest split, and they come with the same kind of footnote. Roborock’s Qrevo page says 5,500 Pa. That footnote says the figure is from internal testing under Max+ mode with a full battery, using IEC 62885-2:2021/5.11. The Qrevo S page says 7,000 Pa, and its footnote 7 names the same Max+ mode, a full battery, and the same IEC standard. Read those notes before treating the gap as the result you will get on a particular floor.

The Qrevo S page also says that at maximum suction the main brush speed increases by 10%. The Qrevo page does not print that brush-speed line. Both pages name an all-rubber brush. The Qrevo calls it an all-rubber brush that adapts to uneven ground. The Qrevo S calls it an all-rubber anti-tangle brush with a floating design.

Mops, lift height, and one fill of water

Both pages put dual spinning mops at a maximum of 200 RPM. On each page the footnote ties that speed to extreme water flow in a custom mode, from Roborock’s internal testing. The lift is not the same. The Qrevo page says the mops can be lifted up to 7 mm. The Qrevo S page says the mopping module lifts 10 mm, which it also writes as 0.4 inches, so the mop can stay off short-pile carpet. A higher stated lift is the Qrevo S line.

How far one tank fill mops runs the other way. The Qrevo page says up to 400 sqm, which it also writes as 4305 sqft, with a full clean-water tank in the dock and default mode on hard floors. The Qrevo S page says up to 330 sqm, also written as 3552 sqft, with a full clean-water tank and default mopping mode. A longer stated mopping range is the Qrevo line. The Qrevo S page also says water flow can be set in 30 levels in the app. The Qrevo page does not print a 30-level line.

Drying is described in different units, so this table does not force one sentence onto the other. The Qrevo page says the dock dries the mops with 45°C warm air. The Qrevo S page says drying noise is as low as 45 dB(A), and that the mop rotates once every 15 minutes while it dries. One number is a temperature. The other is a noise level. They are not the same measurement.

The robot fits the same footprint. The dock does not.

Robot size matches. The Qrevo page prints 350*353*96.5 mm. The Qrevo S page prints 350 * 353 * 96.5 mm, and also 13.78 * 13.9 * 3.80 inches. The dock does not match. The Qrevo dock is 340*487*561 mm. The Qrevo S dock is 340 * 487 * 521 mm, also written as 13.38 * 19.17 * 20.51 inches. Width and depth use the same millimeter figures. Height is 561 mm on the Qrevo and 521 mm on the Qrevo S. If the shelf above the dock is tight, that 40 mm is the line to measure.

Both docks are described as washing the mops, drying them, emptying dust, and refilling the tank, with a base you can remove. The Qrevo page says emptying can cover up to 7 weeks. The Qrevo S page says over 7 weeks. Both footnotes say the real interval depends on how you use the robot. The Qrevo S footnote adds that intelligent auto-empty needs the app set to High. Both pages say charging is 30% faster than the Roborock S7. That sentence compares each robot with the S7, not with the other robot on this page.

The Qrevo S marketing page on Roborock’s Canada site was still up on 19 November 2026. The US store record for the Qrevo S, and the Canada store record, were not available to purchase that day. The original Qrevo marketing address on the US site no longer returns the product page. The Qrevo figures here are from Roborock’s page as saved on 18 December 2025. Pick the Qrevo if you want 5,500 Pa, a 7 mm lift, and up to 400 sqm on one fill, and the 561 mm dock fits. Pick the Qrevo S if you want 7,000 Pa, a 10 mm lift, and the 521 mm dock, with up to 330 sqm on one fill. Match the name on the box to Qrevo or to Qrevo S. Qrevo Pro, Qrevo Master, Qrevo Slim, and Qrevo S Pro are other models.

The eufy X10 Pro Omni comparison and the Roomba Combo j9+ comparison use this Qrevo S, not the original Qrevo. Before Black Friday or Cyber Monday, read the return window on the store page and confirm what the box includes. A different Monday does not change the model name.

Questions

Is the Qrevo S more powerful than the Qrevo?

Roborock lists 7,000 Pa for the Qrevo S and 5,500 Pa for the Qrevo. Both footnotes say the figure is internal testing in Max+ mode with a full battery, under IEC 62885-2:2021/5.11.

Are the robots the same size?

Yes, on the millimeter lines. Both pages print 350 by 353 by 96.5 mm. The Qrevo S page also prints 13.78 by 13.9 by 3.80 inches.

Which dock is taller?

The Qrevo dock is 340 by 487 by 561 mm. The Qrevo S dock is 340 by 487 by 521 mm. The height line is the one that differs.

How far does each one mop on one fill?

The Qrevo page says up to 400 sqm (4305 sqft) with a full clean-water tank and default mode on hard floors. The Qrevo S page says up to 330 sqm (3552 sqft) with a full clean-water tank and default mopping mode.

Do both spin the mops at 200 RPM?

Yes. Both pages say up to 200 RPM, and both footnotes tie that speed to extreme water flow in a custom mode. The lift is 7 mm on the Qrevo page and 10 mm on the Qrevo S page.
