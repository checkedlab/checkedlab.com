How we picked

Checked Lab lines up maker pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The Best for line uses capacity, flow, or material. The Drinkwell page states 168 oz. The Catit page states 3 L (100 fl oz) and 3 flow settings. The Raindrop page states 60 oz and 18/8 stainless steel, dishwasher safe. Ounces and liters stay in the unit printed.

Automatic feeders have their own page. A fountain card is a water capacity, a material, and a power line, not a meal schedule.

What to look for

  1. Read capacity in the printed unit. Drinkwell states 168 oz. Catit states 3 L (100 fl oz). Raindrop states 60 oz. This page does not add those figures together.
  2. Read material and cleaning lines. Drinkwell states BPA-free, top-shelf dishwasher safe. Raindrop states 18/8 stainless steel, dishwasher safe, and a replaceable charcoal filter. Catit states a weight of 1.32 lb and 3 flow settings.
  3. Read power when the card prints it. The Drinkwell page states plug-in. The Catit and Raindrop spec lists on this site do not print a power line.
  4. Read height if the fountain sits under a cabinet. The Raindrop dimensions are 11.75 x 9.75 x 4.25 in. The Catit size is 8.3 x 8.3 x 7.3 in. The Drinkwell dimensions are 15.8 × 10.3 × 10.0 in.
