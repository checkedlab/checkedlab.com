How to check your serial number

Match the model number first. A qualifying serial is not every unit of that model. Each card uses the location printed on that notice.

On the bottom

A1263

The June 12, 2025 CPSC notice says model A1263 and the serial number SN are printed on the bottom. Only A1263 units sold in the U.S. with qualifying serials are included. Check the serial at anker.com/product-recalls.

On the bottom

A1112011

The July 5, 2024 Health Canada notice names Anker 321 PowerCore 5K, black, model A1112011. It says affected units have specific 16-digit serial numbers on the bottom. Check the serial at anker.com/ca/a1112-recall-form.

On the bottom

A1642

The October 17, 2024 CPSC notice names A1642, A1647, and A1652, and says those model numbers are on the back. It does not say where the serial is printed. The Health Canada A1642 notice says the serial is on the bottom. Check it at anker.com/ca/a1642-recall. Anker’s A1642, A1647, and A1652 page says the serial is 16 digits on the bottom and does not contain the letter O or the letter I.

Back, side, or bottom

A1652

The October 17, 2024 CPSC notice says A1652 is on the back. The September 18, 2025 CPSC notice says the model number is on the back or side, and the serial is next to SN Code on the back or side. Check that serial at anker.com/rc2506. Anker’s A1642, A1647, and A1652 page says the serial is 16 digits on the bottom and does not contain the letter O or the letter I. Anker’s rc2506 page says the serial is usually printed on the side or back, and that affected serial numbers do not contain the letters O or I.

Back or side

A1257

The September 18, 2025 CPSC notice names A1257 with A1647, A1652, A1681, and A1689. It says the model number is on the back or side, and the serial is next to SN Code on the back or side. Check it at anker.com/rc2506. Anker’s page for that recall says affected serial numbers do not contain the letters O or I. No Health Canada notice names A1257.

Side or back

A1647

The June 27, 2025 Health Canada notice names A1647, A1681, and A1689. It says the serial is on the side or the back, and that serials of affected products do not contain the letters O or I. Check it at anker.com/ca/rc2506. The same model is also on the October 17, 2024 and September 18, 2025 CPSC notices. The September notice says the serial is next to SN Code on the back or side, checked at anker.com/rc2506.

Side or back

A1681

The June 27, 2025 Health Canada notice says the serial for A1681 and A1689 is on the side or the back. Check it at anker.com/ca/rc2506. The September 18, 2025 CPSC notice says the serial is next to SN Code on the back or side, checked at anker.com/rc2506.

Side or back

A1689

A1689 is named with A1681 on the June 27, 2025 Health Canada notice and the September 18, 2025 CPSC notice. Use the same serial location and the same two check pages as A1681: anker.com/ca/rc2506 and anker.com/rc2506.

Back or bottom

A1366

The March 9, 2023 CPSC notice names Anker 535 PowerCore 20K, model A1366, and says A1366 is on the back. It does not use a serial list. Stop using a recalled unit and contact Fantasia Trading for a full refund. Anker’s A1366 page says to read the bottom of the battery for 535 Power Bank (PowerCore 20K) and Model A1366. The Health Canada notice for the same model says all serial numbers and a refund.

The model numbers in these cards and the table are A1112011, A1257, A1263, A1366, A1642, A1647, A1652, A1681, and A1689. A1289 is not one of them.

If you're replacing it

The notices name specific banks, including A1263, A1642, A1647, and A1652. A1289, the 737 PowerCore 24K, is not one of those numbers. It is a 24,000 mAh bank with up to 140 W from USB-C. A portable power station is a different product.

Questions

Which Anker power banks were recalled?

The notices name A1112011, A1257, A1263, A1366, A1642, A1647, A1652, A1681, and A1689. A qualifying serial is not every unit of a model. A1257, A1263, and A1652 are on CPSC notices. A1112011 is on a Health Canada notice. A1366, A1642, A1647, A1681, and A1689 are on both.

How do I check an Anker recall serial number?

A1263: the model and the serial marked SN are printed on the bottom. Check it at anker.com/product-recalls. A1112011: a 16-digit serial on the bottom, checked at anker.com/ca/a1112-recall-form. A1642: Health Canada says the serial is on the bottom and to use anker.com/ca/a1642-recall. Anker’s page for A1642, A1647, and A1652 says the serial is 16 digits on the bottom and does not contain the letter O or the letter I. A1647, A1652, A1257, A1681, and A1689: the September 18, 2025 CPSC notice says the serial is next to SN Code on the back or side. Check it at anker.com/rc2506. The Health Canada notice for A1647, A1681, and A1689 says the serial is on the side or the back, affected serials do not contain the letters O or I, and the check page is anker.com/ca/rc2506. A1366: the CPSC notice says the model number is on the back and does not use a serial list.

What is the remedy for a recalled Anker power bank?

Stop using a unit the notice includes. The June 12, 2025 CPSC notice for A1263 says a free replacement. The October 17, 2024 CPSC notice for A1642, A1647, and A1652 says a free replacement. The September 18, 2025 CPSC notice for A1647, A1652, A1257, A1681, and A1689 says a full cash refund or an Anker gift card. The March 9, 2023 CPSC notice for A1366 says a full refund, and the Health Canada notice for A1366 says a refund. The Health Canada notice for A1647, A1681, and A1689 says a free replacement or a voucher. The Health Canada notice for A1112011 says Anker sends a new power bank to users verified as part of the recall batch. Do not put the lithium-ion battery in household trash.

What the notices say

A1112011, A1257, A1263, A1366, A1642, A1647, A1652, A1681, and A1689 are the model numbers in the card above. The serial section says where each serial is printed. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-20 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2025-09-18

Anker Power Banks Recalled Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Anker power banks with model numbers A1647, A1652, A1257, A1681, and A1689. It says Anker is engraved on the front, the model number is on the back or side, and the serial number is next to SN Code on the back or side.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • A1647
  • A1652
  • A1257
  • A1681
  • A1689

How to check your model

The notice says to read the model number and the serial number next to SN Code on the back or side, then verify the serial at anker.com/rc2506.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled power banks and visit anker.com/rc2506 to verify the serial and register. It says an impacted product qualifies for a full cash refund or an Anker gift card. It says to submit a photo showing the model number, serial number, the owner’s name, the date, and the word recalled written on the unit.

CPSC · 2025-06-12

Anker Power Banks Recalled Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Anker PowerCore 10000 power banks with model number A1263. It says Anker is engraved on the front, and the model number and serial number are printed on the bottom. It says only A1263 units sold in the U.S. with qualifying serial numbers are included.

Model number in this CPSC notice

  • A1263

How to check your model

The notice says to read model A1263 and the serial number on the bottom, then check that serial number at anker.com/product-recalls.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using a recalled power bank and contact Anker for a free replacement. It says the registration asks for a photo showing the model number, serial number, the owner’s name, the date, and the word recalled written on the unit. It also says not to put the lithium-ion battery in household trash or ordinary recycling.

CPSC · 2024-10-17

Anker Power Banks Recalled Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Anker power banks with model numbers A1642, A1647, and A1652. It says Anker is engraved on the front and the model number is on the back.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • A1642
  • A1647
  • A1652

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the back of the power bank.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled power banks and contact Anker for a free replacement, registering at anker.com/product-recalls. It says to dispose of the lithium-ion batteries under local rules.

CPSC · 2023-03-09

Fantasia Trading Recalls Anker Power Banks Due to Fire Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Anker 535 mobile power banks, PowerCore 20K, with model number A1366. It says Anker is engraved on the front and A1366 is on the back.

Model number in this CPSC notice

  • A1366

How to check your model

The notice says the model number A1366 is on the back of the power bank.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled power banks and contact Fantasia Trading for a full refund and for its instructions on disposing of the lithium-ion batteries.

Health Canada · 2025-06-27

Anker Power Bank A1647 and Anker Zolo Power Banks A1681 and A1689 recalled due to fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says certain units of Anker Power Bank A1647 and Anker Zolo power banks A1681 and A1689 are recalled for a fire hazard. It says affected units are identified by serial number, and that serial numbers of affected products do not contain the letters O or I.

Model numbers in this Health Canada notice

  • A1647
  • A1681
  • A1689

How to check your model

The notice says the serial number is printed on the side or the back, and to compare it with the list at anker.com/ca/rc2506.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled units and contact Anker for a free replacement or a voucher. It says to dispose of the lithium-ion battery so it cannot be used, using the municipality’s instructions, and not to put it in household garbage.

Health Canada · 2024-09-24

Anker 334 MagGo Battery (PowerCore 10K), model A1642, recalled due to potential fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice names the Anker 334 MagGo Battery, PowerCore 10K, model A1642, and says to use Anker’s site to see whether a serial number is included.

Model number in this Health Canada notice

  • A1642

How to check your model

The notice says to check the serial number on Anker’s website.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the product, check the serial number, and if the unit is included, dispose of it so it cannot be used again, following the municipality’s lithium-ion battery instructions.

Health Canada · 2024-07-05

Anker 321 Power Banks (PowerCore 5K) recalled due to potential fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says certain units of the Anker 321 Power Bank, PowerCore 5K, black, model A1112011, are identified by specific 16-digit serial numbers on the bottom.

Model number in this Health Canada notice

  • A1112011

How to check your model

The notice says the serial number is on the bottom and to check it with Anker at anker.com/ca/a1112-recall-form.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using a recalled unit, dispose of it so it cannot be used, follow municipal lithium-ion battery instructions, and not put it in household garbage. It says Anker sends a new power bank to users verified as part of the recall batch.

Health Canada · 2023-02-23

Anker 535 Power Bank (PowerCore 20K) A1366 recalled due to fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice names the Anker 535 Power Bank, PowerCore 20K, model A1366, and describes a fire hazard.

Model number in this Health Canada notice

  • A1366

How to check your model

The notice names model A1366. Use the linked notice for the label details it prints.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled power bank and contact Anker customer support for a refund.
