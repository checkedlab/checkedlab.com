How to choose pet gear

Pet cards split into litter, feeding and water, grooming, and gear the animal wears. The limit is almost always a size the maker stated: a footprint, a cup capacity, a dog weight, a chest measurement, or a kibble size. A product with 'pet' in the name is not automatically the right size.

Match the model, then read the line that names the animal. The gift guide for pet owners uses this same list. It does not add a second list of products.

Litter boxes are limited by footprint and by the household the maker describes. The Litter-Robot 4 page lists 29.5 in tall, a 22 × 27 in footprint, an entry of 15.75 × 15.75 in, 24 lb empty, up to 8 days before emptying the drawer in a 1-cat household, and a household of up to 4 cats. The PetSafe ScoopFree Plus page lists up to 30 days, crystal litter, and dimensions of 28.2 x 20.4 x 6.6 in. Litter-Robot 4 and ScoopFree Plus is the pair.

Eight days and 30 days are the makers' own sentences about different machines. They are not a shared test. A low pan and a tall globe also ask for different floor space.

Measure the spot before you treat either number as a schedule.

Water and food are limited by volume and by the portion the page allows. The Drinkwell Platinum page lists 168 oz. The Catit Flower Fountain page lists 3 L, about 100 fl oz, and 3 flow settings.

The Pioneer Pet Raindrop page lists 60 oz and 18/8 stainless steel. Ounces and liters are both on these cards. Convert only if you need to, and do not drop the unit.

The PETLIBRO Granary page lists a 5 L tank and kibble of 2–15 mm. The PetSafe Simply Feed page lists 24 cups and portions from 1/8 to 4 cups. A 5 L tank and a 24 cup hopper are different containers.

Wearable gear is a fit, not a vibe. The FURminator page lists a medium dog, 25 to 50 lb, with short hair. The PetSafe Easy Walk page lists a front-chest leash point, and it states petite and small ranges with chest and girth inches.

The Kurgo Tru-Fit page lists five adjustment points and front and back leash attachments. The Sleepypod Clickit Sport page lists a rating up to 25 lb by the Center for Pet Safety and a crash test validated at 30 mph. That sentence is the maker's.

The KONG Classic page lists red rubber for average chewers and sizes from X-Small, up to 5 lb, to XX-Large, 85 lb and over. Pick the size line that names the dog.

Ignore a 'works for any pet' line that skips the size. Ignore a days-of-litter claim that drops the household the maker attached. The Litter-Robot sentence is up to 8 days in a 1-cat household.

The recall check is still the model match. US cards use the CPSC SaferProducts feed. Canadian cards use Health Canada's open dataset.

Search CPSC Recalls and Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. A clear line on one fountain does not cover the next fountain. How we check says what the date means. Recall checks lists the whole list.

Questions

How do I compare the two litter boxes?

Read footprint, height, and the household sentence the maker used. Litter-Robot 4 states 22 × 27 in, 29.5 in tall, and up to 8 days in a 1-cat household. ScoopFree Plus states 28.2 x 20.4 x 6.6 in and up to 30 days.

The comparison page keeps the shared labels.

What should I match on a dog harness?

The chest and girth range, or the weight, that the page prints. Easy Walk states those ranges for petite and small. Kurgo states five adjustment points and two leash points.

Sleepypod's page lists a Center for Pet Safety rating up to 25 lb and a 30 mph validation. Use the sentence on the card you are buying.

Are fountain capacities in the same unit?

Not always. Drinkwell states 168 oz. Pioneer Pet states 60 oz.

Catit states 3 L, which that page also writes as 100 fl oz. Keep the unit that is printed.

How does the recall check work for pet gear?

The US line matches the brand and model to the CPSC SaferProducts feed. The Canadian line uses Health Canada's open dataset. Search CPSC Recalls or Health Canada recalls and safety alerts with the model number.

A clear line on one fountain does not cover another fountain.

Where do the day counts on litter boxes come from?

From the product page, with the household that page names. Litter-Robot 4 states up to 8 days before emptying the drawer in a 1-cat household. ScoopFree Plus states up to 30 days.

Those are not one shared measurement.
