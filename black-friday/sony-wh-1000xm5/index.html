The model string is WH-1000XM5

The Sony card states model WH-1000XM5, battery life of max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on or max. 40 hours with it off, a weight of about 8.82 oz, a charge time of about 3.5 hours, multipoint, and that 2 processors control 8 microphones. A listing that says Sony headphones, or 1000X, still has to show WH-1000XM5 if this is the card you are matching.

The QuietComfort Ultra page states up to 24 hours, 0.56 lb, a 3 hour charge, Quiet, Aware, and Immersion modes, multipoint for two devices, and Bose Immersive Audio. The weight units are ounces on the Sony page and pounds on the Bose page. This page does not convert them.

Pick the battery sentence you meant

Pick the WH-1000XM5 if the listing shows that model and you are matching max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on, or max. 40 hours with it off, at about 8.82 oz, with a charge line of about 3.5 hours. The microphone sentence is 2 processors and 8 microphones.

Pick the QuietComfort Ultra if the listing is that Bose model and you are matching up to 24 hours, a 3 hour charge, and the mode names Quiet, Aware, and Immersion. A shorter stated charge time is the Bose line. A longer stated playback line with noise cancelling on is the Sony line.

Neither card is a recall match in the catalogue block below. Confirm the model printed on the earcup or the box, not only the brand in the title of the listing. Multipoint is on both cards. The Sony card states the microphone arrangement, 2 processors and 8 microphones, and the Bose card states the mode names. Those lines stay on their own cards because the other page does not use the same label. The charge lines are about 3.5 hours on the Sony page and 3 hours on the Bose page. Read both charge lines on the maker pages.
