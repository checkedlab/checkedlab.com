Ninja Foodi OP300 and TenderCrisp lid notices

The May 1, 2025 CPSC notice names Ninja Foodi OP300-series pressure cookers. The Health Canada notice names TenderCrisp pressure-cooking lids. The DT201C oven and FN101 Crispi cards are not those model numbers. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-10 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2025-05-01

SharkNinja Recalls Foodi Multi-Function Pressure Cookers Due to Burn Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves all Ninja Foodi OP300 Series Multi-Function Pressure Cookers, with pressure cooking and air frying, sold in black with a 6.5-quart capacity. It says Ninja is printed on the front and on the product label. It says an extra code after the model number is not part of the model designation, and gives the example that a unit labeled OP301 I07 is a model OP301. It also includes OP300-series replacement pressure-cooker lids bought as a separate part.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • OP300
  • OP301
  • OP301A
  • OP302
  • OP302BRN
  • OP302HCN
  • OP302HAQ
  • OP302HW
  • OP302HB
  • OP305
  • OP305CO
  • OP350CO

How to check your model

The notice says the model numbers are printed on a label on the side of the cooker.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the pressure-cooking function and contact SharkNinja for a free replacement lid. It says the air frying and other functions can still be used.

Health Canada · 2025-05-01

Ninja Foodi TenderCrisp pressure-cooking lid recalled due to potential burn hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says the recall involves the pressure-cooking lid on Ninja Foodi TenderCrisp multi-cookers, including replacement pressure-cooking lids bought separately from SharkNinja. It says the cookers have a 6.5-quart chamber and the brand name Ninja is on the front of the hinged lid. It says other functions, such as air crisping, are not part of this recall.

Model numbers in the Health Canada notice

  • OP300C
  • OP301
  • OP301C
  • OP302
  • OP305
  • OP305CCO
  • OP305CO

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the rating label on the back of the unit, near the power cord.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the pressure-cooking function, dispose of the pressure-cooking lid so it cannot be used, and contact SharkNinja to confirm that disposal and arrange a replacement lid.
