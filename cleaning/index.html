How to choose a vacuum

This hub is two kinds of machine: robot vacuums, some of which also mop, and cordless stick vacuums. The model number is the decision. Shark uses AV2501S for the AI Ultra robot and AV2820AE for the PowerDetect robot.

Dyson uses V15 Detect Absolute and V8 for two cordless machines. A listing that says Shark or Dyson without that number is not yet a card. Match the number on the unit to the card, then read the product page linked from the specs.

The comparison pages keep only the labels both of those product pages share.

Weight, run time, and the unit of suction are the lines that change the job. The Dyson V8 page lists 5.58 lb and a 40 minute maximum. The V15 Detect Absolute page lists 6.83 lb, up to 60 minutes, 230 AW in Boost mode, and whole-machine HEPA filtration.

The Shark IP3251 page lists 18.47 lbs and up to 70 minutes measured at the hand vacuum in ECO mode. A lighter stated weight and a longer stated run time are different facts. ECO and Boost are different modes.

The Tineco PURE ONE S15 PRO page lists 6.8 lbs and 40 minutes. Read the mode the maker attached to the minutes.

Robot cards print pascals, not air watts. The Roborock Qrevo S page lists 7,000 Pa and a robot height of 3.80 in. The eufy X10 Pro Omni page lists 8,000 Pa, and 173 minutes vacuuming or 136 minutes vacuuming and mopping, on standard.

The Roomba Combo j9+ page lists a robot weight of 8.9 lb and a separate dock of 15.8 in W x 16.4 in D x 16.3 in H. The dock is not the robot. Pascals are not air watts, and the comparison tables do not convert one into the other. Qrevo S and X10 Pro Omni and Combo j9+ and Qrevo S are the robot pairs.

Ignore a coverage slogan the product page does not print. Ignore a pet-hair line the card does not state. The BISSELL ICONpet 2288 page lists 22 V, a 0.4 L dirty tank, a 10.5 in cleaning path, and 7 lbs.

That is what the page lists. For loose undercoat, the FURminator medium short-hair tool is on the pet hub, sized for a dog of 25 to 50 lb with short hair. A self-empty dock counts when that model's page lists the dock. AV2501S and AV2820AE share a 5.7 in cleaning path and state different weights, voltages, and wattages.

The AI Ultra page lists 120 volts and 650 watts. The PowerDetect robot page lists 14.4 volts and 35 watts.

The recall line is a dated match, not a review of how the vacuum cleans. US cards use the CPSC SaferProducts feed. Canadian cards use Health Canada's open dataset.

Search the model on CPSC Recalls and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. A clear line for the V8 does not cover the V15. A clear line for AV2501S does not cover AV2820AE. How we check says the date is the day the match was recorded. Recall checks lists every model.

Questions

What should I compare on two cordless vacuums?

Weight and run time, in the mode the maker names. The V8 page lists 5.58 lb and 40 minutes maximum. The V15 page lists 6.83 lb and up to 60 minutes.

The IP3251 page lists 18.47 lbs and up to 70 minutes at the hand vacuum in ECO mode.

Can I rank a robot's pascals against a Dyson's air watts?

No. Pascals and air watts are different units. Qrevo S states 7,000 Pa and the X10 Pro Omni states 8,000 Pa.

The V15 page lists 230 AW in Boost mode. The comparison tables leave a label out when only one page prints it.

Why do the two Shark robots show different voltages?

Because each Shark page prints its own electrical line. AV2501S states 120 volts and 650 watts. AV2820AE states 14.4 volts and 35 watts.

Both state a 5.7 in cleaning path. Read the row as the maker printed it.

How does the recall check work for a vacuum?

The US line is a match against the CPSC SaferProducts feed. The Canadian line is a match against Health Canada's open dataset. Search the same model on CPSC Recalls or Health Canada recalls and safety alerts.

A clear line for one model number does not cover the next number in the brand.

Where do the specs come from?

From the product page linked on the model card. The V15 HEPA line, for example, is the whole-machine HEPA sentence on Dyson's page for that model. A HEPA word in a listing is a different sentence unless that model's page says it.
