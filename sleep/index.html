How to choose sleep gear

Sleep cards cover mattresses, pillows, bedding, sound, light, and the chairs people sit in for hours. Comfort language is easy to inflate and hard to compare. The lines that stay useful are the ones a maker page can print: height, materials, coil counts, a stated feel name, a fill, and a weight limit.

This hub does not turn those lines into a softness ranking. Gifts for better sleep regroups some of the same cards. The recall check on each card is separate from the materials list.

Mattresses differ by construction, and the page says which construction. The Casper Original page states a queen size of 60 in by 80 in by 11 in and a queen weight of 79 lb, with a foam top and a zoned memory-foam core. The Saatva Classic page states heights of 11.5 in or 14.5 in, 1,300 coils in a queen, and feel names of Plush Soft, Luxury Firm, or Firm.

The Helix Midnight page states a queen of 60 in by 80 in by 11.5 in. The Zinus 12 inch page states 12 inches, with 2.5 in of gel memory foam, and CertiPUR-US foams. An 11 in foam mattress and a coil mattress with two stated heights are different builds.

Read the height that will meet the sheets. The Brooklinen Luxe Core page states a fitted sheet for mattresses up to 16 in deep.

Pillows are a fill and a size, not a slogan. The Purple Harmony page states a cover of 92 percent nylon and 8 percent spandex, a ventilated Talalay latex core, and a standard low size of 26 × 17 × 5.5 in at 4.2 lb. The Tempur-Neck page states three heights: small 3.5 in, medium 4 in, and large 4.75 in, all 20 × 12.5 in.

The Coop Original page states a fill of cross-cut memory foam and microfiber and a medium-firm feel. The Buffy Cloud page states a 300 thread count lyocell shell and a recycled PET fill. Thread count on a comforter is not loft.

Height on a pillow is not thread count.

Chairs and bedside machines have hard limits too. The Herman Miller Aeron page states sizes A, B, and C, an 8Z Pellicle suspension, and PostureFit SL lumbar. The Steelcase Leap page states a seat height of 15.5 to 20.5 in, a chair weight of 46.7 lb, and a 400 lb limit.

Pick the size or the range the page names. The Hatch Restore 3 page states 7.5 x 2.75 x 5.75 in and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. The LectroFan page states 10 fan sounds and 10 white noises.

The Philips HF3520 page states 5 wake-up sounds and an FM radio. A light, a noise machine, and a mattress are three purchases.

Ignore a cooling word that the materials list does not back. Ignore a 'fits any mattress' line when the sheet page states a maximum depth, as Brooklinen does at 16 in. The recall check does not grade comfort.

US cards use the CPSC SaferProducts feed. Canadian cards use Health Canada's open dataset. Search CPSC Recalls and Health Canada recalls and safety alerts.

A clear line on one mattress does not cover another mattress. How we check says what the date means. Recall checks is the full list.

Questions

What should I read on a mattress card?

Height, construction, and any feel name the maker actually lists. Casper states 11 in for the queen and a foam build. Saatva states 11.5 or 14.5 in, 1,300 queen coils, and the names Plush Soft, Luxury Firm, or Firm.

Zinus states 12 in. Match the height to the sheets. Brooklinen states a fitted sheet for mattresses up to 16 in deep.

How do the pillow cards differ?

By fill and by the size the page prints. Coop states cross-cut memory foam and microfiber. Tempur-Neck states three heights.

Purple Harmony states a latex core and a standard low of 5.5 in at 4.2 lb. A feel word matters when the page names it, as Coop does with medium-firm.

What limits a desk chair on this hub?

The size system or the range the maker prints. Aeron states sizes A, B, and C. Leap states seat height from 15.5 to 20.5 in, a chair weight of 46.7 lb, and a 400 lb limit.

Read the limit on the card, not a general word about ergonomics.

How does the recall check work for a mattress or a pillow?

The US line matches the brand and model to the CPSC SaferProducts feed. The Canadian line uses Health Canada's open dataset. A clear line on one mattress does not cover a different mattress.

Search CPSC Recalls or Health Canada recalls and safety alerts with the model name on the card.

Do these pages rank how soft a bed feels?

They repeat the maker's own words. Saatva lists Plush Soft, Luxury Firm, and Firm as the feels on that page. Casper states the foam layers.

There is no softness number added on top of those sentences.
