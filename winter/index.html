How to choose winter gear

Winter cards on this hub cover heat, humidity, snow, and a few cold-weather objects that are not heaters at all. A ceramic tower, an oil-filled radiator, a propane heater, a humidifier, a snow blower, and a wool blanket do not share a spec. Start by naming the job, then read the label line that limits that job. Winter home safety is the companion set of guides for space heaters, heated bedding, carbon monoxide and smoke alarms, and portable power.

Those guides say what to read on a label. This page is the catalogue of named models.

Electric heaters are limited by wattage, voltage, size, and the safety line the maker prints. The Lasko CT32150 page states 900 W to 1,500 W, 120 V, 31.6 in, 5.7 lb, ETL listing, a tip-over switch, and overheat protection. The CT14401 page states the same wattage and voltage, 14 in, 3.3 lb, ETL listing, and overheat protection. CT32150 and CT14401 is that pair.

The Dreo Atom 316 page states 1500 watts and 200 sq ft. The De'Longhi Dragon 4 page states 1500 W maximum, 700 W minimum, 6 fins, and 25.61 lb. A taller tower and a heavier radiator are different objects.

The Lasko heater explainer quotes notices that name specific heater models.

Fuel-burning heat is a different label. The Mr. Heater Portable Buddy page states 4,000 or 9,000 BTU per hour, up to 225 sq ft, and 3 hours at the maximum setting.

Read the space heater guide and the alarm guide beside that card. Heated bedding has its own guide too. The Pendleton Eco-Wise page states a full/queen wool blanket, 90 in by 96 in, at 5.5 lb.

That is a blanket, not a heater. The electric blanket guide is where the heated-bedding label lines live.

Snow tools are limited by clearing width, depth or intake height, and the power source the page names. The Snow Joe 24V-SS10 manual states a 10 in clearing width, snow depth up to 6 in, and 9.9 lb. The Toro Power Clear 721 E page states a 212 cc engine, a 21 in clearing width, a 12.5 in intake height, and 84 lb.

The EGO SNT2110 page states a 21 in clearing width, throw up to 40 ft, and a steel auger, and it states that peak power uses two 56V batteries. A 10 in shovel width and a 21 in blower width are not the same job. Humidifiers on the winter list are tanks: the Classic 300S page states 6 L and up to 60 hours.

Ignore a square-footage line that is not on that model's page. Ignore a tip-over claim that the safety line does not include. The CT32150 safety line names a tip-over switch.

The CT14401 safety line names ETL listing and overheat protection. The recall check is still the model match. US cards use the CPSC SaferProducts feed.

Canadian cards use Health Canada's open dataset. Search CPSC Recalls and Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. A clear line on one Lasko model does not cover the other. How we check says what the date means.

Questions

What should I read on a ceramic tower heater?

Wattage, voltage, height, weight, and the safety line. Both Lasko cards state 900 W to 1,500 W and 120 V. CT32150 adds a tip-over switch to its safety line.

CT14401 states ETL listing and overheat protection. The space heater guide says which label marks to compare on the unit.

Are all 1,500 watt heaters the same card?

No. Dreo states 1500 watts and 200 sq ft for the Atom 316. De'Longhi states 1500 W maximum and 25.61 lb for the Dragon 4.

Lasko states a range from 900 W to 1,500 W. Match the model number, not the wattage alone.

Where do snow-tool widths come from?

From the maker page or manual on the card. Snow Joe states a 10 in clearing width. Toro and EGO each state 21 in.

Toro also states an 84 lb weight and a 212 cc engine. A shovel width and a blower width are different jobs.

How does the recall check work for a heater?

The US line matches the model to the CPSC SaferProducts feed. The Canadian line uses Health Canada's open dataset. The Lasko explainer quotes notices that name specific heater models.

A clear line on CT32150 does not cover CT14401.

What is the difference between this hub and winter safety?

This hub is the catalogue of named models and the maker specs on those cards. Winter home safety is the set of label guides for space heaters, heated bedding, alarms, and outage power, including models that are not a card.
