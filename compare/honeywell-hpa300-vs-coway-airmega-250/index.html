How to read this

Size, weight, CADR, and filter life as the Honeywell and Coway pages state them for the HPA300 and the Airmega 250, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-14 (UTC).

The HPA300 product details state 20.8 x 10.83 x 22.38 in, 17 lb, smoke CADR 300, dust CADR 320, pollen CADR 300, a True HEPA filter replaced once per year, and a pre-filter replaced every 3 months. The Airmega 250 specifications state 18.5 x 19.7 x 8.3 in, 20.5 lb, smoke CADR 249, dust CADR 261, pollen CADR 230, and a Max2 filter life of 1 year.

Higher stated smoke, dust, and pollen CADR figures are the HPA300 lines. A lower stated weight is the HPA300 line. Noise and the square-foot coverage lines use labels that only one card uses, so they are left out.
