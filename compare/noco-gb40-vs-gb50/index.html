How to read this

Peak current, engine limits, weight, and enclosure rating as the NOCO pages state them for GB40 and GB50, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The GB40 page states 1,000 A peak current, gas engines up to 6 L, diesel engines up to 3 L, and 2.4 lb. The GB50 page states 1,500 A, gas engines up to 7 L, diesel engines up to 4.5 L, and 2.7 lb. Both pages state IP65.

If the engine size is above the limit on the GB40 line and within the limit on the GB50 line, the GB50 figures are the ones that name that size. If you are reading the lower stated weight, that figure is on the GB40 line.
