How to read this

Noise and weight as the Levoit pages state them for the Core 300S-P and the Core 600S-P, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Core 300S-P manual states noise of 22–54.5 dB and a weight of 7.48 lb. The Core 600S-P page states noise of 26–55 dB (as low as 26 dB per the maker) and a weight of 13.7 lb / 6.2 kg.

A lower stated weight is the Core 300S-P line. The 600S-P noise line is 26–55 dB (as low as 26 dB per the maker). The 300S-P noise line is 22–54.5 dB. Power, size, CADR, and room size use labels that only one card uses.

Same brand, two cabinets

Both cards are Levoit Core models, and the shared labels are noise and weight. The 300S-P manual prints 22–54.5 dB and 7.48 lb, with dimensions of 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in and rated power of 39 W at AC 120 V, 60 Hz. The 600S-P page prints 26–55 dB (as low as 26 dB per the maker) and 13.7 lb / 6.2 kg, with a size of 12.3 x 12.3 x 23.6 in and power of 49 W at AC 120 V, 60 Hz.

CADR on the 600S-P page is 391 CFM. Room size on that page is 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour, or 606 sq ft at 4.8. The 300S-P card does not use a CADR label or a room-size label, so those rows stay off the table. Filtration on the 600S-P page says 3-stage. That label is not on the 300S-P card.

Pick the smaller stated weight or the CADR line

Pick the Core 300S-P manual if the weight you are matching is 7.48 lb and the cabinet you are matching is 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in. The power line that goes with that cabinet is 39 W. The noise line is 22–54.5 dB.

Pick the Core 600S-P page if the figure you are matching is 391 CFM, the room line that names 606 sq ft at 4.8 air changes, or the taller cabinet of 12.3 x 12.3 x 23.6 in. The stated weight on that page is 13.7 lb, and the noise line is 26–55 dB (as low as 26 dB per the maker). A lower stated wattage is the 300S-P line, 39 W against 49 W, and those power labels are not identical, so wattage stays on the cards.

Both noise lines are ranges. The 600S-P page prints 26–55 dB (as low as 26 dB per the maker). The 300S-P manual prints 22–54.5 dB. Read the range the page prints before comparing the low ends. The recall lines below are the catalogue match for each model.
