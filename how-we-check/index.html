How we check

Checked Lab checks sources, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada. We do not run a lab.

What we check

  • Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The link sits next to the sentence.
  • Store rows for the region you are in. US rows are Amazon.com, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, and Home Depot, in USD. Canada rows are Amazon.ca, Best Buy CA, Canadian Tire, Walmart.ca, and Wayfair.ca, in CAD.
  • US recalls from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. A daily GitHub Action reruns that match.

What we do not do

  • We do not buy products for review. “Bought by us” is used only when a receipt is on the card. None of the current products have that badge.
  • We do not run a hands-on test, a 30-day lab, or our own noise, heat, or power measurements.
  • We do not invent prices, ratings, check times, stock, or test scores. If a price or a time has not been checked, the cell says “not checked yet.”
  • The BUY NOW / WAIT meter and the fake-sale flag stay off unless a real price history, with a source, is stored for that region.
  • Brands do not pay for placement and do not edit these pages.

Price Truth

The box at the top of a product card is the price check for the region you selected. Each store has its own checked time. Amazon links use the affiliate tag already in the repo: checkedlab01-20 on Amazon.com and checkedlab-20 on Amazon.ca. Other stores have no link until we have a real product URL. Those tags are not placeholders we made up.

Recalls

A red BIN means the brand and the model token both appear in one recall record. A different product from the same brand is not a BIN. It can be listed under the card as a recall that did not match this model. If we have not confirmed the model number, the card says the recall status is not confirmed. That is not a clearance and not a BIN.

“No recall found as of {date}” means the check ran on that date and no record matched this model. It is not a promise that a recall can never exist.

Region

The US / CA switch starts from a choice saved in this browser. If you have not chosen, it uses the browser language, then a Canadian time zone as a stand-in for location, then the US. Choosing US or CA saves that choice. The US is the fallback. Winter copy follows the region: snow-belt states in the US, and a Canadian winter in Canada.
