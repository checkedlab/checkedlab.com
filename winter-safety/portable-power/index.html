Portable power stations and generators

What to read on a portable power station or generator, including CO shutoff, and official notices for battery overheating and a fuel leak.

Space heatersElectric blanketsCO and smoke alarmsOutage power

What to read on the label

  • A 2022 CPSC news release says UL 2201 and PGMA G300 both require a generator to shut off when certain concentrations of carbon monoxide are present around the generator. Read the generator label for a CO shutoff that names one of those standards.
  • Read the model and serial number on the generator frame or nameplate, and on the bottom of a portable power station. A power bank is a different product from a portable power station.

How to search a recall

For a purchase in the United States, search the brand and the model on CPSC Recalls. For a purchase in Canada, search the same words on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. Open the notice and match the model number on the unit. A family name on the box can point at a different model.

The catalogue has no portable power station card and no portable generator card. Anker power bank notices name power bank model numbers. Use those pages for a power bank, and use the notices below for a power station or a generator.

Patterns in official notices

Each pattern below is one official notice. The sentence is what that notice says.

  • CPSC: HALO 1000 Portable Power Stations Recalled Due to Serious Fire and Burn Hazards. The notice says the lithium-ion batteries in the HALO 1000 portable power station can overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. It names model PS-1000. The model number is on the bottom of the unit.
  • Health Canada: EcoFlow Delta Max 2000 Power Stations recalled due to risk of serious burn injury and fire hazard. The notice says the power stations may overheat and ignite. It says EFD310 and EcoFlow DELTA Max (2000) are printed on the nameplate on the underside. The remedy on the notice is a firmware update, not a physical return.
  • Health Canada: Generac portable generators recalled due to fire hazard. The notice says that when some liquid-propane and natural-gas generators are first filled, fuel can leak from the carburetor, posing a fire hazard. It lists model numbers G0077130, G0077150, and G0077210. The model and serial number are on a label on the alternator or the frame.

Questions

What does the CPSC release say about generator CO shutoff?

The 2022 CPSC news release says UL 2201 and PGMA G300 both require a generator to shut off when certain concentrations of carbon monoxide are present around the generator. Read the label for that shutoff.

What does the HALO 1000 notice say?

The CPSC notice says the lithium-ion batteries in the HALO 1000 portable power station can overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. It names model PS-1000, printed on the bottom.

Are the Anker power bank notices the same as a portable power station?

No. Those notices name power bank model numbers. A portable power station is a different product. Use the Anker power bank notices page for a power bank, and search the power station model on CPSC Recalls or Health Canada recalls and safety alerts.
