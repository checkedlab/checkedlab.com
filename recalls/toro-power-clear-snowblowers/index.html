Toro Power Clear snowblower recalls: the model numbers

The 2010 and 2011 CPSC and Health Canada notices name Power Clear 180 models 38272 and 38282, and PC-421 models 38588 and 38589. Catalogue model 38753 is not in those lists. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2010-07-14

Snow Blowers Recalled by The Toro Company Due to Fire Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves Toro Power Clear 180 single-stage snow blowers, model numbers 38272 and 38282, serial numbers 310000001 through 310999999. It says the model and serial number are on a decal on the lower right side of the snow blower. It describes a fire hazard from a corroded carburetor needle that can stick open and leak fuel.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • 38272
  • 38282
  • Serial numbers 310000001 through 310999999

How to check your model

The notice says to read the model and serial decal on the lower right side.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using a unit in the recall and contact an authorized Toro service dealer for a free repair.

Health Canada · 2010-07-14

Toro Power Clear 180 Snow Blower

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says the recall involves Toro Power Clear 180 single-stage snow blowers, model numbers 38272 and 38282, serial numbers 310000001 through 310999999. It says the model serial decal is on the lower right rear of the machine. It is a joint recall with the CPSC.

Model numbers in this Health Canada notice

  • 38272
  • 38282
  • Serial numbers 310000001 through 310999999

How to check your model

The notice says the model serial decal is on the lower right rear of the machine.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to check whether the snow blower is included and contact an authorized Toro service dealer for a free repair.

CPSC · 2011-04-26

Toro Recalls Power Clear Snowblowers and Recycler Mowers Due to Fire or Burn Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the snowblower recall involves Toro PC-421Q models 38588 and 38589. It lists serial ranges for each model and says the model and serial numbers are on a decal on the underside of the rear of the unit. It also names Recycler mower model 20323. It says the carburetors can leak fuel and ignite.

Snowblower model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • 38588
  • 38589
  • Mower model 20323 is also named

How to check your model

The notice says the snowblower model and serial numbers are on a decal on the underside of the rear of the unit.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the products and contact a Toro service dealer for a free repair.

Health Canada · 2011-04-26

Toro Power Clear Snow Blower and Push Recycler Mower

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says the recall involves 2010 and 2011 Toro Power Clear 421Q snow blowers. It names model 38588 with serials 310000001 to 311003576, and model 38589 with serials 310000001 to 311999999. It also names push mower model 20323. It says the snow blower model and serial numbers are on a decal on the underside of the rear of the unit.

Snowblower model numbers in this Health Canada notice

  • 38588
  • 38589
  • Mower model 20323 is also named

How to check your model

The notice says to read the decal on the underside of the rear of the snow blower.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the snow blowers and mowers and contact a Toro service dealer for a free repair.
