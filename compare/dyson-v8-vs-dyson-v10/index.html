Dyson V8 vs V10: which to buy

The V8 is the lighter vacuum: Dyson lists 5.58 lb, 115 air watts in MAX mode, and a 40-minute maximum. The Cyclone V10 Absolute lists 5.71 lb, 150 air watts in Boost mode, and up to 60 minutes, with a 3.5-hour charge and a 0.2 gallon bin. Choose the V8 when the lower weight matters more. Choose the V10 Absolute when you want the higher suction figure and the longer run-time line.

Choose the V8 if

  • You want the lower weight, 5.58 lb.
  • You want 115 air watts in MAX mode and a 40-minute maximum.
  • You want the smaller bin, 0.14 gallon, and a full charge in 5 hours is fine.

How to read this

The table keeps a row only when both makers print that label. The Source link opens the sentence the number came from. A figure only one maker prints sits under this fold. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-13 (UTC).

Dyson’s V8 specification list prints 5.58 lb, a 40 min maximum, a 5 hr charge, a 0.14 gallon bin, and 115 AW in MAX mode. The Cyclone V10 Absolute specification list prints 5.71 lb, up to 60 min, a 3.5 hr charge, a 0.2 gallon bin, and 150 AW in Boost mode.

The V8 list also prints 15 cyclones, whole-machine filtration, and a cabinet of 49.45 in high, 8.7 in long, and 9.84 in wide. The V10 Absolute list prints 14 cyclones, whole-machine filtration, and 48.74 in by 9.53 in by 9.84 in. Width is the same figure. Height and length are not.

Dyson V8 vs V10 run time is not one number

The V8 specifications list says maximum run time of 40 min. A footnote on that same V8 page says actual run time varies with power mode, dust level, floor type, and attachments. The product description also says up to 40 minutes from a six-cell battery. The V8 page says a full charge takes up to 5 hours, and that the battery can be left on charge.

The V10 Absolute specifications list says run time of up to 60 min, with an asterisk. Farther down, the same page says up to 35 minutes with the Torque drive cleaner head in Suction mode I, and up to 40 minutes with the Soft roller cleaner head in Suction mode I. Those are shorter than the 60-minute line. Read the head and the mode before you treat 60 minutes as the time you will get. The charge line on that list is 3.5 hrs. The V10 Absolute page also says a 2-year limited warranty. The V8 page opened here does not print a warranty length.

Suction, cyclones, and what the filtration lines say

The V8 suction line is 115 AW in MAX mode. The V10 Absolute suction line is 150 AW in Boost mode. Those are different mode names, so the air-watt numbers are not a same-mode test written out on either list. The V10 Absolute page also says the in-line motor, bin, and cyclone create 20% more suction power than the V8. That percentage and the two air-watt lines are both on Dyson’s pages. This comparison does not turn one into the other.

Cyclones are 15 on the V8 list and 14 on the V10 Absolute list. Both specification lists say whole-machine filtration and stop there. The V8 body text is less tidy: one block says 99.99% of particles down to 0.1 microns, and another says 99.99% as small as 0.3 microns, with a note that boost mode is the 99.99% figure and other modes are at least 99.97%. The V10 Absolute key-technology block says the sealed system traps 99.99% of particles as small as 0.3 microns. Use the specification list when you want the shared label, and read the micron sentence on the vacuum you are buying.

Power modes are two on the V8 page and three on the V10 Absolute page. Both describe a point-and-shoot bin empty and a wall dock. The V8 motor note says the digital motor spins at up to 110,000 rpm. The V10 Absolute page says the seven-cell battery is the fade-free pack, against the six-cell line on the V8 page.

What is in the box, then what to confirm

The V8 box list names a hair screw tool, a charger, a Motorbar cleaner head, a combination tool, a crevice tool, and a docking station. The V10 Absolute box list names a Motorbar cleaner head, a hair screw tool, a combination tool, a charger, a stubborn dirt brush, a mini soft dusting brush, a soft roller cleaner head, a crevice tool, a docking station, a fabric and mattress tool, and an up-top adaptor. Dyson says the Fluffy cleaner head and three extra tools are only at Dyson.com on that Absolute page. If a store box does not name the Fluffy head, it is not the kit this page is describing.

Both Dyson pages showed Currently out of stock when they were opened. That is availability, not a spec, and it can change. Match the name on the box to V8 or to Cyclone V10 Absolute, then match the suction line (115 AW in MAX, or 150 AW in Boost) and the bin (0.14 gallon or 0.2 gallon). Pick the V8 if you want 5.58 lb, 115 AW, the 40-minute maximum, and the 0.14 gallon bin. Pick the V10 Absolute if you want 150 AW in Boost mode, the up-to-60-minute line, the 3.5-hour charge, and the larger bin with the Fluffy head in that Dyson.com kit.

The V15 Detect Absolute comparison on this site uses the same V8 card, so the weight and run-time lines there are the same V8 figures. It is a different pair.

Questions

Is the Dyson V10 lighter than the V8?

No. Dyson lists the V8 at 5.58 lb and the Cyclone V10 Absolute at 5.71 lb.

How long does Dyson say each vacuum runs?

The V8 specification list says 40 min maximum. The V10 Absolute list says up to 60 min, and the same page also says up to 35 minutes with a Torque drive head in Suction mode I and up to 40 minutes with a Soft roller head in Suction mode I.

What suction numbers does Dyson print?

115 AW in MAX mode for the V8, and 150 AW in Boost mode for the V10 Absolute. The V10 Absolute page also says the in-line layout creates 20% more suction than the V8.

How long is the charge?

Dyson lists 5 hrs for the V8 and 3.5 hrs for the V10 Absolute. The V8 page says a full charge takes up to 5 hours and that the battery can stay on the charger.

Do both include a Motorbar head?

Yes. Both box lists name a Motorbar cleaner head and a hair screw tool. The V10 Absolute list also names a Fluffy soft roller head, which Dyson says is part of the Dyson.com kit.
