How to read this

Heat output, voltage, height, weight, and the safety line as the Lasko pages state them for CT32150 and CT14401, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-20 (UTC).

Both pages state 900 W to 1,500 W and 120 V. The CT32150 page states 31.6 in and 5.7 lb. The CT14401 page states 14 in and 3.3 lb.

The CT32150 safety line names ETL listing, a tip-over switch, and overheat protection. The CT14401 safety line names ETL listing and overheat protection. A taller stated height is the CT32150 line. A shorter stated height and a lower stated weight are the CT14401 lines.
