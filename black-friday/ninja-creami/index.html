NC301 is the model string

The CREAMi card states model NC301, programs of 7 preset programs, pints of Two 16 oz pints included, care of Containers, lids, and paddle are dishwasher safe, and a warranty of One year limited. A listing title can add a season name and still be a different machine. The string to match for this card is NC301. The box line to match is two 16 oz pints included, with containers, lids, and paddle described as dishwasher safe.

The other Ninja card on this page is the Foodi 10-in-1. It states power of 1,800 W, functions of Air Fry, Whole Roast, Air Roast, Bake, Dehydrate, Pizza, Broil, Toast, Bagel, and Reheat, a capacity line of Fits a 5 lb chicken and a sheet pan of vegetables, two 12 in pizzas, or a 12 lb turkey, dimensions of 62.2 × 50.6 × 42.49 cm, and a warranty of 1 year limited. Air Fry is one name in that function list. It is an oven card, not an ice cream maker. The NC301 card does not print an air-fry function or a wattage.

Pick the pint line or the oven function list

Pick the NC301 if the listing shows NC301 and you are matching 7 preset programs, two 16 oz pints included, and a one year limited warranty. The care line says the containers, lids, and paddle are dishwasher safe. Confirm those words on the listing and on the maker page. A different pint size, or a different program count, is a different model string.

Pick the Foodi 10-in-1 if the listing is that oven and you are matching Air Fry, Whole Roast, Air Roast, Bake, Dehydrate, Pizza, Broil, Toast, Bagel, and Reheat, at 1,800 W. The capacity sentence is Fits a 5 lb chicken and a sheet pan of vegetables, two 12 in pizzas, or a 12 lb turkey. The dimensions are 62.2 × 50.6 × 42.49 cm. Do not pick the oven because the word Ninja appears on an ice cream listing. Do not pick the CREAMi because an oven listing says Air Fry.

The comparison page for NC301 and the Deluxe NC501 is the page that lines those two ice cream makers up. This page does not copy the NC501 figures. Open that comparison and the Ninja page it cites if the listing says Deluxe, XL, or NC501. NC301 remains the 7 program card with two 16 oz pints. The Foodi OP300 notice is a pressure-cooker lid notice. It names OP300-series model numbers, not NC301. The air fryer recall list links that notice and says what it covers.

What to verify on the listing

Read the model string, then the included pints, then the warranty sentence. NC301, two 16 oz pints included, 7 preset programs, and one year limited are the four lines on this card that a listing has to match. The care line says which parts are dishwasher safe. A return window and a warranty term belong to the maker page or the retailer page you are buying from. This page does not restate a store policy that is not on the maker card.

The recall block below is the catalogue match for the NC301 and for the Foodi 10-in-1. A family name is not the model number. Search NC301 on CPSC Recalls and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts as its own string. Then search the oven’s name as its own string. The dated line under each card is the catalogue check, not a substitute for the model printed on the box.
