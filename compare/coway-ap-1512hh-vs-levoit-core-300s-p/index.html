How to read this

Size and weight as the Coway and Levoit pages state them for the AP-1512HH and the Core 300S-P, with a dated recall line for each. Specs that only one page lists are left out.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-20 (UTC).

The shared labels are dimensions and weight. Coway states 16.8 × 18.3 × 9.6 in and 12.3 lb. Levoit states 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in and 7.48 lb.

CADR, room coverage, and power are on the Coway card only. Rated power, noise, and power supply are on the Levoit card only. Those rows are left out because the other card does not use the same label.
