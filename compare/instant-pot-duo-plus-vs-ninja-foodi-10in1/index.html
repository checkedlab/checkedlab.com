How to read this

Functions, capacity, power, and dimensions as the Instant Pot and Ninja pages state them for the Duo Plus and the 10-in-1 XL Pro, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-20 (UTC).

The Duo Plus page states pressure cook, slow cook, sauté, sous vide, steam, sterilize, keep warm, rice, and yogurt, a 6 quart volume, 1,000 W at 120 V and 60 Hz, and 12.2 L x 13.4 W x 12.9 H in. The Foodi page states Air Fry, Whole Roast, Air Roast, Bake, Dehydrate, Pizza, Broil, Toast, Bagel, and Reheat, a capacity line for a 5 lb chicken and a sheet pan of vegetables, two 12 in pizzas, or a 12 lb turkey, 1,800 W, and 62.2 × 50.6 × 42.49 cm.

The capacity lines name different things: quarts on the Duo Plus page, and food that fits on the Foodi page. A lower stated wattage is the Duo Plus line. The dimension rows use the units each page prints, inches on the Instant Pot page and centimetres on the Ninja page. Warranty is on the Foodi card only.

Related spec pages: E310 and E320 specs.
