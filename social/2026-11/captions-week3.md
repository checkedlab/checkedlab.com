## Dyson V8 vs V10

Page: https://www.checkedlab.com/compare/dyson-v8-vs-dyson-v10/

### Instagram

Dyson V8 or V10? The V8 is the lighter one.

By Dyson's own numbers, the V8 is 5.58 lb, with 115 air watts in MAX mode and a 40-minute maximum. The Cyclone V10 Absolute is 5.71 lb, with 150 air watts in Boost mode and up to 60 minutes.

The charge is 5 hours on the V8 and 3.5 hours on the V10 Absolute. Bins are 0.14 gallon and 0.2 gallon. Power modes are two and three. Cyclones are 15 and 14.

The same V10 page also says up to 35 minutes with a Torque drive head in Suction mode I and up to 40 minutes with a Soft roller head in Suction mode I.

## Sony XM5 vs XM6

Page: https://www.checkedlab.com/compare/sony-wh-1000xm5-vs-xm6/

### Instagram

Sony XM5 or XM6? The music-hour line matches.

By Sony's own numbers, both list max 30 hours with noise cancelling on and max 40 hours with it off. Both charge in about 3.5 hours.

The XM5 is about 8.82 oz, with Bluetooth 5.2 and a QN1 processor with 8 microphones. The XM6 is about 8.96 oz, with Bluetooth 5.3 and a QN3 processor with 12 microphones.

Call time with noise cancelling off is max 32 hours on the XM5 and max 28 hours on the XM6. The XM6 codec list adds LC3.

## KitchenAid Artisan vs Classic

### Instagram

KitchenAid Artisan or Classic? A larger bowl and a stronger motor.

By KitchenAid's own numbers, the Artisan is a 5 quart mixer at 325 watts. The Classic is a 4.5 quart mixer at 275 watts.

## Mr Heater Buddy vs Big Buddy

Page: https://www.checkedlab.com/compare/mr-heater-buddy-vs-big-buddy/

### Instagram

Buddy or Big Buddy? One bottle, or two.

By Mr. Heater's own numbers, the Portable Buddy MH9BX is 4,000 or 9,000 BTU per hour, up to 225 sq ft, on one 1 lb propane cylinder. The Big Buddy MH18B is 4,000, 9,000, or 18,000 BTU per hour, up to 450 sq ft, on two 1 lb propane cylinders.

The Buddy settings are hi and low. The Big Buddy settings are hi, med, and low. Both standard listings mark indoor safe as yes, and both list Massachusetts and Canada restrictions. The separate Massachusetts and Canada Big Buddy listing marks indoor safe as no.

The Buddy page says 3 hours at the maximum BTU and 6 hours at the minimum. A bullet on that page also says up to 5.4 hours. The Big Buddy page says up to 11 hours with dual tanks. Elevation is 7000 ft on the Buddy and 7,000 feet on the Big Buddy. Both list a tip-over shutoff and an oxygen depletion sensor.

https://www.checkedlab.com/compare/mr-heater-buddy-vs-big-buddy/
