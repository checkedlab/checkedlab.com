How to read this

Play time and ingress rating as the Apple and Shokz pages state them for AirPods Pro 3 and the OpenRun Pro 2, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-20 (UTC).

The AirPods Pro page states up to 8 hours of listening with Active Noise Cancellation and an IP57 rating. The OpenRun Pro 2 page states up to 12 hours of play time and an IP55 rating.

A longer stated play line is the OpenRun Pro 2 line. The rating row is IP57 on the Apple page and IP55 on the Shokz page. Hearing Aid listening time and a heart-rate sensor are on the AirPods card only. Quick charge and drivers are on the Shokz card only.
