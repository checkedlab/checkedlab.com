Five lines on the E310 card

The Vitamix E310 page states a 2 HP motor, a 48 oz container, dimensions of 9 × 8 × 18 in, a weight of 10.5 lb, and a 5-year full warranty. Worth matching means those lines describe the counter and the jar you want. A 2 HP line is a motor claim from Vitamix. An 18 in height is the cabinet line to check against the space under a cabinet.

The nearest motor appliance on the kitchen list that shares a motor label and a dimensions label is the Cuisinart Custom 14-Cup DFP-14BCNY. That page states a 14 cup bowl, a 720 W motor, dimensions of 11 in L x 7.9 in W x 14.8 in H, a 5-year motor warranty, and a 3-year limited warranty on the whole unit. A blender jar in ounces and a food-processor bowl in cups are different tools. The shared labels are only a reason to notice the difference, not a reason to treat them as the same machine.

Pick the blender line or stop for the recall

Pick the E310 if you are matching a blender, the motor line you want is 2 HP, the jar line is 48 oz, and an 18 in height at 10.5 lb fits the counter. The warranty line on that page is a 5-year full warranty. Pick it for those sentences.

Do not pick the DFP-14BCNY as a second E310. Pick that card only if you are matching a 14 cup food processor with a 720 W motor line and a lower stated height of 14.8 in. The US catalogue line for DFP-14BCNY is a recall match. The notice linked from that model page is the Conair Cuisinart food-processor recall. Read the model numbers on the notice before buying that processor. The Canadian line on the card is a separate check.

A wattage and a horsepower line are not the same unit. This page does not convert 2 HP into watts or 720 W into horsepower. The recall block below repeats the catalogue status for each model.
