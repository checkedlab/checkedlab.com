Questions

What do these guides cover?

Four holiday checks: button and coin batteries, high-powered magnet sets, water beads, and ride-ons and scooters. Each guide says what to read on the product, where to search a recall, and which official page states the rule.

Where is the official recall search?

CPSC Recalls is the United States search. Health Canada recalls and safety alerts is the Canada search. Search the brand and the model number printed on the toy or the box.

Does a guide assign a pass to a toy?

No. A guide quotes an official page and links it. Gifts for kids names mainstream product lines and the check to do. A catalogue card is a separate dated match for a model that is in the catalogue.
