One other self-cleaning box is on the list

The Litter-Robot 4 page states 15 V DC, a height of 29.5 in, a footprint of 22 × 27 in, an entryway of 15.75 × 15.75 in, a weight of 24 lb empty, cleaning of up to 8 days before emptying the waste drawer in a 1-cat household, and suitability for up to 4 cats. That is the full card.

The other self-cleaning litter box in the catalogue is the ScoopFree Crystal Plus. Its page states up to 30 days of cleaning, 99% dust free crystal litter, dimensions of 28.2 x 20.4 x 6.6 in, an odor line, plug-in power, and single or multi-cat households. There is not a third automatic box on this list. A fountain, a feeder, or a harness is not an alternative to a litter box.

Pick by height, interval, or litter type

Pick the Litter-Robot 4 if the interval you are matching is up to 8 days before emptying the waste drawer in a 1-cat household, the household line is up to 4 cats, and the cabinet you can place is 29.5 in tall with a 22 × 27 in footprint and a 15.75 in entry. The power line is 15 V DC. The empty weight is 24 lb.

Pick the ScoopFree Crystal Plus if the cleaning line you are matching is up to 30 days, the height you can place is 6.6 in, and the litter line names crystal litter. The dimensions are 28.2 x 20.4 x 6.6 in, a lower stated height and a wider stated length than the Litter-Robot footprint. The power line says plug-in. The household line says single or multi-cat households, without a cat-count cap on that card.

The two cleaning lines are not the same chore. One names days before you empty a waste drawer for one cat. The other names days of cleaning and pairs that with a crystal-litter line. A longer stated interval is the ScoopFree line. A stated cat cap of 4 is the Litter-Robot line. The odor sentence lives only on the ScoopFree card. Read it there instead of treating it as a shared row.

The recall block is the catalogue match for each box. Neither card is a US or Canadian recall match on the check date printed below. Confirm the model name on the box against the name on the card before you buy.
