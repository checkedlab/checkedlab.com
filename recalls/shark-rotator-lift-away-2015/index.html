What the notices say

CPSC names NV650W, NV651, NV652, and NV660. Health Canada names NV650W and NV652. AV2501S, AV2820AE, and IP3251 are not those models. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-13 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2015-04-22

Shark Vacuum Cleaners Recalled by Euro-Pro

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves four models of Shark Rotator Powered Lift-Away upright vacuum cleaners: NV650W, NV651, NV652, and NV660. It says the model number is on a silver sticker on the upper right-hand corner of the back of the unit. It says recalled units have a date code on the power-plug prong beginning with 48 or lower and ending with 14, in WWYY form. It says units marked with a dimple or a green circle sticker on the plastic cuff that holds the aluminum wand were inspected and are not included. It describes an electric-shock hazard if the aluminum wand disconnects and stays energized.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • NV650W
  • NV651
  • NV652
  • NV660

How to check your model

The notice says to read the model sticker on the back, the date code on the plug prong, and whether the cuff has a dimple or a green circle sticker.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled vacuum cleaners and contact Euro-Pro for a free replacement wand.

Health Canada · 2015-04-22

Euro-Pro Operating LLC recalls Shark brand vacuum cleaners

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says the recall involves certain Shark Rotator Powered Lift-Away upright vacuum cleaners and names models NV650W and NV652. It says the model number is on a silver sticker on the upper right-hand corner of the back. It uses the same date-code rule, codes beginning with 48 or lower and ending with 14, and the same dimple or green-circle exclusion. It is a joint recall with the CPSC. The CPSC notice also names NV651 and NV660, which this Health Canada model table does not list.

Model numbers in this Health Canada notice

  • NV650W
  • NV652

How to check your model

The notice says to read the model sticker, the date code on the plug prong, and the cuff mark.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled vacuum cleaners and contact Euro-Pro for a free replacement wand.
