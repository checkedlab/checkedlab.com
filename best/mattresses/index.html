How we picked

Checked Lab lines up maker pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The label uses the construction line the maker page prints: foam height, coil count, or a stated trial. Feel names such as Plush Soft, Luxury Firm, or Firm are the names on the Saatva page. They are quoted as printed.

Queen dimensions are compared only when both pages state a queen size. The Casper page states 60 in W x 80 in L x 11 in H. The Helix page states 60 in W x 80 in L x 11.5 in H. The Saatva page states height as 11.5 in or 14.5 in and does not print that width-and-length line in this spec list.

Casper Original Foam Mattress

The Casper Original page states a queen size of 60 in W x 80 in L x 11 in H, a queen weight of 79 lb, and a core of zoned memory foam. The top line is breathable polyurethane foam. The label matches the 11 in queen height.

Saatva Classic Mattress

The Saatva Classic page states 1,300 total queen coils, a height of 11.5 in or 14.5 in, and feels of Plush Soft, Luxury Firm, or Firm. Comfort coil gauge is 14.5. The label matches the coil count.

Zinus 12-Inch Green Tea Memory Foam Mattress

The Zinus page states a height of 12 inch, gel foam of 2.5 in cooling gel memory foam, and a certification of CertiPUR-US foams. Extra foam on that page is 1/2 in memory foam. The label matches the 12 inch height.

Helix Midnight Mattress

The Helix Midnight page states a trial of 365 nights, a queen size of 60 in W x 80 in L x 11.5 in H, and a warranty of limited lifetime. The label matches the trial line on that page.

What to look for

  1. Read height with the size it belongs to. Casper queen height is 11 in. Helix queen height is 11.5 in. Zinus height is 12 inch. Saatva height is 11.5 in or 14.5 in.
  2. Read the construction noun. Casper states a breathable polyurethane foam top and a zoned memory foam core. Zinus states 2.5 in cooling gel memory foam and CertiPUR-US foams. Saatva states 1,300 total queen coils. Helix states a queen size and a limited lifetime warranty.
  3. Read a trial or warranty line only on the card that prints it. The Helix page states a trial of 365 nights and a limited lifetime warranty. The other three cards on this page do not print that trial line.
  4. Read weight when you need to move the mattress. The Casper page states a queen weight of 79 lb. The other three cards on this page do not print a queen weight in this spec list.

Questions

Which mattress card states 1,300 coils?

The Saatva Classic card. The page states 1,300 total queen coils, a comfort coil gauge of 14.5, and a height of 11.5 in or 14.5 in.

Which mattress card states a 12 inch height?

The Zinus card. The page states a height of 12 inch, 2.5 in cooling gel memory foam, and CertiPUR-US foams.

Which mattress card states a 365 night trial?

The Helix Midnight card. The page states a trial of 365 nights, a limited lifetime warranty, and a queen size of 60 in W x 80 in L x 11.5 in H.

Where does the mattress recall line come from?

The US line is the CPSC SaferProducts match for that model. The Canadian line is the Health Canada open dataset match. The date on the card is the day the match was recorded.
