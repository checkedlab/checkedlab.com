What to confirm before you pay

Black Friday week and Cyber Monday 2026, which falls on Monday, November 30, are when a listing and a model number drift apart. The brand in the title can be right while the model in the cart is a different card. Read the model number on the unit, on the box, and in the listing until those three strings match.

Then open that model's card. The status under the card is the recall check. A store listing is not a recall check. How we check says what the printed date means, and recall checks lists every model for the United States and for Canada.

Amazon has published the 2026 Black Friday Week dates for Amazon.com and for Amazon.ca. The dated lines higher on this page quote those store pages, one on the US view and one on the Canada view. The shopping window is not the same string in both countries.

Use the line that matches the region switch. The buttons under those lines open the store's own Black Friday page with the Checked Lab associate tag for that country. Cyber Monday does not change the model check: match the number on the unit, the box, and the listing, then read what is in the box and the return window on that store page.

Family names hide model numbers. Foodi covers more than one Ninja oven. Cosori covers more than one air fryer.

The Cosori air fryer recall and the Ninja Foodi OP300 notices quote the official notices and name the model cards inside those lists. The air fryer recall list is the brand table. The Crispi and Cosori Pro II comparison repeats only the dimensions and weight both product pages share.

Power, containers, and modes stay on the Crispi card. Capacity and rated power stay on the Pro II card. A spec that only one page prints is left out of the table on purpose.

Look-alike listings and third-party sellers can put a familiar brand in the title and ship a different model. Match the model number before you pay. Then read the return window on that store page.

The window is printed there, and it can change by seller, so this site does not restate it. Gift guides regroup the same cards by recipient if you are buying for a person rather than filling a category. Gifts for kids and toy safety are the pages for children's products. Winter safety is the page for heaters, heated bedding, alarms, and outage power.

The recall step is the same one used on every hub. For a US purchase, search the model on CPSC Recalls. The recall check for that search is the CPSC SaferProducts feed.

For a Canadian purchase, search Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. The Canadian recall check uses Health Canada's open dataset. A clear line on one model does not cover the next model in the brand.

Official recall found means the brand and the model token both appear in one notice, and that notice is linked from the card.

The hubs below are the gift and seasonal models already in the list. If you want shared specs for a pair, use the comparison index. Lasko CT32150 and CT14401 both state 900 W to 1,500 W and 120 V, from the Lasko pages, and they state different heights. NOCO GB40 and GB50 state different peak current and different engine-size limits. Confirm the exact model number on the box and on the listing. Check that model with CPSC for a US purchase and with Health Canada for a Canadian purchase. A look-alike listing or a third-party seller can use the brand name for a different model. Read what is in the box, and read the return window on the store page before you pay.
