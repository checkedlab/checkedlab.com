What the XM5 card states

The Sony card states model WH-1000XM5, battery life of Max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on, max. 40 hours with it off, noise cancelling described as 2 processors control 8 microphones, connection of Multipoint, a weight of Approx. 8.82 oz, and a charge time of Approx. 3.5 hours. An alternative on this page is another personal-tech card with a battery or play-time line you can read next to those sentences. Two of them are not over-ear headband headphones.

The QuietComfort Ultra page states battery of Up to 24 hours, modes of Quiet, Aware, and Immersion, Bluetooth of Multipoint, two devices, audio of Bose Immersive Audio, a weight of 0.56 lb, and a charge time of 3 hours. The AirPods Pro 3 page states listening of Up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, transparency of Up to 10 hours with the Hearing Aid feature, a sensor line of Heart rate, a hearing line of Hearing Aid feature, play time of Up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, and a rating of IP57. The OpenRun Pro 2 page states play time of Up to 12 hours, a quick-charge line of 5 minutes for about 150 minutes at 75% volume, drivers of Dual, and a rating of IP55.

Pick the battery sentence, then the form

Pick the WH-1000XM5 if the listing shows that model and you are matching Max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on, max. 40 hours with it off, a weight of Approx. 8.82 oz, and a charge time of Approx. 3.5 hours. The microphone sentence is 2 processors control 8 microphones. The connection line says multipoint. A listing that says Sony headphones, or 1000X, still has to show WH-1000XM5 if this is the card.

Pick the QuietComfort Ultra if you are matching Up to 24 hours, a charge time of 3 hours, and modes named Quiet, Aware, and Immersion. The weight on that page is 0.56 lb. The Sony weight is in ounces. This page does not convert ounces into pounds. A shorter stated charge time is the Bose line. A longer stated playback line with noise cancelling on is the Sony line. Multipoint is on both cards, and the Bose card adds two devices.

Pick the AirPods Pro 3 if you are matching in-ear listening of Up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, the same Up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation play-time line, a rating of IP57, and the hearing line that says Hearing Aid feature. The transparency line is Up to 10 hours with the Hearing Aid feature. The sensor line says Heart rate. Those sentences are not on the WH-1000XM5 card. Eight hours and thirty hours are different playback lines.

Pick the OpenRun Pro 2 if you are matching open-ear play time of Up to 12 hours, a rating of IP55, and the quick-charge sentence 5 minutes for about 150 minutes at 75% volume. The driver line says Dual. That card is not a headband headphone and it is not an in-ear bud. A 12 hour line sits between the AirPods play-time line and the Sony noise-cancelling line, and it is still its own sentence.

Do not collapse the form factor

WH-1000XM5 and QuietComfort Ultra are over-ear cards with weight and charge lines. AirPods Pro 3 is an in-ear card with a listening line, a hearing line, and IP57. OpenRun Pro 2 is an open-ear card with IP55 and a quick-charge line. A shared idea of battery life is not a shared label. Read the hours on the page for the form you are actually buying.

The recall block is the catalogue match for each of the four cards. Confirm the model printed on the box. WH-1000XM5, QuietComfort Ultra, AirPods Pro 3, and OpenRun Pro 2 are four model strings. The comparison page for Sony and Bose repeats only the labels both of those cards share.
