Questions

What do these guides cover?

Four winter checks: space heaters, electric blankets and heated throws, carbon monoxide and smoke alarms, and portable power stations and generators. Each guide says what to read on the label, where to search a recall, and which official notices show a pattern.

Where is the official recall search?

CPSC Recalls is the United States search. Health Canada recalls and safety alerts is the Canada search. Search the brand and the model number printed on the unit.

Does a guide replace a catalogue card?

No. A catalogue card is the dated CPSC and Health Canada match for that model. A guide links the card when the model is in the catalogue, and it links a notice when that notice names a different model.
