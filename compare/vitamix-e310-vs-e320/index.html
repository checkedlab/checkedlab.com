Vitamix E310 vs E320: which one to buy

The E310 is a 48 oz jar with a 5-year full warranty, and the E320 is a 64 oz jar with a 7-year limited warranty.

Get the E310 if

  • You blend for one or two and want a smaller 48 oz jar.
  • You want the 2 HP motor.
  • You want the 5-year full warranty.

How to read this

The E310 is a 48 oz jar with a 5-year full warranty, and the E320 is a 64 oz jar with a 7-year limited warranty.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-13 (UTC).

Both are Explorian household blenders. The E310 card says 2 HP, a 48 oz container, 9 × 8 × 18 in, 10.5 lb, and a 5-year full warranty. The E320 product page says 2.2 HP, a 64-ounce container, 9 x 8 x 18 in, 10.5 lbs, and a 7-year limited warranty.

The weight lines match. The motor, the container, and the warranty wording do not. The E320 owner’s manual prints a different height, width, and depth from the product page. Both links are on this page.

What actually changes between the two Explorians

The E320 page is a current Vitamix product page, with colorways listed for sale. It describes a low-profile 64-ounce container, ten variable speeds, a pulse feature, and a self-clean with dish soap and warm water in 30 to 60 seconds. The E310 card already records a 48 oz container and a self-clean in that same 30 to 60 second wording. The motor line is the clean split: 2 HP on the E310 card, 2.2 HP on the E320 page.

Dimensions on the E320 product page are 9 x 8 x 18 in, the same inches the E310 card prints. The E320 domestic manual, revision B, prints a height of 17 inches with the 64 oz container on the base, a width of 8 inches, and a depth of 11 inches. This table uses the product-page dimensions because that is the line next to the 2.2 HP and 64-ounce figures. The manual line is listed under the table so the disagreement stays visible. Vitamix has not, on these two pages, said which measuring method produced which set.

Warranty words are not interchangeable

The E310 card says a 5-year full warranty. The E320 page says a 7-year limited warranty and points readers at the warranty text. Full and limited are the words Vitamix printed. A longer year count on a limited warranty is not the same sentence as a shorter full warranty. Open the warranty link on the machine you are ordering.

The E320 page also lists a low-profile tamper in the box with the motor base and the 64-ounce container, and it says household use. There is a separate E320 plus Personal Cup Adapter page, with a different weight and a split warranty. This comparison is the E320 blender page, not that bundle.

Pick the container you will actually fill

Pick the E310 if the container you are matching is 48 oz, the motor line is 2 HP, and the warranty line is the 5-year full warranty. The catalogue card is that machine. The 2024 container notice linked from the recall page names Ascent and Venturist 8-ounce and 20-ounce containers, not the Explorian 48 oz container. Read that notice before you assume it covers the E310.

Pick the E320 if the container you are matching is 64 ounces, the motor line is 2.2 HP, and the warranty line is the 7-year limited warranty. The product page and the manual disagree on the cabinet inches. Measure the shelf against the line you trust, and keep the other line in view. Ten speeds and pulse are on the E320 page. The E310 evidence line says 10 variable speeds as well, so speed count is not the split.

Both pages print 10.5 lb. If weight is the only line you care about, these two product pages do not separate the machines.
