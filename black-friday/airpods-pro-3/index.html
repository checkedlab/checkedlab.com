AirPods Pro 3 listening lines

The AirPods Pro 3 page states listening of Up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, transparency of Up to 10 hours with the Hearing Aid feature, a sensor of Heart rate, hearing of Hearing Aid feature, play time of Up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, and a rating of IP57. A listing that says AirPods, or Pro, still has to match these lines if this is the card you mean. The listening line and the play-time line both say up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation. The transparency line is a different hour count and names the Hearing Aid feature.

The WH-1000XM5 card states battery of Max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on, max. 40 hours with it off, a weight of Approx. 8.82 oz, a charge time of Approx. 3.5 hours, and noise cancelling of 2 processors control 8 microphones. The OpenRun Pro 2 card states play time of Up to 12 hours, a quick charge of 5 minutes for about 150 minutes at 75% volume, and a rating of IP55. One is an over-ear headphone. One is an open-ear headphone. The AirPods card is the in-ear card.

Pick the hour line that matches the form

Pick the AirPods Pro 3 if the listing is that model and you are matching up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, IP57, and the hearing line that says Hearing Aid feature. The sensor line says heart rate. The transparency line says up to 10 hours with the Hearing Aid feature. Those are Apple’s sentences on this card. A season name in the listing title does not change the model.

Pick the WH-1000XM5 if the listing shows WH-1000XM5 and you are matching max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on, or max. 40 hours with it off, at about 8.82 oz, with a charge time of about 3.5 hours. That is a headband headphone. It is not an in-ear bud, and it does not print IP57. Eight hours and thirty hours are different playback lines.

Pick the OpenRun Pro 2 if the listing is that model and you are matching up to 12 hours, IP55, and the quick-charge sentence of 5 minutes for about 150 minutes at 75% volume. The driver line says dual. That card does not print a noise-cancelling hour line and it does not print IP57. IP55 and IP57 are different rating lines. This page does not treat them as the same code.

What to verify on the listing

Match the generation and the form before the accessory list. AirPods Pro 3 on this card is the listening line of up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation, the IP57 line, and the Hearing Aid feature line. WH-1000XM5 is the over-ear battery line. OpenRun Pro 2 is the open-ear 12 hour line. A case-only listing, or a listing for an earlier Pro generation, is a different card. The comparison with AirPods Pro 2 is the page that lines those two Apple cards up.

The recall block is the catalogue match for each of the three. Confirm the name on the box. Search that name on CPSC Recalls for a US purchase and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts for a Canadian purchase. The dated line on the card is the CPSC and Health Canada check. A listing title is not that check.
