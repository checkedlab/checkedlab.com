Litter-Robot 4 vs Litter-Robot 5: which one to buy

The Litter-Robot 4 is the lighter one, for up to 4 cats, and the 5 is for up to 5 cats.

Get the Litter-Robot 4 if

  • You want the lighter unit, at 24 lb empty.
  • You have up to 4 cats.
  • Up to 8 days before emptying the drawer, for one cat, is enough.
See the model

Get the Litter-Robot 5 if

  • You have up to 5 cats.
  • You want up to 10 days before emptying the drawer for one cat.
  • You can fit 29.9 in of height and 28.7 lb empty, or the manual figure of 29.50 in and 28.7–29.6 lb.

How to read this

The Litter-Robot 4 is the lighter one, for up to 4 cats, and the 5 is for up to 5 cats.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-14 (UTC).

Whisker sells Litter-Robot 5 for shipment in the United States and Canada. The sales page says 15 volt DC, 29.9 in tall, a 22 by 27 in footprint, a 15.75 in entry, 28.7 lb empty, up to 5 cats, and up to 10 days before emptying the drawer for one cat.

The Litter-Robot 4 card says 15 V DC, 29.5 in, the same 22 by 27 in footprint, the same 15.75 in entry, 24 lb empty, up to 4 cats, and up to 8 days for one cat. Power, footprint, and entry match. Height, weight, cats, and days do not.

The sales page and the manual do not print one height

On the Litter-Robot 5 product page the dimension table says height 29.9 inches and empty weight 28.7 lb, with 17.5 inches of inside headroom. The Litter-Robot 5 manual’s fully assembled row says 29.50 inches high, 22.00 inches wide, and 27.25 inches deep, and a weight of 28.7 to 29.6 lb. The table on this page uses the sales-page height and the 28.7 lb figure. The manual’s range is the other official line. A 29.9 in shelf measurement and a 29.50 in manual measurement are both Whisker’s, and they are not the same number.

Litter-Robot 4’s card says 29.5 in and 24 lb empty. Against the Litter-Robot 5 sales page, that is a shorter stated height and a lower stated empty weight. Against the manual’s 29.50 in, the 4’s 29.5 in height matches the manual and not the 29.9 in sales line. Quote the document you are using.

Cats and days are the lines that move

Household size is up to 4 cats on the Litter-Robot 4 card and up to 5 cats on the Litter-Robot 5 page. The waste-drawer line is up to 8 days for one cat on the 4, and up to 10 days for one cat on the 5. Whisker’s own FAQ on the 5 page says how often you empty the drawer depends on the number and size of the cats, and that you may only need to empty it as low as once every 10 days. The 10-day sentence is an upper figure for a one-cat household, not a schedule.

The 5 page also says the robot needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi signal, and that it ships within the United States and Canada. The 4 card does not use a Wi-Fi label, so that line is left out. Litter-Robot 5 Pro is a different product on Whisker’s compare chart, with cameras. This page is the Litter-Robot 5, not the Pro.

Pick the weight and the cat count you can point at

Pick the Litter-Robot 4 if the empty weight you are matching is 24 lb, the cat line is up to 4, and the drawer line is up to 8 days for one cat. Height on that card is 29.5 in. Power is 15 V DC. The footprint and the entry match the 5’s sales page.

Pick the Litter-Robot 5 if the cat line you are matching is up to 5 and the drawer line is up to 10 days for one cat, and you have chosen which height to plan around: 29.9 in on the sales page or 29.50 in in the manual. Empty weight on the sales page is 28.7 lb. The manual allows 28.7 to 29.6 lb. There is no separate model page here for the 5.

The PetSafe ScoopFree comparison uses the Litter-Robot 4 card. It does not describe the 5. Open that page only when the 4 is the machine you are weighing against a ScoopFree.
