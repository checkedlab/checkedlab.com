How to read this

Robot size, dock size, and battery as the iRobot and Roborock pages state them for the Combo j9+ and the Qrevo S, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-13 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Combo j9+ page states robot dimensions of 3.4 x 13.7 x 13.7 in, dock dimensions of 15.8 in W x 16.4 in D x 16.3 in H, and a lithium-ion battery. The Qrevo S page states robot dimensions of 13.78 x 13.9 x 3.80 in, dock dimensions of 13.38 x 19.17 x 20.51 in, and a 14.4 V/5200 mAh lithium-ion battery.

The battery line on the Roomba page names the chemistry. The battery line on the Roborock support sheet names voltage and capacity. Robot weight and dock weight are on the Roomba card only. Suction, mop speed, and charge time are on the Qrevo S card only.
