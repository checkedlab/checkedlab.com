Black Friday week is when a listing and a model number drift apart. The brand in the title can be right while the model in the cart is a different card. Read the model number on the unit, on the box, and in the listing until those three strings match.

Then open that model's card. The status under the card is the catalogue check. A store listing is not a recall check. How we check says what the printed date means, and recall checks lists every catalogue model for the United States and for Canada.

Amazon has published the 2026 Black Friday Week dates for Amazon.com and for Amazon.ca. The dated lines higher on this page quote those store pages, one on the US view and one on the Canada view. The shopping window is not the same string in both countries.

Use the line that matches the region switch. The buttons under those lines open the store's own Black Friday page with the Checked Lab associate tag for that country.

Family names hide model numbers. Foodi covers more than one Ninja oven. Cosori covers more than one air fryer.

The Cosori 2023 model list and the Ninja Foodi OP300 notices quote the official notices and name the catalogue cards inside those lists. The Crispi and Cosori Pro II comparison repeats only the dimensions and weight both maker pages share. Power, containers, and modes stay on the Crispi card.

Capacity and rated power stay on the Pro II card. A spec that only one page prints is left out of the table on purpose.

Look-alike listings and third-party sellers can put a familiar brand in the title and ship a different model. Match the model number before you pay. Then read the return window on that store page.

The window is printed there, and it can change by seller, so this catalogue does not restate it. Gift guides regroup the same cards by recipient if you are buying for a person rather than filling a category. Gifts for kids and toy safety are the pages for children's products. Winter safety is the page for heaters, heated bedding, alarms, and outage power.

The recall step is the same one used on every hub. For a US purchase, search the model on CPSC Recalls. The catalogue match for that search is the CPSC SaferProducts feed.

For a Canadian purchase, search Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. The Canadian catalogue match uses Health Canada's open dataset. A clear line on one model does not cover the next model in the brand.

Official recall found means the brand and the model token both appear in one notice, and that notice is linked from the card.

Notices to open before you buy

These pages quote the official notice and name the catalogue card.

  • Cosori air fryer recall: the 2023 model list and the Pro II. The February 23, 2023 CPSC and Health Canada notices name specific Cosori air fryer model numbers. The COSORI Pro II card uses the CS358-AF series, which is not in those lists.
  • Ninja Foodi OP300 and TenderCrisp lid notices. The May 1, 2025 CPSC notice names Ninja Foodi OP300-series pressure cookers. The Health Canada notice names TenderCrisp pressure-cooking lids. The DT201C oven and FN101 Crispi cards are not those model numbers.
  • Anker power bank recalls: official model numbers. CPSC and Health Canada power-bank notices name specific Anker model numbers, and some of those notices cover only certain serial numbers. A1289 is not one of those model numbers.
  • Vitamix Ascent and Venturist container recall, and the E310. The June 20, 2024 CPSC notice covers Ascent and Venturist 8-ounce and 20-ounce blending containers and blade bases. The E310 card lists an Explorian blender with a 48 oz container.
  • Instant Pot official notices: Smart, Smart-60, and Gem 65. Official CPSC and Health Canada notices name Instant Pot Smart, Smart-60, and Gem 65 multicookers. They do not name the Duo Plus. The Duo Plus card has no matching recall record as of the catalogue check date.
  • Lasko heater recalls: official model numbers. CPSC heater notices name specific older Lasko and Air King model numbers. CT32150 and CT14401 are not those model numbers. The catalogue check found no matching recall for either card on the check date.
  • Toro Power Clear snowblower recalls: the model numbers. The 2010 and 2011 CPSC and Health Canada notices name Power Clear 180 models 38272 and 38282, and PC-421 models 38588 and 38589. Catalogue model 38753 is not in those lists.
  • Shark Rotator Lift-Away recall: the 2015 model numbers. CPSC names NV650W, NV651, NV652, and NV660. Health Canada names NV650W and NV652. AV2501S, AV2820AE, and IP3251 are not those models.

Questions

What should I check before buying on Black Friday?

Confirm the exact model number on the box and on the listing. Check that model with CPSC for a US purchase and with Health Canada for a Canadian purchase. A look-alike listing or a third-party seller can use the brand name for a different model. Read the return window on the store page before you pay.

When is Black Friday 2026 on Amazon?

Amazon announced that Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deals on Amazon.com run November 19–30, 2026. Amazon.ca’s Black Friday page, titled 2026 Black Friday, describes Black Friday Week as November 19–27.

What should an air fryer shopper match before buying?

Match the model number on the unit to the listing. The Cosori 2023 notices and the Ninja Foodi OP300 notices name specific models. The catalogue cards for the COSORI Pro II, the Ninja Foodi 10-in-1 oven, and the Ninja Crispi link those explainers.

Does a Black Friday listing check the recall?

A sale listing is not a recall check. Each model on this page repeats the catalogue status and date from the CPSC and Health Canada match. A notice for another model in the same brand can still exist.
