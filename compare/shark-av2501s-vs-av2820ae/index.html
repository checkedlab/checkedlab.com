How to read this

Weight, cleaning path, dimensions, voltage, and wattage as the Shark pages state them for the AV2501S and the AV2820AE, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-21 (UTC).

The AV2501S page states 14.77 lbs, a 5.7 in cleaning path, 15.35 in L x 12.01 in W x 17.72 in H, 120 volts, and 650 watts. The AV2820AE page states 14.55 lbs, a 5.7 in cleaning path, 15.39 in L x 10.87 in W x 16.93 in H, 14.4 volts, and 35 watts.

A lower stated weight is the AV2820AE line. The voltage and wattage rows are the figures each Shark page prints for that model. Dust cup capacity is on the AV2501S card only.
