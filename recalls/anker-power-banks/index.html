What the notices say

CPSC and Health Canada power-bank notices name specific Anker model numbers, and some of those notices cover only certain serial numbers. A1289 is not one of those model numbers. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-13 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2025-06-12

Anker Power Banks Recalled Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Anker PowerCore 10000 power banks with model number A1263. It says Anker is engraved on the front, and the model number and serial number are printed on the bottom. It says only A1263 units sold in the U.S. with qualifying serial numbers are included.

Model number in this CPSC notice

  • A1263

How to check your model

The notice says to read model A1263 and the serial number on the bottom, then check that serial number at anker.com/product-recalls.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using a recalled power bank and contact Anker for a free replacement. It says the registration asks for a photo showing the model number, serial number, the owner’s name, the date, and the word recalled written on the unit. It also says not to put the lithium-ion battery in household trash or ordinary recycling.

CPSC · 2024-10-17

Anker Power Banks Recalled Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Anker power banks with model numbers A1642, A1647, and A1652. It says Anker is engraved on the front and the model number is on the back.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • A1642
  • A1647
  • A1652

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the back of the power bank.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled power banks and contact Anker for a free replacement, registering at anker.com/product-recalls. It says to dispose of the lithium-ion batteries under local rules.

CPSC · 2023-03-09

Fantasia Trading Recalls Anker Power Banks Due to Fire Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Anker 535 mobile power banks, PowerCore 20K, with model number A1366. It says Anker is engraved on the front and A1366 is on the back.

Model number in this CPSC notice

  • A1366

How to check your model

The notice says the model number A1366 is on the back of the power bank.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled power banks and contact Fantasia Trading for a full refund and for its instructions on disposing of the lithium-ion batteries.

Health Canada · 2025-06-27

Anker Power Bank A1647 and Anker Zolo Power Banks A1681 and A1689 recalled due to fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says certain units of Anker Power Bank A1647 and Anker Zolo power banks A1681 and A1689 are recalled for a fire hazard. It says affected units are identified by serial number, and that serial numbers of affected products do not contain the letters O or I.

Model numbers in this Health Canada notice

  • A1647
  • A1681
  • A1689

How to check your model

The notice says the serial number is printed on the side or the back, and to compare it with the list at anker.com/ca/rc2506.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled units and contact Anker for a free replacement or a voucher. It says to dispose of the lithium-ion battery so it cannot be used, using the municipality’s instructions, and not to put it in household garbage.

Health Canada · 2024-09-24

Anker 334 MagGo Battery (PowerCore 10K), model A1642, recalled due to potential fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice names the Anker 334 MagGo Battery, PowerCore 10K, model A1642, and says to use Anker’s site to see whether a serial number is included.

Model number in this Health Canada notice

  • A1642

How to check your model

The notice says to check the serial number on Anker’s website.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the product, check the serial number, and if the unit is included, dispose of it so it cannot be used again, following the municipality’s lithium-ion battery instructions.

Health Canada · 2024-07-05

Anker 321 Power Banks (PowerCore 5K) recalled due to potential fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice says certain units of the Anker 321 Power Bank, PowerCore 5K, black, model A1112011, are identified by specific 16-digit serial numbers on the bottom.

Model number in this Health Canada notice

  • A1112011

How to check your model

The notice says the serial number is on the bottom and to check it with Anker at anker.com/ca/a1112-recall-form.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using a recalled unit, dispose of it so it cannot be used, follow municipal lithium-ion battery instructions, and not put it in household garbage. It says Anker sends a new power bank to users verified as part of the recall batch.

Health Canada · 2023-02-23

Anker 535 Power Bank (PowerCore 20K) A1366 recalled due to fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice names the Anker 535 Power Bank, PowerCore 20K, model A1366, and describes a fire hazard.

Model number in this Health Canada notice

  • A1366

How to check your model

The notice names model A1366. Use the linked notice for the label details it prints.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled power bank and contact Anker customer support for a refund.
