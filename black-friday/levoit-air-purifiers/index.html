What the maker says

Core 300S-P, Core 600S-P, and Vital 200S-P are three different purifiers. Noise, cabinet size, and room figures are not the same, and the Classic 300S is a humidifier.

Three purifier cards, three room sentences

A listing that says Levoit, or 300S, can be more than one card. The Core 300S-P manual states a power supply of AC 120 V, 60 Hz, rated power of 39 W, noise of 22–54.5 dB, dimensions of 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in, and a weight of 7.48 lb. That card does not print a CADR label or a room-size label.

The Core 600S-P page states a room size of 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour, or 606 sq ft at 4.8, noise of 26–55 dB, filtration of 3-stage, CADR of 391 CFM, a weight of 13.7 lb / 6.2 kg, a size of 12.3 x 12.3 x 23.6 in / 31.3 x 31.3 x 60 cm, and power of 49 W, AC 120 V, 60 Hz. The Vital 200S-P page states filtration of 3-stage, a room rate of Changes the air in a 1,862 sq ft room once per hour, noise of As low as 23 dB, a sensor of Infrared dust sensor, and a warranty of 2-year limited. The Classic 300S card in the winter list is a humidifier, with a tank line, not one of these three purifiers.

Pick the cabinet, the CFM line, or the room-rate line

Pick the Core 300S-P if the listing shows that model and you are matching a cabinet of 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 in at 7.48 lb, with rated power of 39 W and noise of 22–54.5 dB. Pick it when you do not need a CADR figure from the same page, because this card does not have one. A listing that only says 300S still has to show Core 300S-P if this is the purifier.

Pick the Core 600S-P if you are matching CADR of 391 CFM or the room line 2,909 sq ft at 1 air change per hour, or 606 sq ft at 4.8. The cabinet is 12.3 x 12.3 x 23.6 in / 31.3 x 31.3 x 60 cm at 13.7 lb / 6.2 kg. Power is 49 W, AC 120 V, 60 Hz. Noise is 26–55 dB. The 300S-P noise line is 22–54.5 dB. Those ranges are not the same sentence. The 600S-P page prints both an air-change rate and a CFM figure. Read the sentence you are matching.

Pick the Vital 200S-P if you are matching the room-rate line Changes the air in a 1,862 sq ft room once per hour, filtration of 3-stage, noise of As low as 23 dB, and a sensor line of Infrared dust sensor. The warranty line is 2-year limited. That card does not print a CADR in CFM and it does not print the 300S-P cabinet size. A 1,862 sq ft once-per-hour line is not the 606 sq ft line at 4.8 air changes on the 600S-P page.

What to verify on the listing

Read the full model, not the word Levoit and not the digits 300 alone. Core 300S-P, Core 600S-P, and Vital 200S-P are three purifier strings. Classic 300S is a humidifier card. A filter bundle that names a different model is a different listing. The power line to confirm is 39 W on the 300S-P manual and 49 W on the 600S-P page. The Vital card’s warranty line is 2-year limited. The other two cards on this page do not print that warranty sentence.

The recall block is the catalogue match for each purifier. Search the model on CPSC Recalls and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. A season name in the listing is not a CADR figure and it is not the cabinet size. The dated line under the card is the CPSC and Health Canada check. Confirm the name on the rating label before you pay.
