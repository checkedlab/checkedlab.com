The question is which lines fit the room

Worth matching, on this page, means the maker’s lines describe the space and the chore you have. It is not a price verdict. The Litter-Robot 4 page states a height of 29.5 in, a footprint of 22 × 27 in, an entryway of 15.75 × 15.75 in, an empty weight of 24 lb, power of 15 V DC, up to 8 days before emptying the waste drawer in a 1-cat household, and up to 4 cats.

If the floor opening is smaller than 22 × 27 in, or the vertical space is under 29.5 in, the cabinet line does not fit, whatever else the page says. If the household is one cat and the chore you are matching is the waste-drawer interval, the line to read is up to 8 days. If the household line you need is a stated cap, the page says up to 4 cats.

Pick it, or pick the lower box

Pick the Litter-Robot 4 if all of these are true on the page you are reading: the height is acceptable at 29.5 in, the footprint fits at 22 × 27 in, the entry is 15.75 in square, and the interval you want is the waste-drawer line for one cat. The power supply line is 15 V DC, so the card is not describing a battery tray.

Pick the ScoopFree Crystal Plus instead if the height you can give the box is 6.6 in, the cleaning line you want is up to 30 days, and the litter line you want names crystal litter. That page also states 28.2 x 20.4 x 6.6 in, plug-in power, and single or multi-cat households. It does not state an empty weight or a cat-count cap. A short cabinet and a long interval are the ScoopFree lines. A tall cabinet and an 8-day drawer line are the Litter-Robot lines.

The two pages do not share a label, so there is no single table row that decides this. Read the height first, then the interval, then the household sentence. The recall lines below are the catalogue match. Open the model page for the feed link.
