How we picked

Checked Lab lines up maker pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The Best for line is a use: tip-over shutoff, a short tower for a small space, a stated coverage, or a low watt setting. The Vornado card states a weight and two heat settings and no room size, so it has no Best for line. Watt lines and BTU lines stay in the unit the maker printed.

Two Lasko towers state the same heat output, 900 W to 1,500 W, and different heights. The CT32150 page states 31.6 in, 5.7 lb, and a tip-over switch. The CT14401 page states 14 in and 3.3 lb. The short tower is the small-space card. The taller tower is the tip-over card.

Lasko 32 inch Digital Ceramic Tower Heater CT32150

The CT32150 page states a height of 31.6 in, heat output of 900 W to 1,500 W, and a weight of 5.7 lb. Voltage is 120 V. The safety line states ETL listed, tip-over switch, and overheat protection. The tip-over switch in that safety line is the use. The 31.6 in height is quoted with it.

Lasko Ready Heat 14 inch Ceramic Tower Heater CT14401

The CT14401 page states a height of 14 in, heat output of 900 W to 1,500 W, and a weight of 3.3 lb. Voltage is 120 V. The safety line states ETL listed and overheat protection. The 14 in height and the 3.3 lb weight are the small-space use. The watt line matches the taller Lasko.

Dreo Atom 316 Space Heater

The Atom 316 page states coverage of 200 sq ft, heat of 1500 watts, and 4 speeds. Voltage is 120 volts. The thermostat line is 41 F to 95 F. The 200 sq ft coverage sentence is the room use.

Mr. Heater Portable Buddy MH9BX

The Portable Buddy page states heat of 4,000 or 9,000 BTU per hour, an area of up to 225 sq ft, and a run time at max of 3 hours. The model line is MH9BX. The area line, up to 225 sq ft, is the space use. The 9,000 BTU setting is the high heat on that page.

Vornado AVH2 Whole Room Heater

The AVH2 page states a weight of 4.48 lb, heat settings of low and high, and dimensions of 9.21 x 11.65 x 11.89 in. Electrical is 120 V, 60 Hz. Those lines name a weight and two heat settings. They do not name a room size, a runtime, or a noise figure, so this card has no Best for line.

De'Longhi Dragon 4 Radiator TRD40615E

The Dragon 4 page states 6 fins, a heat max of 1500 W, and a weight of 25.61 lb. Heat min on that page is 700 W. Voltage is 120 V, 60 Hz. The 700 W minimum is the low-heat use. The 1,500 W maximum and the 25.61 lb weight stay on the card.

What to look for

  1. Read the heat unit before comparing numbers. The CT32150, CT14401, Atom 316, AVH2, and Dragon 4 pages state watts or heat settings on 120 V. The Portable Buddy page states 4,000 or 9,000 BTU per hour.
  2. Read a coverage line only when the card has one. The Atom 316 page states 200 sq ft. The Portable Buddy page states up to 225 sq ft. The Lasko, Vornado, and De’Longhi cards on this page do not print a square-foot line.
  3. Read the safety sentence when the maker page prints one. The CT32150 page states ETL listed, tip-over switch, and overheat protection. The CT14401 page states ETL listed and overheat protection. The space-heater guide is at Winter home safety.
  4. Read the weight if the heater has to be lifted. The CT14401 page states 3.3 lb. The AVH2 page states 4.48 lb. The Dragon 4 page states 25.61 lb and 6 fins.

Questions

Which heater card is the short tower?

The CT14401 card. The page states a height of 14 in, a weight of 3.3 lb, and heat output of 900 W to 1,500 W.

Which heater card states BTU?

The Portable Buddy MH9BX card. The page states 4,000 or 9,000 BTU per hour, an area of up to 225 sq ft, and a run time at max of 3 hours.

Do the two Lasko cards state the same watts?

Yes. Both the CT32150 page and the CT14401 page state heat output of 900 W to 1,500 W at 120 V. The heights differ: 31.6 in and 14 in.

Where does the heater recall line come from?

The US line is the CPSC SaferProducts match for that model. The Canadian line is the Health Canada open dataset match. The date on the card is the day the match was recorded.
