Cosori air fryer recall: the 2023 model list and the Pro II

The February 23, 2023 CPSC and Health Canada notices name specific Cosori air fryer model numbers. The COSORI Pro II card uses the CS358-AF series, which is not in those lists. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-10 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2023-02-23

Two Million COSORI Air Fryers Recalled by Atekcity Due to Fire and Burn Hazards

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says this recall involves Cosori air fryers with the model numbers listed below. It says the brand name Cosori is on the front of each unit, the model number is printed on the bottom label and in the user manual, and the units are 3.7 and 5.8 quart, sold in black, gray, white, blue, or red.

Model numbers in the CPSC notice

  • CP158-AF-R19
  • CP158-AF-RXW
  • CP158-AF-RXR

  • CAF-P581-RUSR
  • CP137-AF

  • CP137-AF-RXW
  • CS158-AF
  • CS158-AF-RXB
  • CS158-AF-R19
  • CAF-P581S-BUSR
  • CAF-P581S-RUSR
  • CAF-P581S-AUSR
  • CO137-AF
  • CO158-AF
  • CO158-AF-RXB
  • CP258-AF

How to check your model

The notice says to read the model number on the bottom label of the unit and in the user manual.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled air fryers and contact Cosori for a free replacement air fryer or another Cosori product. It says registration asks for contact information and photos of the unit with the cord cut, and that no receipt is needed.

Health Canada · 2023-02-23

Certain models of Cosori-branded Air Fryers recalled due to potential fire hazard

What the notice says

The Health Canada notice, updated 2023-02-23, says the recall involves certain Cosori-branded air fryers and describes a fire hazard. It says the notice is a joint recall with the CPSC. Its model column also uses the shorter names CO137, CO158, CP137, CP158, CP258, and CS158.

  • CAF-P581S-AUSR
  • CAF-P581S-BUSR
  • CAF-P581S-RUSR

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the bottom label and in the user manual, and the batch number is on the bottom of the unit or at the back below the rear exhaust vent.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the recalled products immediately and contact Cosori for a replacement unit.
