The Barista Express lines

Worth matching means the BES870XL sentences describe the espresso setup you are placing on the counter. It is not a price verdict, and it is not a claim about when a listing changes. The Breville page states model BES870XL, a grinder of Integrated conical burr grinder with 16 grind settings, a portafilter of 54 mm stainless steel, a dose of 18 g, and extraction of Low-pressure pre-infusion, then 9 bar, from a 15 bar pump.

Two other coffee cards sit on the kitchen list, and neither is a second espresso machine. The Encore ESP page states burrs of 40 mm conical steel, a hopper of 300 g, power of 70 W, 100-120 V, settings of 1-20 espresso, 21-40 filter, and a warranty of 1 year. The Stagg EKG page states power of 1200 W, temperature of To-the-degree control, a hold line of Keeps the set temperature while you pour, and listed capacities of 0.9 L (30 oz) and 1.5 L (50 oz) sizes on the maker page. A kettle and a separate grinder are different tools from an integrated machine.

Pick the integrated machine, the grinder, or the kettle

Pick the BES870XL if the listing shows that model and you are matching an integrated conical burr grinder with 16 grind settings, a 54 mm stainless steel portafilter, an 18 g dose, and extraction that the page describes as low-pressure pre-infusion, then 9 bar, from a 15 bar pump. Those five lines are the card. A listing that says Breville, or Barista, still has to show BES870XL if this is the machine.

Pick the Encore ESP if you are matching a separate grinder: 40 mm conical steel, a hopper of 300 g, and settings of 1-20 espresso, 21-40 filter. The power line is 70 W, 100-120 V. The warranty line is 1 year. The BES870XL card does not print a hopper weight or a 40 mm burr. Sixteen grind settings on the Breville page and the 1-20 espresso range on the Baratza page are different sentences.

Pick the Stagg EKG if you are matching a kettle, not an espresso machine. The power line is 1200 W. The temperature line says To-the-degree control. The hold line says it keeps the set temperature while you pour. The capacity line names 0.9 L (30 oz) and 1.5 L (50 oz) sizes on the maker page. A 1200 W kettle line is not the extraction line on the BES870XL card. This page does not turn one into the other.

What to read on the listing

Match the model string first. BES870XL is the espresso machine. The Encore ESP is the grinder with 40 mm conical steel burrs. The Stagg EKG is the kettle. A listing that bundles a different model number is a different card. The recall block below is the catalogue match for each of the three. None of these three cards is a stand mixer or a rice cooker.

If you want the dose and the portafilter in one machine, the lines to read are 18 g and 54 mm stainless steel on the BES870XL page, with the extraction sentence that names 9 bar from a 15 bar pump. If you want a grinder you can use for filter as well as espresso, the settings line to read is on the Encore ESP page: 1-20 espresso, 21-40 filter. If you want water at a set temperature, that sentence is on the Stagg EKG page.
