How to read this

Clearing width and throw as the Snow Joe manual and the EGO page state them for the 24V-SS10 and the SNT2110, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Snow Joe manual states a clearing width of 10 in and a throw of up to 20 ft. The EGO page states a clearing width of 21 in and a throw of up to 40 ft.

Battery, snow depth, and weight are on the Snow Joe card only. Auger, power, and tool warranty are on the EGO card only.

Width and throw are different kinds of machine

The shared labels are clearing width and throw, and both numbers differ. The Snow Joe manual prints 10 in and up to 20 ft. The EGO page prints 21 in and up to 40 ft. A wider stated path and a longer stated throw are the EGO lines. A narrower stated path and a shorter stated throw are the Snow Joe lines.

The Snow Joe manual also states a 24 V max, 4.0 Ah battery, snow depth up to 6 in, and a weight of 9.9 lb. Those labels are not on the EGO card. The EGO page states a steel auger, Peak Power from two 56V ARC Lithium batteries, and a 5-year limited tool warranty. Those labels are not on the Snow Joe card. The table leaves them out because a shared table only repeats a label both pages use.

Pick by path, depth, and weight

Pick the Snow Joe manual if the path you are matching is 10 in, the stated snow depth is up to 6 in, and the stated weight is 9.9 lb. That combination is a shovel-sized cordless tool. The throw line that goes with it is up to 20 ft. The battery line on that manual is 24 V max, 4.0 Ah, which is a different voltage line from the EGO page.

Pick the EGO page if the path you are matching is 21 in and the throw line you are matching is up to 40 ft. The power line on that page names two 56V batteries, and the auger line names steel. Those are the figures to read for a walk-behind cordless blower rather than a 10 in shovel.

A 9.9 lb stated weight and an unstated EGO weight cannot share a weight row. If weight is the deciding line, it is on the Snow Joe card. If auger material is the deciding line, it is on the EGO card. The recall block below is the catalogue match for each model, not a ranking of the two tools.
