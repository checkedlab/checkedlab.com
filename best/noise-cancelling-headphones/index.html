What to look for

  1. Read the battery sentence with the feature it names. Sony states max. 30 hours with noise cancelling on, max. 40 hours with it off. AirPods Pro 3 states up to 8 hours with Active Noise Cancellation. Bose states up to 24 hours, next to modes Quiet, Aware, and Immersion.
  2. Read weight in the unit printed. The Sony page states approx. 8.82 oz. The Bose page states 0.56 lb. This page does not convert ounces and pounds.
  3. Read charge time on the cards that print it. The Sony page states approx. 3.5 hours. The Bose page states 3 hours. The AirPods Pro 3 spec list on this site does not print a charge-time line.
  4. Read the microphone or mode sentence on its own card. The Sony page states that 2 processors control 8 microphones. The Bose page states Quiet, Aware, and Immersion, plus multipoint for two devices. The AirPods page states a Hearing Aid feature and a heart rate sensor.
