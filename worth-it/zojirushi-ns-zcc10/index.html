The question is which lines fit the pot

On this page, worth it means the maker’s lines describe the rice pot you are trying to place. It is not a price verdict. The NS-ZCC10 page states a capacity of Up to 5.5 cups / 1.0 liter, dimensions of 10-1/8 x 13 x 8-1/8 inches, electrical of 120 volts / 680 watts, and origin of Made in Japan. Those four lines are the card. A listing that says Zojirushi, or rice cooker, still has to show NS-ZCC10 if these are the lines you mean.

This catalogue has no Tiger rice cooker card, so there is no Tiger capacity line to put beside the 5.5 cup line. The kitchen card that names rice is the Duo Plus. Its function list is Pressure cook, slow cook, sauté, sous vide, steam, sterilize, keep warm, rice, and yogurt. It also states a capacity of 6 quart, power of 1,000 W, 120 V, 60 Hz, cleanup of Dishwasher-safe, and dimensions of 12.2 L x 13.4 W x 12.9 H in. Rice is one name in that function list. It is not a dedicated rice-cooker capacity in cups.

Pick the cup line or the function list

Pick the NS-ZCC10 if you are matching a rice cooker, the capacity line is Up to 5.5 cups / 1.0 liter, the cabinet is 10-1/8 x 13 x 8-1/8 inches, and the electrical line is 120 volts / 680 watts. The origin line says Made in Japan. The height in that dimension line is 8-1/8 inches. That is the vertical figure to hold against the space under a cabinet.

Pick the Duo Plus if you are matching a 6 quart multi-cooker whose function list includes rice among pressure cook, slow cook, sauté, and the other names on that page, at 1,000 W, 120 V, 60 Hz. The dimension line is 12.2 L x 13.4 W x 12.9 H in. A 6 quart bowl and a 5.5 cup rice line are not the same measure. This page does not convert cups into quarts or 680 watts into 1,000 watts.

The two cleanup stories differ as well. The Duo Plus card says Dishwasher-safe. The NS-ZCC10 card does not print a cleanup line. Do not borrow the dishwasher sentence for the rice cooker. Read the electrical line on the cooker you are plugging in: 120 volts / 680 watts on the Zojirushi page, and 1,000 W, 120 V, 60 Hz on the Duo Plus page.

What a listing still has to show

A season name or a price on a listing is not the model string. For the rice cooker, the string to match is NS-ZCC10, with the 5.5 cup / 1.0 liter capacity and the 10-1/8 x 13 x 8-1/8 inch cabinet. For the multi-cooker, match the Duo Plus function list and the 6 quart line. The recall block below is the catalogue match for each card. Official Instant Pot notices on the recall desk name other Instant Pot models. Open that explainer and read the model list before treating a family name as this Duo Plus card.

If the cabinet under your shelf is shorter than 8-1/8 inches, the NS-ZCC10 dimension line does not fit, whatever else the page says. If you need pressure cook in the same function list as rice, that sentence is on the Duo Plus card, not on the Zojirushi card. Pick the card whose sentence you can point to on the maker page.
