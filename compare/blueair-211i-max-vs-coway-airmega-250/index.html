How to read this

Dimensions and weight as the Blueair and Coway pages state them for the Blue Pure 211i Max and the Airmega 250, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Blueair page states 22.7 x 14.4 x 14.4 in and 16.2 lb. The Airmega 250 specifications state 18.5 x 19.7 x 8.3 in and 20.5 lb.

A lower stated weight is the Blueair line. Room coverage, sound, CADR, noise, and filter life use labels that only one card uses, so they stay off this table.

The shared rows are the cabinet, not the airflow

Dimensions and weight are the labels both pages print. Blueair states a 22.7 x 14.4 x 14.4 in cabinet at 16.2 lb. Coway states an 18.5 x 19.7 x 8.3 in cabinet at 20.5 lb. A lower stated weight is the Blueair line. The shape lines are not the same proportions: one page is closer to a square footprint, and the other prints a shallower 8.3 in side.

Airflow is not a shared label. The Blueair page states a recommended room of 674 sq ft and sound of 23 to 53 dB. The Coway page states coverage of 465 sq ft at 4 air changes per hour, a large-room line of 1,860 sq ft at 1 air change per hour, noise of 22 to 54 dB(A), smoke CADR 249, dust CADR 261, pollen CADR 230, and a Max2 filter life of 1 year. Those lines stay on the cards because the other page does not use the same label.

Pick by the room line you can actually cite

Pick the Blueair page if the room figure you are matching is the 674 sq ft recommended line, and the cabinet you are matching is 22.7 x 14.4 x 14.4 in at 16.2 lb. The sound line on that page is a range, 23 to 53 dB, which is a different label from Coway’s noise line.

Pick the Airmega 250 page if the figures you are matching are the CADR trio, the 4-air-change coverage of 465 sq ft, or the 1-year Max2 filter line. The stated weight on that page is 20.5 lb, higher than the Blueair weight line. The noise line is 22 to 54 dB(A). Do not treat the Blueair decibel range and the Coway decibel range as one table row. The pages do not share that label.

A square-foot line with no air-change rate and a square-foot line that names 4 air changes per hour are not the same claim. Read the sentence the maker printed. The recall block is the catalogue match for each model on the check date.
