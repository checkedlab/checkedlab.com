How to read this

Capacity, dimensions, and material as the PetSafe and Pioneer Pet pages state them for the Drinkwell Platinum and the Raindrop 6023, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. Recall lines use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) catalogue check.

The Drinkwell Platinum page states 168 oz, dimensions of 15.8 × 10.3 × 10.0 in, and a BPA-free material line that includes top-shelf dishwasher safe. The Raindrop 6023 page states 60 oz, dimensions of 11.75 x 9.75 x 4.25 in, and 18/8 stainless steel, dishwasher safe.

A larger stated capacity is the Drinkwell line. A smaller stated footprint is the Raindrop line. Power is on the Drinkwell card only. Weight and filter are on the Raindrop card only.

Capacity and the bowl material

Capacity, dimensions, and material are the shared labels. PetSafe prints 168 oz, 15.8 × 10.3 × 10.0 in, and BPA-free, top-shelf dishwasher safe. Pioneer Pet prints 60 oz, 11.75 x 9.75 x 4.25 in, and 18/8 stainless steel, dishwasher safe. A larger stated capacity is the Drinkwell line. A lower stated height, 4.25 in against 10.0 in, is the Raindrop line.

The material sentences are not the same claim. One page names a BPA-free plastic description and top-shelf dishwasher safe. The other names 18/8 stainless steel and dishwasher safe. Both say dishwasher safe. Only the Raindrop page names a steel grade in that field.

Pick by ounces, height, or the filter line

Pick the Drinkwell Platinum page if the capacity you are matching is 168 oz, or the power line you need is plug-in. The cabinet on that page is 15.8 × 10.3 × 10.0 in. Weight is not on that card.

Pick the Raindrop 6023 page if the capacity you are matching is 60 oz, the material line you need names 18/8 stainless steel, or you need the stated weight of 1.7 lb and the replaceable charcoal filter line. The dimensions on that page are 11.75 x 9.75 x 4.25 in, a shorter stated height than the Drinkwell line.

A charcoal filter line is not a purification certification. The Raindrop page says the filter is replaceable charcoal. It does not, in these fields, name an NSF standard. The recall block is the catalogue match for each fountain.
