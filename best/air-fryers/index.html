What to look for

  1. Read capacity in the unit printed. The Crispi page states 4-qt and 6-cup CleanCrisp glass. The COSORI page states 5.8 qt / 5.5 L. The BOV900 page states an interior of 1 cubic ft. The Foodi capacity line names a 5 lb chicken, two 12 in pizzas, or a 12 lb turkey.
  2. Read watts next to the capacity. The Crispi page states 1,500 W. The COSORI page states 1,700 W. The Foodi page states 1,800 W. The BOV900 page states 6 independent quartz heating elements and does not print a watt line in this spec list.
  3. Read the function count the page prints. The Crispi page names four modes: Max Crisp, Bake, Air Fry, and Recrisp. The Foodi page names ten functions. The BOV900 page states 13, including Air Fry and Dehydrate.
  4. Confirm the model token on the box. The Crispi recall match uses FN101. The BOV900 page states model BOV900BSSUSC. The COSORI card is the Pro II 5.8 qt model on this page.

What should I read on the Foodi card before buying?

The model name Ninja Foodi 10-in-1 XL Pro Air Fry Oven, the 1,800 W line, and the recall block. The block is the recorded match for that model. Other notices that mention the brand name are labeled as other models.

Where does the air fryer recall line come from?
