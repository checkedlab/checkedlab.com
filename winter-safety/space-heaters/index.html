Space heaters

What to read on a space heater label, where to search CPSC and Health Canada, and official notices for overheating and a failed tip switch.

Space heatersElectric blanketsCO and smoke alarmsOutage power

What to read on the label

  • Read the rating label for a certification mark. UL, ETL, and CSA are the marks to compare with what is printed on the unit.
  • Read the label and the manual for a tip-over shutoff and an overheat shutoff. Then read the model number on the unit. If a notice uses a serial number or a manufacture date, read that on the same label.

How to search a recall

For a purchase in the United States, search the brand and the model on CPSC Recalls. For a purchase in Canada, search the same words on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts. Open the notice and match the model number on the unit. A family name on the box can point at a different model.

Heater notices in this guide put the model number on the underside, on the bottom decal, or on the item number for a tank-top propane heater. Match that string, not the brand alone.

Patterns in official notices

Each pattern below is one official notice. The sentence is what that notice says.

  • CPSC: Vornado Air Recalls Electric Space Heaters. The notice says the VH110 can overheat and cause the units to melt, catch fire, and ignite nearby items. It says recalled heaters have 1 and 3 as the fourth and fifth digits of the serial number, on a silver decal on the bottom.
  • CPSC: Harbor Freight Tools Recalls One Stop Gardens Tank Top Propane Heaters Due to Burn Hazard. The notice says the flame can be directed to the rear in windy weather and can melt the tip switch safety shut off. It says the heater may not turn off if it tips over. It names item numbers 63073 and 63072.
  • Health Canada: DREO Space Heaters recalled due to fire and burn hazards. The notice says model DR-HSH015 can overheat and ignite. It says affected units were manufactured from April 2025 (2025/04) to January 2026 (2026/01). The model number and manufacture date are on labels on the underside.
  • Health Canada: GoveeLife and Govee Smart Electric Space Heaters recalled due to fire hazard. The notice says the products can overheat, posing fire and burn hazards. It lists model numbers H7130, including H7130101, plus H7131, H7132, H7133, H7134, and H7135. The model number is on the label on the underside.

Questions

Where do I search a space heater recall?

Search the brand and the model number on CPSC Recalls for a US purchase and on Health Canada recalls and safety alerts for a Canadian purchase. Match the model on the unit to the model on the notice.

What should I read on the heater before I buy?

Read the certification mark on the rating label, whether the label or manual names a tip-over shutoff and an overheat shutoff, and the model number. Read the serial number or manufacture date when a notice uses those.

Is the Dreo Atom 316 card the same unit as DREO model DR-HSH015?

No. The catalogue card is the Atom 316. The Health Canada notice names DR-HSH015 and a manufacture range printed on the underside. Read that label.
