How we picked

Checked Lab lines up maker pages, store links, and public recall records for shoppers in the US and Canada.

Manufacturer pages, and only the sentences those pages actually state. The source link sits next to the spec.

US recalls come from the CPSC SaferProducts API, and Canadian recalls from Health Canada’s open dataset d38de914-c94c-429b-8ab1-8776c31643e3. The date is the day that match was recorded.

Placement on a card is editorial. Brands do not buy a slot on the page. Prices stay on the store sites. The buttons open the store.

The label uses tank size, runtime, or mist type. The Classic 300S and the LV600HH both state a 6 liter tank and different runtimes: up to 60 hours and up to 50 hours. The HEV615W page states the tank in gallons, 1.5 gallons, and a run time of 24 hours on low.

Coverage lines are read as printed. The Classic 300S page states 505 ft². The LV600HH page states 430 to 753 sq ft. The HEV615W card does not print a square-foot line in this spec list. A gallon line is not converted into liters on this page.

Levoit Classic 300S Smart Humidifier

The Classic 300S page states a runtime of up to 60 hours, a tank of 6 L, and coverage of 505 ft². Mist levels are 4. The label matches the 60 hour runtime.

Levoit LV600HH Hybrid Ultrasonic Humidifier

The LV600HH page states mist of warm and cool, a tank of 6 liter, and a runtime of up to 50 hours. Coverage is 430 to 753 sq ft. The label matches the warm-and-cool mist line.

Honeywell Top Fill Cool Moisture Tower Humidifier HEV615W

The HEV615W page states a tank of 1.5 gallons, a run time of 24 hours on low, and dimensions of 9.84 x 8.66 x 24.72 in. Weight is 9.5 lb. The label matches the 1.5 gallon tank.

What to look for

  1. Read the tank unit. Classic 300S states 6 L. LV600HH states 6 liter. HEV615W states 1.5 gallons. The numbers are not in one unit.
  2. Read runtime with its condition. Classic 300S states up to 60 hours. LV600HH states up to 50 hours. HEV615W states 24 hours on low.
  3. Read mist type when the page names it. The LV600HH page states warm and cool mist. The Classic 300S page states 4 mist levels. The HEV615W title line is a cool-moisture tower.
  4. Read the tower size when floor space is the constraint. The HEV615W page states dimensions of 9.84 x 8.66 x 24.72 in and a weight of 9.5 lb. The two Levoit cards print tank and coverage rather than that tower height.

Where does the humidifier recall line come from?

The US line is the CPSC SaferProducts match for that model. The Canadian line is the Health Canada open dataset match. The date on the card is the day the match was recorded.
