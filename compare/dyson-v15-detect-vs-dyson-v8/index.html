How to read this

Weight, run time, charge time, bin volume, and suction as the Dyson pages state them for the V15 Detect Absolute and the V8, with a dated recall line for each.

Each line below is copied from that model’s maker page. The Source link opens that page. A spec that only one card lists is left out of the table. The recall line is the CPSC and Health Canada check dated 2026-11-21 (UTC).

The V15 Detect Absolute page states 6.83 lb, up to 60 min of run time, a 4.5 hr charge time, a 0.2 gallon bin, and 230 AW in Boost mode. The V8 page states 5.58 lb, 40 min maximum run time, a 5 hr charge time, a 0.14 gallon bin, and 115 AW in MAX mode.

A lower stated weight and a shorter stated charge time are the V15 lines. A shorter stated run time, a smaller stated bin, and a lower stated suction figure are the V8 lines. Whole-machine HEPA filtration is on the V15 card only.
