What the notices say

CPSC heater notices name specific older Lasko and Air King model numbers. CT32150 and CT14401 are not those model numbers. The catalogue check found no matching recall for either card on the check date. Catalogue lines below use the 2026-11-12 (UTC) check.

CPSC · 2011-02-08

Lasko Recalls Portable Electric Heaters Due To Fire Hazard

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves Lasko model 5540 and Air King model 8540 portable electric tower heaters manufactured in 2002, with date codes that begin with 2. It says heaters with date codes beginning with 3, 4, or 5 are not in this recall. It describes the heaters as dark grey with silver front covers and black vent slats, 20.5 inches tall.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • Lasko 5540
  • Air King 8540

How to check your model

The notice says the date code is a four-digit number on the label on the bottom of the unit, on the bottom left of the label, above the voltage number.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters and contact Lasko for a free replacement heater.

CPSC · 2007-06-28

Ceramic Heaters Recalled By Lasko

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says the recall involves Lasko ceramic heaters manufactured in 2005, in the model numbers listed below.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • 5132
  • 5345
  • 5362
  • 5364
  • 5420
  • 5532
  • 5534
  • 5566

How to check your model

The notice says the model number is on the bottom of the unit or at the rear of the base.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters and contact Lasko for a free replacement heater.

CPSC · 2004-03-04

CPSC, Lasko Products Inc. Announce Recall of Space Heaters

What the notice says

The CPSC notice says two heaters are in this recall. Model 5500 is an oscillating ceramic heater with dark gray vents and a bronze trim. Model 5700 is an oil-filled radiator. It says Lasko is printed near the top of both.

Model numbers in this CPSC notice

  • 5500
  • 5700

How to check your model

The notice says the 5500 model number is on a label under the round base, and the 5700 model number is on a label on the side near the right front wheel.

What the notice tells owners to do

The notice tells owners to stop using the heaters, unplug them, and contact Lasko for instructions on returning the heater for a free replacement or a product of similar value.
